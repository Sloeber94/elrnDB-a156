Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

The plastic cam cover (1.8 - 20 T. SPARK) is built to withstand high temperatures and supports the ignition coil retaining frame.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods

The cam covers are in aluminium. The right hand cylinder head cover is integral with the intake chamber.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

The cam cover is in aluminium while the right cylinder head cover is integral with the air chamber.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

The cam cover is in aluminium and a gasket ensures a seal against the cylinder head.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

Apart from its normal function, the aluminium cam cover also acts as a separator/condenser for the engine oil vapour recovery system.

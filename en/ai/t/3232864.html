Combined sender unit for coolant temperature gauge and overheating warning light (PREMODIFICATION)

Mounted on the cylinder head, gearbox side, it indicates the coolant temperature and controls the warning light:

  • contacts open 112 ± 3° C
  • contacts closed 122 ± 2° C

On postmodification versions, the functions of this sender unit are built into the double NTC engine temperature sensor fitted to the thermostat (see 1056 PETROL INJECTION SYSTEM ).

Specifcations

Function

The two-speed electric cooling fans increase the heat dissipation capacity of the radiator and/or air conditioning condenser.

They are controlled directly by the engine management control unit in accordance with a specific operating logic 1060G pressure pump electric control .

Control of the engine coolant temperature

At engine coolant temperatures above 105° C, the engine management control unit:

  • reduces the quantity of fuel injected (reduces the engine power);
  • operates the cooling fan through the fan interface;
  • lights up the coolant overheating warning light in the instrument panel.

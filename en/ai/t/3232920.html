Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02

7/8" brake pump integral with Bosch 8" brake servo

The reservoir is fitted in the engine bay and supplies the brake and clutch systems

The brake fluid level sensor is built into the reservoir cap.

The brakeforce distributor is built into the ABS control unit and works iin accordance with an EBD function (see 33).

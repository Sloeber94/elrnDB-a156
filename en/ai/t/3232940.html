Applies to: all engines; all production periods

SHOCK ABSORBERS

Double acting, hydraulic shock absorbers pressurized to reduce the operating noise.

The walls of the shock absorber and the spring plate are made from high resistance steel with reduced shims.

Front shock absorber mounting with flexible insulation of the stem separated in relation to the spring support (through a rubber mounting) and the buffer.

SPRINGS

Coil springs, coaxial to the shock absorber, resting on the lower and upper plates with a rubber seal to prevent noise during operation.

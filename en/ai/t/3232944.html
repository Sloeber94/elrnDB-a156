Applies to: all engines; all production periods

SHOCK ABSORBERS

Double acting, hydraulic shock absorbers pressurized to reduce the operating noise.

The walls of the shock absorber and the spring plate are made from high resistance steel with reduced shims.

Front shock absorber mounting with flexible insulation of the stem separated in relation to the spring support (through a rubber mounting) and the buffer.

A new tapered fastening system means that the block need no longer be bolted to the body. This brings advantages in terms of assembly and reliability.

SPRINGS

coil springs greatly offset in relation to the shock absorber axis to reduce sliding friction.

Springs rest on the lower and upper plates with an interposed rubber seal to prevent noise during operation.

Upper end of travel buffer made from "CELLASTO" (closed cell polyurethane which maintains its properties of flexibility in time) with very gradual intervention for improved comfort and a gradual response round bends.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Side levers in high-strength pressed steel of reduced thickness.

On the rear lever, a cam toe-in adjustment device replaces the conventional worm and screw mechanism. This weighs less and is easier to adjust.

A progressive hydraulic bush has been fitted to the front attachment of the longitudinal rod. This improves suspension comfort.

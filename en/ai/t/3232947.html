Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Aluminium crossmember produced using a vacuum moulding process (PREMIUM technology). This allows a weight reduction of 1.8 kg compared to a similar sheet steel crossmember.

The result is achieved by using redued wall thicknesses and due to the purity and refinement of the grain.

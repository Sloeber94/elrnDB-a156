Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the alarm is operated the direction indicators do not flash

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

WARNING: the flashing of the direction indicators may or may not be activated, according to the various country modes ( see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5580E anti-theft device ).

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check, in order:

  • no faults in the alarm control unit
  • no faults in the alarm receiver

Continue to Step 2

Error detected in the control unit or receiver

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Dialogue failure for diagnostic equipment between the control unit and the receiver

Restore the connection between the control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E7015 ALARM ).

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the operation of the direction indicators

Continue to Step 3

Lights not activated

Restore connections between alarm control unit and lights ( See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS ).

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

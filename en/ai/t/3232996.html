Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the alarm is operated the siren does not emit auditory signals

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

WARNING: the auditory signals may or may not be activated according to the various country modes ( see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5580E anti-theft device ).

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check, in order:

  • no faults in the alarm control unit
  • no faults in the alarm receiver

Continue to Step 2

Error detected in the control unit or receiver

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Dialogue failure for diagnostic equipment between the control unit and the receiver

Restore the connection between the control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E7015 ALARM ).

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the operation of the siren

Continue to Step 3

Siren not activated

Check and, if necessary, replace the alarm control unit Op. 5580E46 antitheft device ecu / siren unit r + r .

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

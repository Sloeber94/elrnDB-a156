Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is switched on, nothing happens, the instruments do not light up; In the Start position, the starter motor does not turn.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check the efficiency of the battery

Continue to Step 1

Battery discharged

Charge or replace the battery

1

CHECK BATTERY CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • correct fixing or connection to earth of negative battery terminal
  • correct fixing of positive battery terminal and that all leads are connected
  • no corrosion at the negative battery lead earth point

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the battery connections ( See E1010 SUPPLY )

Cable damaged

Replace the cable concerned (see Assembly 5530B)

2

CHECK MAXIFUSE BOX CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • condition of two maxifuse boxes
  • correct fixing and connection to battery of maxifuse boxes

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections of the maxifuse boxes to the battery ( See E1010 SUPPLY ).

Maxifuse box damaged

Replace maxifuse box

3

CHECK IGNITION SWITCH CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the correct supply is reaching the ignition switch. In particular, check the condition of the 'IGN' maxifuse

Continue to Step 4

Connections not correct

Restore connections between battery and ignition switch ( See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING ).

Fuse damaged

Replace the fuse concerned: (then search for the cause of the problem)

4

CHECK LOCK BARREL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition of lock barrel and correct operation

Continue to Step 5

Lock barrel faulty

Renew or replace the lock barrel Op. 5520A14 ignition key block - r+r

5

CHECK IGNITION SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition and correct operation of ignition switch.

Continue to Step 6

Ignition switch faulty.

Renew or replace the ignition switch Op. 5520A12 ignition switch assembly - r r for versions with air bag

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

When the ignition is switched on, nothing happens, the instruments do not light up; In the Start position, the starter motor does not turn.

Results of the tests

1

BATTERY EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency of the battery using the appropriate diagnostic equipment

Continue to Step 2

Battery not working properly

Replace battery

2

CHECK FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Correct fixing or connection to earth of negative battery terminal
  • Correct fixing of positive battery terminal and that all leads are connected
  • No corrosion of the cable fastenings
  • Correct fixing and connection between positive terminal and control unit with maxifuse at battery

Continue to Step 3

Cable fastenings at battery slack

Tighten cables

Earth lead frayed

Replace lead Op. 5530B22 battery earth lead - r+r

Connections with slack fastenings

Restore fastenings

Maxifuse burnt

Check the causes of the burnt fuse and replace it: wiring diagram See E1010 SUPPLY

3

CHECKING POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check switch supply

Continue to Step 4

No supply reaches the switch

Check the continuity of the wiring between the maxifuse at the battery control unit and the switch See E1010 SUPPLY

4

CHECKING SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition and correct operation of the switch

Continue to Step 5

Switch contacts not working properly

Replace switch Op. 5520A12 ignition switch assembly - r r for versions with air bag

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

B10 ENGINE DOES NOT START / UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES

Path

Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to

Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-a156/en/#/3233003

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The engine does not start. When the key is turned, the instruments light up, the starter motor turns to the Avv position, but the engine will not start.

The MIL warning light should come on with the ignition on and go out once the engine has started;

If there is a fault in the reconfigurable matrix the CODE failure message comes on.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check that there is fuel in the tank and whether the inertia switch has intervened

Then check:

  • that there is no water, other fluid or dirt in the fuel tank
  • the state of the spark plugs and leads
  • the condition of the air intake circuit
  • for a possible obstruction in the catalyzer

Continue to Step 1

Presence of dirt in the fuel tank

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Clean or replace the spark plugs Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace the spark plug leads Op. 5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

Catalyzer obstructed

Replace the catalyzer Op. 1080B62 catalytic converter - r + r

B12 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY' / WHEN COLD

Path

Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-a156/en/#/3233005

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

When the outside temperature is low, the engine does not start when cold, or it starts with difficulty and only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned the instruments light up normally, the starter motor turns, but the engine does not start

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check that the voltage is correct and that the battery is working properly

Then, check:

  • state of the spark plugs and leads
  • condition of the air intake circuit

Continue to Step 1

Battery run down

Recharge or replace the battery

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Clean or replace the spark plugs Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace the spark plug leads Op. 5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace component (see Assembly 1048A)

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • the plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

Error detected in the control unit

Proceed according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Failed dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT )

2

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors .

Continue to Step 3

Compression values not correct

Restore the correct cylinder compression, for example, checking the seal of the injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt and the cylinders (see ENGINE OVERHAUL, Subassembly 1004)

3

FUEL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fuel supply pressure Op. 0510C10 fuel system - check delivery pressure ,

Continue to Step 4

Pressure not correct

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

4

INJECTORS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out an active diagnosis for the injectors

Continue to Step 5

Injectors connectino faulty

Restore the injectors connection with the control unit,( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT )

Injectors faulty

Replace injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

5

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

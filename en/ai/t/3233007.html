B16 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY'

Path
Fault diagnosis > B STARTING > B16 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY'
Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-a156/en/#/3233007

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

The engine does not start, especially when cold, or it starts with difficulty and only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned the instruments light up, the starter motor turns, but the engine does not start

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • that there is no water, other fluid or dirt in the fuel tank
  • the seal of the fuel pipes and connectors
  • the condition of the air intake circuit

Continue to Step 1

Presence of dirt in the fuel tank

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel

Fuel pipes damaged

Renew or replace the pipes (see Assembly 1040B)

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT ).

2

FUEL PRE-HEATING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other iinstruments) carry out the active diagnosis for the fuel pre-heating relay:

Continue to Step 3

Connections broken

Restore the connections between:

  • the fuel pre-heating relay
  • the fuel heater
  • engine management control unit ( See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT )

Fuel pre-heating not working

Replace the fuel heater Op. 1044D10 fuel heater - r + r

Fuel pre-heating not working properly

Replace the thermal contact Op. 1044D20 thermal switch for fuel heater - r + r

3

HEATER PLUGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the starting system (heater plugs): See Test 5520CA Check on operation of spark plugs

Continue to Step 4

Heater plugs not working

Replace the heater plugs Op. 5520C10 glow plugs - r+r

4

LOW PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fuel supply circuit, low pressure side: if necessary, use the diagnostic equipment (Examiner) to measure the pressure values

Continue to Step 5

Pressure not correct: fuel filter faulty

Replace the fuel filter Op. 1044B10 fuel filter assembly - r + r or replace

Pressure not correct: electric pump faulty

Replace the electric pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Pressure not correct: low pressure pipes faulty

Replace the low pressure pipes (see Assembly 1060F)

5

HIGH PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fuel supply circuit, high pressure side: for example, using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner) to measure the pressure values

Continue to Step 6

Pressure not correct: pressure pump faulty

Replace the pressure pump Op. 1060E10 pressure pump - r r

Pressure not correct: manifold faulty

Replace the manifold Op. 1060F30 fuel delivery manifold - r r

Pressure not correct: injectors faulty

Replace the injectors Op. 1060F10 fuel injectors

Pressure not correct: high pressure pipes faulty

Replace the high pressure pipes (see Asembly 1060F)

6

VALVE CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the valve tappet clearance is correct Op. 1036H14 valve tappets - check clearance .

Continue to Step 7

Clearance not correct

Restore the correct valve clearance Op. 1036H18 VALVE TAPPETS - Adjust. following clearance check .

7

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors

Continue to Step 8

Compression values not correct

Restore the correct cylinder compression, for example, checking the seal of the injectors Op. 1060F10 fuel injectors (see OVERHAULING ENGINE, Subassembly 1004)

8

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present.

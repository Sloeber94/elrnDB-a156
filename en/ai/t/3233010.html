The engagement of the gears is difficult in all conditions.

CHECK FIXING OF GEARBOXCONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixings of the gearbox control cables: they should not be slack or incorrectly fixed

Continue to Step 2

Cable not fitted correctly

Restore the correct fixing of the control cables (see Assembly 2125C)

2

CHECK GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox control cables: the ball joints and the bushes should not be worn

Continue to Step 3

Ball joints worn

Replace the control cables (see Assembly 2125C)

3

All OK

Operation

Check the gear lever ball joint: it should not be worn

Continue to Step 4

Ball worn

Replace the gear lever Op. 2125C24 gear lever knob - r + r .

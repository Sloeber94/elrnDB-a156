D01 ENGINE NOT RUNNING SMOOTHLY / (HESISTANT)

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

There are decreases in performance, possibly prolonged, during acceleration

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check:

  • that there is no water, other fluid or dirt in the fuel tank
  • the state of the spark plugs and leads
  • correct fixing of battery earth

Continue to Step 1

Presence of dirt in the fuel tank

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Clean or replace the spark plugs Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace spark plug leads Op. 5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Battery earth lead not correctly fixed

Restore correct fixing Op. 5530B22 battery earth lead - r+r

Problems met

Operation

Check::

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • the plausibility of all the parameters.

Continue to Step 2

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT ).

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out active diagnosis on the:

  • vapour recovery solenoid valve
  • Injectors

Continue to Step 3

Solenoid valve connection faulty

Restore the connection of the solenoid valve with the control unit ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT )

Solenoid valve faulty

Replace the solenoid valve Op. 1080E24 fuel vapour by-pass valve/solenoid - r + r

Injector connections faulty

Restore injector connections with control unit, ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT )

Injectors faulty

Replace injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

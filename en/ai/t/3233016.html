Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The engine does not run smoothly in all conditions (load, speed, etc.).

In particular, there are brief, but intense 'flat spots'

Operation

Check, in order:

  • whether the diesel pipes are leaking
  • that there is no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and filter

  • that there are no obstructions in the exhaust system

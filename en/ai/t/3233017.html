Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

The engine does not run smoothly in all conditions (load, speed, etc.). This problem is accompanied by noise and vibrations coming from the engine.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • the seal of the fuel pipes and connectors
  • the condition of the air intake circuit

Continue to Step 1

Fuel pipes damaged

Renew or replace the pipes (see Assembly 1040B)

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

check

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • the plausibility of all the parameters.

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT ).

Filter faulty or damaged

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

The engine does not run smoothly in all conditions (load, speed, etc.). This problem is accompanied by noise and vibrations coming from the engine.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the condition of the air intake circuit
  • that there are no faults in the air filter
  • whether the diesel pipes are leaking
  • the accelerator pedal travel
  • that there is no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and filter
  • that there are no obstructions in the exhaust system

Continue to Step 1

Fault in air intake circuit

Repair the air intake circuit

Air filter blocked

Replace the filter element

Leak from diesel pipes

stop the leak

Accelerator pedal travel incomplete

Eliminate the cause of the problem

Presence of water, petrol, paraffin or dirt detected

Clean the tank and pipes and replace the filter element

Obstruction in the exhaust system

Replace the damaged system component

CHECK EGR AND ADJUST TURBOCHARGER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keep the diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic socket.

Carry out active diagnosis of EGR solenoid valve

Carry out active diagnosis of the turbocharger regulator solenoid valve

Check the operation of the variable geometry control actuator by carrying out the test See Test 1064BC Variable geometry turbocharger actuator leakage test

Continue to Step 3

EGR solenoid valve not working

Check the continuity of the wiring See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT and if necessary replace the solenoid Op. 1080C74 E.G.R. - ELECTRICAL VALVE R.R.

Restore the condition of the filter, the pipes and the heater device

Turbocharger solenoid valve not working

Check the continuity of the wiring See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT and if necessary replace the solenoid Op. 1064A28

Actuator does not work correctly

Check the condition of the vacuum circuit and, if necessary, replace the damaged components

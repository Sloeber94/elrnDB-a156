D05 ENGINE DOES NOT PRODUCE BEST PERFORMANCE

Path

Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to

Interactive page

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The engine runs smoothly at medium-low speeds, but does not reach iits usual maximum performance (both in terms of speed and acceleration)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • the state of the spark plugs and leads
  • the condition of the air intake circuit
  • for possible obstruction of the catalyzer and other exhaust pipes
  • for any obstacles to the complete accelerator pedal travel

Catalyzer obstructed

Exhausts obstructed

Replace the component involved (see Assembly 1076B)

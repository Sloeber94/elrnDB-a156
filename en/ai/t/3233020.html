LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check as described in the test See Test 1040AA Check supply voltage and absorption current that the pump's supply voltage is 12V and that the absorbed current is between 2 and 10 amps.

Continue to Step 12

Voltage < 12 V

Check electric wiring See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Current > 10 Amp: pump defective

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Current < 2 Amp.: pump blocked

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r and clean the fuel tank

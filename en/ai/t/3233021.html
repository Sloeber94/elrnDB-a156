The reading on the special instrument in the panel shows a high engine coolant temperature

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary check on the engine coolant level

Then check:

  • regular engagement of cooling fan
  • engine oil level
  • condition of water pump drive belt

Continue to Step 1

Insufficient engine coolant level

Top up the engine coolant level Op. 1088A10

Electric fan does not switch on

See table D31 ENGINE OVERHEATING / WITH FAN NOT WORKING

Tension incorrect

Replace the belt Op. 1092G10 single engine belt r r

Engine oil level too low

Top up the engine oil level Op. 1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary

1

TEMPERATURE GAUGE OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket (in the engine management control unit ambient): read the engine temperature value shown amongst the parameters and compare it with the instrument reading

Continue to Step 2

Values do not agree.

Instrument reading faulty

See table K25 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE GAUGE NOT WORKING

2

VISUAL INSPECTION OF CCOLANT EXPANSION TANK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Start up the engine and visually check that there are no air bubbles in the expansion tank

Continue to Step 3

Air in the cooling circuit

Eliminate the air from the cooling circuit

THERMOSTAT GROUP CHECK

Operation

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and seal of the pipes. In particular:

  • no obstructions
  • condition of hoses, connectors and plugs

Continue to Step 6

Tightening not correct or components damaged

Restore the correct tightening or replace the components involved (see Assembly 1088D)

RADIATOR CHECK

Operation

  • the seal of the gaskets

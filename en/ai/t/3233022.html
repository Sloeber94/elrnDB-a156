Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The reading on the instrument in the panel shows a high engine coolant temperature and the cooling fan does not come on

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the correct operation of the engine cooling fan(s)

this check can be carried out by connecting the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and carrying out the active diagnosis of the engine cooling fans:(in the engine management control unit ambient)

Continue to Step 2

Fan(s) faulty

Continue to Step 1

1

FAN OPERATING CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • fan fuses
  • fan engagment relays
  • fan resistances
  • connection between management control unit and fan relays
  • connection between fan relays and fans ( See E5020 ENGINE COOLING ).

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Fan resistances faulty

NOTE in that case the fan is not working at two different speeds

Replace the fan resistances Op. 1088E20 resistor for radiator cooling fan - r + r

Fan(s) faulty

Replace fan(s) Op. 1088E11 fans with single engine duct - r+r

2

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

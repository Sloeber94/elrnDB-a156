E01 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There is sticking or noise during low gear changes or when engaging reverse gear

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 3330B26 clutch pedal - r+r

2

FLUID LEAKS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

2.5 V6

1.9 JTD 8v

2.4 JTD 10v

Visually check for fluid leaks from the clutch pump and pipes

Continue to Step 3

Fault in the clutch operating circuit

Replace the components involved (see Assembly 1020B)

1.6 TS

1.8 TS

Visually inspect for fluid leaks from the pump, the clutch pipes and the bell housing (clutch actuator area)

Continue to Step 3

Fault in the clutch operating circuit

Replace the components involved - Assy. 1820B.

3

AIR PRESENCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is no air inside the hydraulic circuit

Continue to Step 4

Presence of air in the system

Bleed the air from the system Op. 1820B08 Clutch release HYDRAULIC SYSTEM - Bleeding air and restoring level

4

PUMP SEEPAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Press the clutch pedal slowly and, at the same time, check that there are no returns of fluid to the tank

Continue to Step 5

Seepage inside the clutch pump

Replace the clutch pump Op. 1820B20 (Pump) - MASTER CYLINDER R.R.

5

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • clutch plate wear
  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 6

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

6

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for the presence of dirt, rust or dents in the splined part of the clutch disc hub and on the gearbox main shaft

Continue to Step 7

Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft (see OVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110B)

Clutch plate hub bushes faulty

Restore the correct lubrication for the bushes

If necessary, replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

7

FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel (in particular, the condition of the surface and that there are no signs of overheating)

Continue to Step 8

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

8

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

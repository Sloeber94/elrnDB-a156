Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During acceleration, a consistent increase in the engine speed is noticed without a corresponding increase in the speed of the vehicle

This symptom can be easily checked by pressing the clutch pedal and engaging 4th speed; when accelerating and gradually releasing the clutch pedal, the vehicle does not move and the engine does not cut out

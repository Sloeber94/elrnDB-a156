The clutch pedal vibrates and "jerks". When released, the vehicle does not start smoothly, but jerks or vibrates

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check the engine idle speed

Continue to Step 1

Engine idle irregular

See table D10 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 3330B26 clutch pedal - r+r

2

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the front crossmember and the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 3

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

Engine support crossmember faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torque Op. 4410D13 front suspension beam - r r

3

Check:

  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring
  • that there is no damage to the flexible coupling springs

Continue to Step 4

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C22 gear input shaft seals - r r

2.4 JTD 10v

Check:

  • clutch disc wear or "vitrification"
  • that there is no damaged to the flywheel flexible coupling springs

Continue to Step 4

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C22 gear input shaft seals - r r

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

4

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft

Continue to Step 5

Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft (see OVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110B)

Clutch plate hub bushes faulty

Restore the correct lubrication for the bushes

If necessary, replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

5

FLYWHEEL CHECK

Problems met

Operation

Continue to Step 6

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

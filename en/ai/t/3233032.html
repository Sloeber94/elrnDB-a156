As the pressure exerted on the brake pedal increases, the braking action does not correspond.

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Continue to Step 1

Pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

CHECKING FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect:

  • brake fluid level in the reservoir
  • for fluid leaks from the brake pump, lines or system connections

Continue to Step 2

Leaks in system

Tighten the connectors or replace the damaged parts (see Assembly 3340B)

2

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • there are no faults in the ABS control unit

Continue to Step 3

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the supply for the ABS control unit ( See E7020 ABS ).

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of:

  • pump motor
  • solenoids

Continue to Step 4

Fault detected in the pump or a solenoid

Replace electrohydraulic unit-electronic control unit Op. 3340A10

CHECK PAD TYPE AND SURFACE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of pad friction surfaces
  • pad type

Friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault

Front brake pads damaged or wrong type

Replace pads Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replace

Rear pads damaged or wrong type

Replace pads Op. 3310B60 rear brake pads - replace - includes handbrake adjustment if necessary

F21 ABS NOT WORKING / (ONE WHEEL LOCKED)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brakes are operated abruptly without warning (panic braking), the ABS does not work and one wheel will therefore lock

Locking of one wheel could - under certain circumstances - be a normal system reaction (see ABS operation). this is particularly likely on an unsurfaced road.

Operation

Turn the ignition on and check that the "ABS fault" warning light comes on for about 4 seconds, and then goes out.

Continue to Step 1

The "ABS fault" warning light comes on and stays on

See table K60 ABS FAILURE WARNING LIGHT ON

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the ABS control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E7020 ABS ).

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of:

  • pump motor;
  • solenoid valves;
  • speed sensors

Continue to Step 3

Fault detected in one solenoid

Replace electrohydraulic unit-electronic control unit Op. 3340A12 A'B.S. system. - electronic and hydraulic CONTROL UNIT R.R.

Fault detected at the front

Replace the sensor Op. 3340A30 rpm sensor (one) of one l/h or r/h front wheel - r+r

Fault detected in a rear sensor

Replace the sensor Op. 3340A34 rpm sensor (one) of one l/h or r/h rear wheel - r+r

Fault detected in a front wheel hub

Replace the wheel hub Op. 4450C28 front wheel hub - r r

Fault detected in a rear wheel hub

Replace the wheel hub Op. 4450D10 hub of one rear wheel, l/h or r/h - r+r with wheel removed

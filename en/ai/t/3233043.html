H01 VEHICLE DRIFT

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst driving in a straight line, the vehicle tends to drift towards the right and/or the left.

If necessary, carry out a test on a straight, flat road with no cambers.

Check the effective deviation from the trajectory by, very carefully, loosening your grip on the steering wheel

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Check that the tyres are correctly fitted and that the wheels are correctly fixed

WHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the wheels are properly balanced
  • that the wheels rotate freely (correct fitting of brake calipers)

Continue to Step 2

Wheels not balanced

Balance the wheels

Incorrect fitting of front brake calipers

Restore the correct fitting or replace the brake calipers Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

Incorrect fitting of rear brake calipers

Restore the correct fitting or replace the brake calipers Op. 3310B36 rear brake calliper - r r

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Angles not correct

Restore, where possible, the correct angles

WHEEL BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and operation of the wheel hub bearings

Continue to Step 6

Front wheel bearing worn or damaged

Replace the wheel hub bearing Op. 4450C32 one front wheel hub bearing, lh or rh - replace

Rear wheel bearing worn or damaged

Replace the wheel hub bearing Op. 4450D14 hub axle pillar of one rear wheel, l/h or r/h - r+r with hub removed - includes bush replacement if necessary

FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS AND BUSHES, FRONT AREA, CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings. In the order:

  • steering box rods
  • flexible supports for front selector forks and track control arms
  • anti-roll bar flexible mountings

Continue to Step 7

Steering box rods worn or damaged

Replace the rods Op. 4110B14 steering links - r+r with steering box removed

Arm supports worn or damaged

Replace the supports (see Assembly 4410B)

Selector fork supports worn or damaged

Replace the supports (see Assembly 4410A)

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or damaged

Replace the mountings Op. 4410C12 front suspension anti roll barjoint(end)(one), lh or rh - r r

7

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the front crossmember and the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 8

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

Engine support crossmeber faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torque Op. 4410D13 front suspension beam - r r

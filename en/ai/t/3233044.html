H02 DRIFT WHILST BRAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During braking, the car pulls to the right and/or left.

If necessary, carry out a test on a straight, flat road with no cambers.

Check the effective deviation from the trajectory by braking and very carefully loosening your grip on the steering wheel

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Check that the wheels are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

Wheel fixing not correct

Tighten the wheels correctly Op. 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1

CHECK PAD TYPE AND SURFACE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of pad friction surfaces
  • pad type

Continue to Step 2

Friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault

Front brake pads damaged or wrong type

Replace pads Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replace

Rear pads damaged or wrong type

Replace the pads Op. 3310B60 rear brake pads - replace - includes handbrake adjustment if necessary

2

CHECK DISCS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the brake discs
  • the type of discs

Continue to Step 3

Front discs worn or damaged

Skim or if necessary replace the discs Op. 3310A48 front brake disc - r r

Rear discs worn or damaged

Replace the discs or, if necessary, replace them Op. 3310B48 rear brake disc - r r

3

CHECK BRAKE CALIPERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the brake caliper pistons

Continue to Step 4

Front caliper pistons worn or seized

Replace brake calipers Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

Rear brake calipers worn or seized

Replace brake calipers Op. 3310B36 rear brake calliper - r r

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

  • Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit.

Continue to Step 5

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the supply for the ABS control unit ( See E7020 ABS ).

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of:

  • pump motor
  • solenoid valves

Continue to Step 6

Fault detected in the pump or a solenoid

Replace electrohydraulic unit-electronic control unit Op. 3340A12 A'B.S. system. - electronic and hydraulic CONTROL UNIT R.R.

Leaks or damage in the components of the circuit

Replace the components involved or replace the brake servo unit Op. 3330D10 brake servo - r+r - includes separation from master cylinder (pump)

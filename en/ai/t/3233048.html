The steering oscillates rapidly along a straight road at sustained speed.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Check that the wheels are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

Wheel fixing not correct

Tighten the wheels correctly Op. 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1

WHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the wheels are properly balanced
  • the condition of the wheel rims (there should not be dents or distortions)

Continue to Step 2

Wheels not balanced

Balance the wheels

Wheel rims dented or distorted

Replace the wheel rims

2

WHEEL ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the alignment of the wheels

Continue to Step 3

Alignment not correct

Restore the correct wheel alignment

3

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the front crossmember and the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 4

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

Engine supports beam faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torque Op. 4410D13 front suspension beam - r r

4

BUSHES AND FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings. In the order:

  • steering box rods
  • flexible supports for front selector forks and track control arms
  • anti-roll bar flexible mountings

Continue to Step 5

Steering box rods worn or damaged

Replace the links Op. 4110B14 steering links - r+r with steering box removed

Arm supports worn or damaged

Replace the supports (see Assembly 4410B)

Selector fork supports worn or damaged

Replace the supports (see Assembly 4410A)

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or damaged

Replace the mountings Op. 4410C12 front suspension anti roll barjoint(end)(one), lh or rh - r r

5

SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition and the efficiency of the shock absorbers and the springs
  • the fixings for the spring/shock absorber assembly

Continue to Step 6

Tightening not correct

Restore the correct fixing

Shock absorbers not working properly

Replace the shock absorber involved Op. 4410A12 front suspension damper (one), lh or rh - for mcpherson strut system - includes dismantling/re-building of assembly at bench r r

Springs not working properly

Replace the spring involved Op. 4410A12 front suspension damper (one), lh or rh - for mcpherson strut system - includes dismantling/re-building of assembly at bench r r

6

STEERING TIE-RODS AND ARMS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the steering tie-rods and arms.

Continue to Step 7

Tie-rods damaged or worn

Replace the tie-rids or balljoints Op. 4110B14 steering links - r+r with steering box removed

Arms damaged or worn

Replace the steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

7

STEERING COLUMN BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and efficiency of the steering column support bearings

Continue to Step 8

Bearings worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column.

If necessary, replace the bearings involved Op. 4110A30 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R.

8

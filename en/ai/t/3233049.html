Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the engine running and the vehicle stationary, the steering wheel oscillates and vibrates

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tyre inflation pressure

Preliminarily check the engine idle speed

Continue to Step 1

Engine idle irregular

See table D10 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

1

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the front crossmember and the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 2

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

Engine support crossmember faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to torque Op. 4410D13 front suspension beam - r r

2

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the efficiency of the steering column support. In particular, check:

  • that there is no clearance between the support bushes and the steering column
  • that the steering column joints are correctly tightened

Continue to Step 3

Bushes worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column.

If necessary, replace the bearings involved Op. 4110A30 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R.

Spider joint clearance too large

Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column Op. 4110A30 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R.

3

STEERING WHEEL ADJUSTMENT DEVICE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check th condition of the steering wheel adjustment device. In particular check:

  • that the clearance between the device and the steering column is correct

Continue to Step 4

Coupling with excessive clearance

Restore the efficiency of the steering wheel adjustment device. If necessary, replace it

4

TIMING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the engine timing is correct

Continue to Step 5

Timing not correct

Restore the correct engine timing Op. 1032B14 toothed timing belt - r + r for timing adjustment or replacement versions with air conditioner

5

FLYWHEEL BALANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the flywheel balance is correct

Continue to Step 6

Balance not correct

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B10 engine flywheel - r + r for versions with manual gearbox

6

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

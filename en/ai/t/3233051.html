I01 SIDE LIGHTS/NO. PLATE LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the steering column switch unit ring nut is operated, the side lights or the number plate lights do not work and this fault is indicated by the light failure warning light.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the oepration of the light check device. If the side lights or number plate lights do not work, the light failure warning light comes on.

Continue to Step 1

The fault is not indicated by the light failure warning light.

See table I30 LIGHTS CHECK NOT WORKING

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs

Rear lights cluster faulty

Repair or replace the rear lights cluster Op. 5540A80 rear exterior lightbulb - r r

Front lights cluster faulty

Repair or replace front lights cluster Op. 5540B26 bulb (one) for side lights of one headlamp/light cluster, l/h or r/h - r+r

No. plate lights faulty

Renew or replace the no. plate lights Op. 5540A94 l/h or r/h number plate light (one) - r+r

2

SIDE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

  • connections between the ignition switch and the stalk unit
  • the connection between the stalk unt and the rev counter
  • connections between the rev counter and bulbs
  • bulbs earth

( See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).

Continue to Step 3

Connections broken

Restore the connections (

3

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the stalk unit is working properly: in particular, check the side light switch see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5550A stalk unit

Continue to Step 4

Stalk unit not working properly

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

4

CHECK IGNITION SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the side lights come on when the ignition key is turned in the opposite direction

Continue to Step 5

Ignition switch faulty

Renew or replace the ignition switch Op. 5520A10

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Check that the fault is no longer present

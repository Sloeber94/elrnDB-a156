Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The rear fog lamp does not work when the switch on the control panel in the centre of the facia is operated with the dipped beams on (or fog lamp on). This fault is indicated by the light failure warning light

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check operation of the light check device. The light failure warning light will come on if the rear fog lamps do not work.

Check the operation of the dipped beam headlamps

Continue to Step 1

The fault is not indicated by the light failure warning light

See table I30 LIGHTS CHECK NOT WORKING

See table I05 DIPPED HEADLAMPS NOT WORKING

All OK

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs

Bulb holder faulty

CHECK REAR FOG LAMP ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between rear fog lamp switch and rev counter
  • connections between rev couner and bulbs
  • bulbs earth
  • rear fog lamp switch earth

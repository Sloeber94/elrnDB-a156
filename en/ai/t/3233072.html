J22 DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, when the appropriate button on the climate control panel is pressed, the door mirrors do not warm up properly.

If the battery voltage is not below 11.6 V, the heated rear window and door mirrors are not supplied by the control unit.

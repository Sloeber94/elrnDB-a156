Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the rev counter does not work or it works irregularly

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the engine management control unit: in particular, check that the rev counter signal is sent correctly by the control unit

Continue to Step 2

Signal not sent

Restore the connection between the rev counter and the engine control unit See E4040 INSTRUMENT WARNING LIGHTS CHECK

2

REV COUNTER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • ignition-operated service fuse
  • rev counter supply
  • rev counter earth

( See E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT ).

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECK REV COUNTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the rev counter is working see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 4

Instrument not working

Replace the rev counter Op. 5560B03 rev counter - r r

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

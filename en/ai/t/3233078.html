Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the speedometer works properly, but does not manage to switch to the outside temperature reading.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speedometer

Continue to Step 1

The speedometer is not working at all

See table K05 MILOMETER NOT WORKING

1

ZEROING BUTTON CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speedometer zeroing button

( See E4021 SPEEDOMETER ).

Continue to Step 2

Zeroing button faulty

Renew or replace the zeroing button Op. 5540C18 headlamp/light cluster alignment correction motor switch - r+r

2

OUTSIDE TEMPERATURE SIGNAL ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the connection between the speedometer and the climate control unit
  • the connection between the speedometer and the zeroing button
  • the trip meter zeroing button earth

( See E4021 SPEEDOMETER ).

Continue to Step 3

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the climate control unit, in particular check the outside temperature sensor and the signal

Continue to Step 4

Signal not sent

Check and, if necessary, replace the outside temperature sensor Op. 5010D12 outside air temperature sensor on door mirror - r+r following electrical circuit check

Climate control unit faulty

Check and, if necessary, replace the climate control unit Op. 5040D18 air conditioner control unit - r r

4

SPEEDOMETER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speedometer, in particular the outside temperature display function: see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 5

Instrument not working

Replace the speedometer Op. 5560B02

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

Check that the fault is no longer present

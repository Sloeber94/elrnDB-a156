Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

On starting, the "i.e. fault" warning light does not come on (it should come on for 4 seconds to indicate the initial test of the control unit, and then go out)

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there is correct dialogue with the engine management control unit

Continue to Step 2

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • connection between control unit and diagnostic connector
  • engine management control unit supply ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT ).

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECK I.E. FAULT WARNING LIGHT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

2.5 V6

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments), carry out active diagnosis for the "i.e. fault" warning light

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

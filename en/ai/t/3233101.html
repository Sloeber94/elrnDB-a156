Applies to: all engines; all production periods

On starting, the passenger Air Bag disablement warning light does not come on (it should come on for 4 seconds to indicate the initial test on the Air Bag control unit, and then go out)

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there is a regular dialogue with the Air Bag control unit

Continue to Step 2

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the Air Bag control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the Air Bag control unit supply

( See E7030 AIRBAG ).

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING SPEEDOMETER POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connection between airbag control unit and speedometer
  • speedometer power supply

( See E4021 SPEEDOMETER ).

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

3

SPEEDOMETER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the speedometer: see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 4

Instrument not working

Replace the speedometer Op. 5560B02

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

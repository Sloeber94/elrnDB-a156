When the ignition is on, the instrument panel comes on and the Infocenter does not light up

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the power supply to the Infocenter

See E4024 INFOCENTER INSTRUMENT .

Continue to Step 2

Positive supply incorrect

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Infocenter earth incorrect

Renew the correct earth for the Infocenter

2

CHECKING INFOCENTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Infocenter is working properly

Continue to Step 3

Infocenter faulty

Replace Infocenter

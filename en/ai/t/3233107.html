Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan which sends air into the passenger compartment is not working properly, for example, the different speeds do not always work, or the air flow is not constant

Results of the tests

1

POLLEN FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pollen filter

It should be remembered that the pollen filter should be replaced about once a year.

Continue to Step 2

Filter obstructed

Replace the pollen filter Op. 5010B09 purifying filter (antipollen) filter element - replace

2

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

  • Check that there are no faults in the climate control control unit

Continue to Step 3

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the climate control control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the climate control control unit supply.

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

3

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments), carry out the active diagnosis for the electric fan at the different speeds

Continue to Step 4

Electric fan faulty

Replace the electric fan Op. 5040C30 fan motor - r+r

Electronic speed variator faulty

Replace the electronic speed variator Op. 5040C44 fan motor electronic variator - r+r

4

CHECKING THE AIR DUCTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the air ducts inside the dashboard and the vents are intact and there are no obstructions

Continue to Step 5

Ducts obstructed or damaged

Restore the operation of the ducts and the vents PROCEDURE — Cmp. 5010B.

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

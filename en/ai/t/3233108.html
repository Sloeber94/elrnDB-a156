L10 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit operates the fan which sends air into the passenger compartment normally with the pre-set flows, but this air is at the outside temperature and is not cooled.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the compressor drive belt

Continue to Step 1

Tension incorrect

Replace the belt Op. 1092G10 single engine belt r r

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

  • Check that there are no faults in the climate control control unit
  • Check that there are no fault in the engine management control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in one of the two control units

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the climate control control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the climate control control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the climate control control unit supply.

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

2

COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the operation of the compressore relay (in the engine management control unit ambient)

Continue to Step 3

Compressore relay faulty

Check and, if necessary, replace the compressor engagement relay

4 stage pressure switch faulty

Replace the 4 stage pressure switch Op. 5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

Connections not correct

Restore the connection between the climate control control unit and the compressor coupling, via the 4 stage pressure switch and the engine management control unit.

( See E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT ).

3

CHECK COMPRESSOR ELECTRO-MAGNET COUPLING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the compressor electro-magnet coupling and, in particular, that it is correctly connected to earth

Continue to Step 4

Compressor electro-magnet coupling malfunction

Replace the compressor Op. 5040A20 air conditioner compressor - r+r - includes fluid level check and top up if necessary complete with electro-magnet coupling

4

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the system pressure: See Test 5040B Check on operation of air conditioning system (pressures)

Continue to Step 5

High pressure does not increase or low pressure decreases too much: leaks in the circuit

Locate these leaks Op. 5040A14 air conditioner cooling circuit - check and repair any leaks by tightening fittings , then restore the condition of the circuit

High pressure continues to increase: and the compressor cooling fan does not operate.

See table D30 ENGINE OVERHEATING / WITH THE FAN WORKING

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no reasons why the climate control control unit or the engine control unit do not enable the engagement of the cooling circuit.

For more details see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS

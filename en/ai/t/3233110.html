L12 CLIMATE CONTROL TOO COLD

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit regularly operates the fan which sends air to the passenger compartment in accordance with the pre-set flows, but this air lowers the high temperature in the passenger compartment too much

This fault may be accompanied by a sudden increase in the temperature of the air sent to the passenger compartment

This is due to the operation of the system which switches off the cooling circuit if the temperature at the evaporator decreases too much.

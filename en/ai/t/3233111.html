L15 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT HEATING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit operates the fan which sends air into the passenger compartment normally with the pre-set flows, but this air does not guarantee the required heating.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

  • Check that there are no faults in the climate control control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the climate control control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the climate control control unit supply.

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

2

MIXTURE MOTOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments), carry out the active diagnosis for the mixture motor

Continue to Step 3

Mixture motor connection faulty

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

Mixture motor faulty

Replace the mixture motor Op. 5040D77 air conditioner mixer flap control motor - r r

Mixture motor flap faulty

Replace the mixture motor flap Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

3

HEATER PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency and the seal of the supply and return pipes from the heater; with the engine hot, the pipes should be hot

Continue to Step 4

Pipes not connected

Restore the correct connection

Pipes dirty or obstructed

Clean the pipes or replace them Op. 5010A10

4

HEATER RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the heater radiator

Continue to Step 5

Pipes not connected

Restore the correct connection

Radiator dirty or obstructed

Clean the radiator or replace it Op. 5040C50 air conditioner heater radiator - r+r

6

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

The recirculation function is not working properly

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the climate control control unit.

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the climate control control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the climate control control unit supply.

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

2

MIXTURE MOTOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis for the recirculation motor

Continue to Step 3

Recirculation motor faulty connection

Restore the connection between the control unit and the recirculation motor.

( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

Recirculation motor faulty

Replace the recirculation motor Op. 5040D75

Recirculation motor flap faulty

Replace the recirculation flap Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

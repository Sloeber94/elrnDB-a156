The fan which sends air to the passenger compartment is not operating

Results of the tests

1

CLIMATE CONTROL FAN ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • climate control fan fuse
  • climate control fan
  • climate control fan controls
  • connection between climate control fan and its controls, via the adjustment resistor

( See E6010 HEATER ).

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Electric fan defective

Replace the electric fan Op. 5020A30 heater fan motor - r+r

2

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

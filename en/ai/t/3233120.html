M10 (ELECTRICALLY OPERATED) REAR WINDOWS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the buttons on the doors are operated, the rear electric windows do not work properly.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no obstacles preventing the travel of the window

Continue to Step 1

Presence of obstacles

Restore the correct mechanical operation of the window - up/down

1

STOP DEVICE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the rear electric windows stop device

( See E3051 ELECTRIC REAR WINDOWS ).

Continue to Step 2

Stop device faulty

Replace the stop device Op. 7010M16 rear electric window enablement switch - r+r

2

ELECTRIC WINDOW SWITCHES CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the rear electric window switches (both those located on the appropriate door and those located on the driver's door)

Continue to Step 3

Switch faulty

Replace the electric window switch (on the rear door) on the driver's (front) door Op. 7005M10 driver's side front side door glass control switch (one) r r

3

ELECTRIC WINDOWS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • electric rear windows fuse
  • services relay
  • stop device supply and earth
  • switches supply and earth
  • connection between the switches and the electric window motors

( See E3051 ELECTRIC REAR WINDOWS ).

Continue to Step 4

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

4

ELECTRIC WINDOW MOTORS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the electric window motors

Continue to Step 5

Electric window motor faulty

Replace the electric window motor Op. 7010M21 rear side door electrically-operated window-r r

5

ELECTRIC WINDOWS DEVICE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the mechanical part of the electric windows control device and that it is correctly fixed

Continue to Step 6

Electric window device not correctly fixed or adjusted

Restore the operation of the electric window device Op. 7010M25 lh rear side doorelectrically-operated window mechanism-check position and fastening

Electric window device faulty

Replace the electric window device Op. 7010M21 rear side door electrically-operated window-r r

6

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The radio works properly, but the reception of the stations is not the same as previously.

Results of the tests

1

AERIAL CONNECTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connection between radio and aerial/rear window control unit
  • condition of aerial and rear window control unit

See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore connections

Fault in aerial and rear window control unit

Repair or replace aerial and rear window control unit Op. 5570T28 ecu for car radio aerial in rear window heater element - r+r

2

AERIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • continuity of aerial coaxial cable (between radio and aerial/rear window control unit)
  • that rear window filament is intact

if the heated rear window is not working either See table J21 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN NOT WORKING

Continue to Step 3

Aerial cable faulty

Repair or replace aerial cable Op. 5570T31 aerial coaxial lead - r r

3

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

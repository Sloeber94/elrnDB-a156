Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever is operated, the seat does not slide forwards or backwards along the guides

The mechanism works better when a person is sitting on the seat.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • absence of obstacles or dirt on the travel
  • correct lubrication of the guides

Continue to Step 1

Presence of obstacles or dirt along the travel

Restore correct operation

Absence of lubrication

Repair correct lubrication

1

CHECK SEAT RUNNER ATTACHMENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the seat ( Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r ).

Check that the seat runners are attached correctly to the car floor

Continue to Step 2

Faulty attachment of runners

Reattach the seat runners correctly

2

CHECK SEAT RUNNER GUIDES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the seat runner guides and the absence of deformation

Check that the guides are attached correctly to the seat

Continue to Step 3

Faulty guide attachment

Refit the guides correctly Op. 7045A22 l/h or r/h front seat support/guide - r+r with seat removed

Faulty guides

Replace the seat runner guides Op. 7045A22 l/h or r/h front seat support/guide - r+r with seat removed

3

CHECK SEAT SLIDE RELEASE LEVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the release lever for the longitudinal adjustment of the seat. In particular check its condition and that it is fitted correctly

Continue to Step 4

Release lever faulty

Repair or replace the release lever Op. 7045A29 seat slide control lever - r r

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

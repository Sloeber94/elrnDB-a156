Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the knob is operated, the seat squab does not recline as required

The mechanism works better when a person is sitting on the seat.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING SEAT SQUAB RECLINE DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the squab recline device (knob)

In particular, check its condition and that it is fixed correctly

Continue to Step 2

Fault

Repair or change the seat squab recline knob Op. 7045A24 squab knob - r r

2

CHECKING SEAT SQUAB JOINT UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the seat ( Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r ).

Check that there are no obstacles or foreign material in the way of the squab

Check the condition of the cushion/squab joint unit, inside and out

Continue to Step 3

Presence of obstacles or dirt along the travel

Restore correct operation

External cushion/squab joint unit faulty

Replace the device

Internal cushion/squab joint unit faulty

Change the device

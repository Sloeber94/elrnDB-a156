Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat will not go up or down when the lever is operated

The mechanism only works when someone is sitting on the seat.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING SEAT LIFT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the outer seat guard ( Op. 7045A43 ).

Check that there are no obstacles or foreign material

Check the seat lift device. In particular, check the condition of the device gear teeth and ensure it is properly secured.

Continue to Step 1

Presence of obstacles or dirt along the travel

Restore correct operation

Device faulty

Repair or replace the device Op. 7045A36

2

CHECKING SEAT JOINTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the seat ( Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r ).

Check the condition of the seat lift joints

Continue to Step 3

External seat joints faulty

Repair seat joints

3

CHECKING GAS SPRINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

faulty gas springs make the seat lift manoeuvre particularly difficult. Seat lowering is less affected.

Check the gas spring for seat lifting. In particular, check its condition and that it is fixed correctly

Continue to Step 4

Gas spring faulty

Repair installation or replace the gas spring Op. 7045A60 front seat lift device - r r

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

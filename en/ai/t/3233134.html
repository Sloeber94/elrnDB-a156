Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the appropriate knob is operated, the squab does not straighten ("lumbar" adjustment)

Results of the tests

1

CHECK LUMBAR ADJUSTMENT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the lumbar adjustment device (knob)

In particular, check its condition and that it is fixed correctly

Continue to Step 2

Knob faulty

Repair or replace lumbar adjustment knob Op. 7045A25 squab lumbar knob - r r

2

CHECK LUMBAR ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the squab cover ( Op. 7045A21 Front seat BACKREST COVER, left or right - R.R. ).

Check that there are no obstacles or foreign material in the lumbar adjustment mechanism

Check the condition of the traction mechanism cables: they must not be damaged, nor must they emerge from their seating.

Continue to Step 3

Presence of obstacles or dirt along the travel

Restore the correct operation of the mechanism

Faulty lumbar adjustment mechanism

Repair or replace the device Op. 7045A38

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

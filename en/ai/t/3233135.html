Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, when the appropriate button on the front seats is pressed, the heating function does not come on.

Device operation is automatically cut out at a certain temperature by a thermal contact inside the device.

0

All OK

Problems met

Operation

When the button on the seat is pressed, check that the led on the button comes on to indicate that the function has been activated.

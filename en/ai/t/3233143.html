Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The sunroof does not move at all when its control is operated

Results of the tests

1

CHECK SUNROOF SUPPLY CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • sunroof supply fuse
  • sunroof supply fuse
  • sunroof electronic control unit
  • connections between relay, fuse and sunroof electronic control unit

See E3060 SUN ROOF

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Electronic control unit not working

Replace control unit Op. 7030B66 sun-roof movement ecu - r+r

2

CHECK SUNROOF MOTOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the sunroof motor In particular, ascertain:

  • condition of motor pinion
  • state of control rack

Continue to Step 3

Faults in motor components

Replace the motor Op. 7030B70 sun-roof control switch - r+r

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

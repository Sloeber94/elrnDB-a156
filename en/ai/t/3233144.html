Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the control is operated, the sun-roof opens/closes in irregular manner. It sticks at some points or remains locked at an angle

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the glass Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r

Check the sun-roof operating devices are working properly. Check in particular:

  • control cable condition
  • slide and guide condition
  • presence of end travel blocks

Continue to Step 2

Dirty guides

Clean the guides and repair the slide mechanism

End stop screws missing

Replace the screws and the entire slide mechanism

Guides disconnected

Repair the slide mechanism Op. 7030B50 sun roof guide , one , lh or rh - r r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the motor. Op. 7030B70 sun-roof control switch - r+r

Check that sun-roof opening devices are working properly. Check in particular:

  • condition of motor pinion
  • control cable condition

Continue to Step 3

faults in sun-roof operating devices

Replace the components involved

3

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICE ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check installation of the sun-roof assembly

In particular, ascertain:

  • slides are properly aligned with timing points
  • flexible racks are properly secured and undamaged

Continue to Step 4

Faulty fitting of the sunroof

Refit the sunroof correctly Op. 7030B54 sun roof systems

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

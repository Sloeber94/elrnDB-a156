S02 ENGINE KNOCK / CYCLICAL WHEN WARM

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The engine is making a cyclical noise, noticeable when the engine is hot during brisk acceleration and using long ratios

Preliminarily check:

the type of fuel

that there is no water or dirt in the tank

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the type of fuel

Preliminarily check that there is no water or other fluids in the fuel tank

Continue to Step 1

Presence of dirt in the fuel tank

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel

Fuel unsuitable

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel and fill with suitable fuel

1

TYPE OF NOISE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test and check whether the noise sounds metallic during brisk acceleration and using long ratios

Metallic sound:

Continue to Step 2

Duller sound: probably knocking

Overhaul the engine: check the condition of the main bearings and replace them: Op. 1024A10 crank shaft - r + r with engine removed - check main and connecting rod bearingand replace if necessary

2

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 3

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector

See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

3

PISTON CROWN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the cylinder head and check the condition of the piston crowns

Continue to Step 4

Deposits on the piston crowns

Remove the deposits or replace the pistons and connecting rods (see Assembly 1028H)

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

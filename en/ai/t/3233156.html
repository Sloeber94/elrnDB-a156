With the engine running and the vehicle moving, there is a noise from the area of the gearbox in all conditions.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary test to check the conditions which give rise to the noise

General noise:

Continue to Step 1

Noise during take-off

See table S21 NOISE' GEARBOX / AT TAKE-OFF

Noise in neutral

See table S23 NOISE' GEARBOX / IN A SPECIFIC GEAR

1

GEARBOX/DIFFERENTIAL OIL LEVEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox / differential oil level Op. 2110A04 Manual gearbox and differential OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary

Continue to Step 2

Level too low

Restore the correct level

2

CHECK GEARS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox (SEE GEARBOX SERVICING Assembly 2110B) Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings In particular, check the gear teeth: they should not be worn or show traces of seizing

Continue to Step 3

Gears worn

Replace the gears involved

3

CHECK SYNCHRONIZER RINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox (SEE GEARBOX SERVICING Assembly 2110B) In particular, check the gear teeth: they should not be worn or show traces of seizing

Continue to Step 4

Seals worn

Replace the synchronizers

4

REDUCTION TORQUE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the crown wheel and pinion reduction gear (see OVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110D)

In particular:

  • it should not be worn or damaged
  • there should not be excessive clearance

Continue to Step 5

Reduction gear damaged

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

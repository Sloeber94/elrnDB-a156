During braking, a whistle or screech may be heard from the area of the wheels. Vibration may also be detected in the interior compartment.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Steer to the right or the left and check whether the noise increases.

Noise does not change:

Continue to Step 1

The noise varies during steering

See table S45 NOISE' FRONT / ROUND BENDS

1

CHECK PAD TYPE AND SURFACE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of pad friction surfaces
  • pad type

Continue to Step 2

Front friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Brake pads wrong type

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault. Replace the pads if necessary Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replace

Rear friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Brake pads wrong type

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault. Replace the pads if necessary. Op. 3310B60 rear brake pads - replace - includes handbrake adjustment if necessary

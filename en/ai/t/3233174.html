When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the rear of the car interior

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise really is coming from the rear of the car interior

Continue to Step 1

The noise comes from one of the rear doors

See table S63 REAR DOORS CREAKING

1

CHECK REAR SEAT ATTACHMENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • cushion to bodywork
  • squab to boot compartment wall
  • rear seat belt attachments to bodywork

Continue to Step 2

Fastenings not correct

Refix correctly (see Assembly 7050A)

2

CHECK PARCEL SHELF

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Dismantle the parcel shelf and check:

  • presence of sound-deadening rubber buffers on parcel shelf rear hooks
  • correct attachment of components on parcel shelf (e.g. loudspeakers)
  • correct insulation of boot wiring junction

Continue to Step 3

Rubber buffers or insulation missing

Restore rubber buffers and insulating material

Attachments faulty

Refit components correctly on parcel shelf Op. 7040H84

3

CHECK REAR PILLAR COVERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the pillar covers and check:

  • presence of felt between pillar cover and pillar
  • presence of felt between pillar cover and parcel shelf

Continue to Step 4

Felt missing

Refit felt

4

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the rear of the car interior

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise really is coming from the rear of the car interior

Continue to Step 1

The noise is coming from the rear doors

See table S63 REAR DOORS CREAKING

1

CHECK REAR SEAT ATTACHMENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • the backrest to the attachments on the floor and the adjustment of the actual backrest
  • rear seat belt attachments to bodywork
  • luggage cover
  • presence of vibration felt/foam and correct fixing of rear blinds

Continue to Step 2

Fastenings not correct

Restore the correct fixing

2

LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT AREA CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the components in the luggage compartment are correctly fitted:

  • side brackets - flaps on brackets and rubber mountings correctly tightened
  • presence of rubber mountings on tailgate and correct adjustment
  • spare wheel correctly fitted in housing and not moving

Continue to Step 3

Damaged components

Restore the correct components

Fastenings not correct

Re-attach correctly

3

CHECK CORRECT FIXING OF BUMPER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the rear bumper is correctly fixed and aligned

Continue to Step 4

Rear bumper damaged

Restore correct fitting/alignment of bumper

4

CHECK REAR PILLAR COVERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the pillar covers and check:

  • presence of felt between pillar cover and pillar
  • correct fixing of electrical wiring
  • check that the height adjusters are correctly fixed
  • rear seat belt attachments to bodywork

Continue to Step 5

Felt missing

Renew the felt

6

TAILGATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the felt/foam inside the tailgate is present and correctly secured and that the wiring is correctly secured inside the actual tailgate

Continue to Step 5

Wiring not correctly secured

Reconnect the wiring correctly

Felt inside the tailgate missing

Position the felt correctly

6

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

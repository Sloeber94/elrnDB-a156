S61 DASHBOARD CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the area of the dashboard.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Road test the vehicle to try and identify the area where the creaking is coming from.

For example, press the various parts described, checking whether the creaking disappears

Move directly onto the step involved

End of diagnosis

The creaking is not located

Continue to Step 1

Noise coming from one of the front doors

See table S62 FRONT DOORS CREAKING

1

DASHBOARD/WINDSCREEN MATCHING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the gap between the lower area of the windscreen and the dashboard is correct: it should be possible to pass a piece of card through this opening

Continue to Step 2

Dashboard fixing faulty

Renew the felt behind the dashboard in the contact area with the windscreen

2

VENT FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the tabs fixing the air vents to the dashboard

Continue to Step 3

Vents fixing faulty

Renew the felt or replace the damaged vents (see Assembly 5010C)

3

INSTRUMENTS FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the three instrument modules behind the steering wheel are correctly fixed: in particular, make sure that the felt is fitted in the dashboard matching area

Continue to Step 4

Instrument fixings behind the steering wheel faulty

Restore the correct fixing for the instruments.

Renew any felt, if necessary (see Assembly 5560B)

Check instrument fixings behind the steering wheel faulty

Restore the correct fixing of the check instrument.

Renew any felt, if necessary (see Assembly 5560C)

4

STEERING COLUMN COVER FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column cover is correctly fixed to the dashboard: in particular, make sure that the following are present:

  • insulating material around the cables
  • felt in the matching area with the dashboard
  • free movement of the steering wheel height adjustment device
  • steering column cover casing fixings

Continue to Step 5

Fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing to the dashboard.

Restore any felt, if necessary Op. 7040A26 steering column trim - r+r

5

CENTRE COVER FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the cover for the centre console in the dashboard is correctly fixed, in particular make sure that the following are present:

  • correct fixing of centre instrument module
  • insulating material around the cables
  • felt in the matching area with the dashboard

Continue to Step 6

Centre console fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing

Renew any felt, if necessary Op. 7040A60 under facia trim - r+r

Centre instrument module fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing Op. 5560C51 combined analogue control panel - r+r or replace

6

CLIMATE CONTROL CONTROLS FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the climate control control unit is correctly fixed (or the heater controls) in the dashboard

Continue to Step 7

Climate control control unit fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing Op. 5040D18 air conditioner control unit - r r

Heater controls fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing Op. 5020B10 heater control support - r+r

7

GLOVE COMPARTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the glove compartment and the flap are correctly fixed in the dashboard: in particular, make sure that the rubber mounting is fitted

Continue to Step 8

Glove compartment fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing of the glove compartment in the dashboard.

Renew any felt, if necessary Op. 7040A31 flap assembly - r r

Flap fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing of the compartment flap in the dashboard. Renew any felt, if necessary Op. 7040A14 flap of glove compartment trim element (one) - arrange

The flap does not shut properly

Adjust the striker Op. 7040A33 flap striker - adjust

8

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

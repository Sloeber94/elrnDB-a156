S63 REAR DOORS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from one of the rear doors

Results of the tests

1

DOOR FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixing

  • of the door check strap
  • of the hinges (upper and lower)
  • of the lock

Continue to Step 2

Check strap fixing faulty

If necessary, lubricate the check strap; refit correctly Op. 7010A28 rear side door opening limiter-r r

Hinge fixing faulty

If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin; refit correctly Op. 7010A24 rear side door hinges - repair or replace with door removed

Lock fixing faulty

Refit the lock fixing correctly Op. 7010N21 rear side door lock motor-lock assembly r r

Fixing faulty

Refix the striker correctly Op. 7010B14 rear side door catch striker r r

2

DOOR PANEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the door panel Op. 7010E10 rear side door inner panel - r r or replace and check that there are no foreign bodies inside the door

Continue to Step 3

Presence of foreign bodies

Remove foreign bodies

3

DOOR INTERIOR FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixing

  • of the window opening mechanism (electric or manual)
  • of the moving window guides
  • of the moving window fixing element
  • of the door opening/closing linkage

Continue to Step 4

Window opening fixing faulty (mechanical)

Refit the window opening fixing correctly Op. 7010D36 rear side door window mechanism - check position and fastening with door panel removed

Window opening fixing faulty (electric)

Refit the window opening fixing correctly Op. 7010M24 front side door electrically operated window mechanism - check positioning and fastening

Guides and fixing element faulty

Refit the guides correctly Op. 7010D22 rear side door descending window glass guide (one) - r r with door panel (and support frame) removed

Fixing element faulty

Replace the fixing element Op. 7010D12 fixed window glass element - r r

Linkage fixing faulty

Renew the linkage fixing springs PROCEDURES - Assy. 7010C.

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

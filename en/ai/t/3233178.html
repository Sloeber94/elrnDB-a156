S65 SEATS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the one of the front seats.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING GUARD AND CONTROL FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • of external guards
  • of seat squab and lumbar adjustment knob
  • of seat belt anchorage

Continue to Step 2

External guard fastenings faulty

Refasten correctly and refit the felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A43

Internal guard fastenings faulty

Repair the fastenings and refit the felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A44 seat interior guard - r r

Seat squab adjustment knob fastenings faulty

Repair the fastening and refit the felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A24 squab knob - r r

Squab adjustment knob fastenings faulty

Refasten correctly and refi the felt pads if necessary Op. 7045A25 squab lumbar knob - r r

2

CHECKING SEAT RUNNER ATTACHMENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seat runners are properly fastened

  • to the car floor pan
  • to the seat

Continue to Step 3

Device fastening faulty

Reattach the seat runners correctly Op. 7045A23 front seat mounts - r r

3

CHECKING SEAT LIFT DEVICE (driver's side only)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seat lifting device In particular, check clearance is not excessive

The seat will lift only it someone is sitting in it

Continue to Step 4

Excessive clearance

Replace the seat lift device and fastenings Op. 7045A60 front seat lift device - r r

4

CHECKING SEAT SQUAB RECLINE DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seat squab recline device In particular, check that the clearance is not excessive

Continue to Step 5

Clearance excessive

Replace the seat squab recline device Op. 7045A24 squab knob - r r

5

CHECK SEAT SLIDE RELEASE LEVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the release lever for longitudinal adjustment of the seat.

In particular, check that the clearance is not excessive

Continue to Step 6

Excessive clearance

Replace the release lever Op. 7045A29 seat slide control lever - r r

6

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

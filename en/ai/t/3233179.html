Applies to: all engines; all production periods

While driving with the roof fully open, noise can be heard from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise can heard only when the sun-roof is open

Continue to Step 1

The noise can be heard when the roof is closed

See table S76 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED

The noise can be heard when the roof is tilted open

See table S77 SUN ROOF NOISY / WTH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • sun-roof glass to the slides
  • roof mechanism assembly to the body

Continue to Step 2

Faulty fastenings

Repair the fastening of the entire mechanism Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

Repair the roof fastening Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check sun-roof operating devices are working properly:

  • with the roof tilted open, ensure the rubber pads on the rear parts of the slides are present
  • with the roof tilted open, check the end stop springs and central channel slides are undamaged and properly fastened
  • with the roof fully open, check the spoiler rubber end stop pads are undamaged and properly fastened

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fastenings

Repair all components and refasten the entire mechanism (see Assembly 7030B)

Faults in sun-roof opening devices

Replace the components concerned (see Assembly 7030B)

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

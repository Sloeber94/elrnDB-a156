Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion with the sun-roof closed, a noise is heard coming from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof closed

Continue to Step 1

The noise occurs only with the roof open

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

The noise occurs only with the roof tilted open

See table S77 SUN ROOF NOISY / WTH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • roof glass to slides
  • roof mechanism assembly to body

Continue to Step 2

Fastenings faulty

Repair the mechanism fastenings Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

Repair the roof fastening Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove glass Op. 7030B18 sliding roof glass - r+r and check operation of the sun-roof operating devices

  • check the felt pads are present on the central beam
  • check the internal trim control springs are present and properly fastened
  • check that the teflon mount between slides and rails is present
  • check that the felt pads between spoiler and front channel are present

Continue to Step 3

Fastenings faulty

Repair all mechanism components and fastenings (see Assembly 7030B)

Sun-roof operating devices faulty

Replace the parts concerned (see Assembly 7030B)

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

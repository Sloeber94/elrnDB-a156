While in motion with the roof tilted open, noise is heard from the sun-roof area

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise is heard only with the roof tilted open

Continue to Step 1

The noise is heard with the roof open

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

The noise is heard with the roof closed

See table S76 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED

1

CHECKING SUN-ROOF FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the following are fixed correctly:

  • roof glass to slides
  • roof mechanism assembly to body

Continue to Step 2

Fastenings faulty

Refit the entire mechanism correctly Op. 7030B14 sliding roof - dismantle and rebuild with assembly removed

Refit the roof correctly Op. 7030B12 sliding roof (assembly) - r+r

2

CHECKING SUN-ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Checking operation of sun-roof devices

  • with the roof fully open, check that the felt pads beneath the front spoiler are present
  • with the roof fully open, check that the teflon mount between slides and rails is present
  • with the roof tilted open, check the clearance between central groove and slides
  • with the roof tilted open, check the pads sliding along the central groove are in good condition and properly fastened

Continue to Step 3

Fastenings faulty

Repair all mechanism components and fastenings (see Assembly 7030B)

Sun-roof operating devices faulty

Replace the parts concerned (see Assembly 7030B)

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, a noise is heard coming from the sun roof with it closed.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise disappears when the roof is opened

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the sun roof by carrying out the preliminary test for identifying the location of wind noise See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

The noise persists with the roof open

See table S75 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

The wind noise comes from another area

Check the diagnostic table corresponding to the area located.

1

CHECK SUNROOF ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fitting of the sun roof. In particular, make sure that the roof - compartment alignment is correct.

Continue to Step 2

Faulty fitting of the sunroof

Adjust the sun roof Op. 7030B17 sun-roof glass - fit

2

SUN ROOF ADJUSTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the sun roof end of travel adjustment is correct

Continue to Step 3

Electrical end of travel not aligned with the mechanical stops

Adjust the sun roof end of travel Op. 7030B54 sun roof systems

3

CHECK SUNROOF SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the sun roof seal

Continue to Step 4

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7030B22 glass seal - r+r with glass removed

4

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

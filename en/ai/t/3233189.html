With the car in motion, a noise is heard coming from the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly, check the boot is properly closed and ensure any external aerial is properly secured

Check the wind noise comes from the rear by carrying out the test for preliminary location of wind noise See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

1

CHECK TAIL-LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tail-lights. In particular, ascertain:

  • alignment between tail-lights and wing or boot
  • tail-light installation

Continue to Step 2

Exterior tail-lights incorrectly fitted

Refit the exterior tail-lights correctly Op. 5540A56 dipped/main beam electrical wiring - check component operation, line continuity and repair contacts - includes relay replacement if necessary

Interior tail-lights incorrectly fitted

Refit the interior tail-lights correctly Op. 5540A68 l/h or r/h interior tail light (one) - r+r

2

REARSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the rear window weather strip (there should be no wrinkles or kinks)

Continue to Step 3

Weather strip not fitted correctly

Adjust and if necessary change the weather strip by removing the window Op. 7035B10 Rearscreen (bonded type) WINDOW GLASS - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of glass and housing in bodyshell

3

With the car in motion, a noise is heard coming from the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly, check the correct closing and alignment with the surrounding surfaces (wings and roof) of the tailgate and the correct fixing of the radio phone aerial, if fitted, and the spoiler.

Check the wind noise comes from the rear by carrying out the test for preliminary location of wind noise See Test 7010A Location of rear door wind noise

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from another area

See the fault diagnosis for locating the area.

1

CHECK TAIL-LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tail lights and, in particular, check:

  • the alignment of the tail lights with the tailgate
  • the correct matching between the tailgate and the housing trim
  • the correct fitting/matching of the rear wheel arch

Continue to Step 2

Exterior tail-lights incorrectly fitted

Refit the exterior rear light clusters correctly ( Op. 5540A56 dipped/main beam electrical wiring - check component operation, line continuity and repair contacts - includes relay replacement if necessary )

Interior tail-lights incorrectly fitted

Refit the interior tail-lights correctly ( Op. 5540A56 dipped/main beam electrical wiring - check component operation, line continuity and repair contacts - includes relay replacement if necessary )

Components not correctly fitted

Restore the correct fitting/alignment of the components involved

2

REARSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the rearscreen sealant by carrying out a test:

  • See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 3

No sealant

Renew the sealant, following the instructions given in the test in question

3

ROOF RACK CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the roof rack and the bolt covers are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 4

Roof rack not correctly fitted

Refit the roof rack correctly

No bolt covers

Repositin the bolt covers correctly

4

THIRD SIDE WINDOW CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the perimeter of the sealant for the third side windows: See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 5

No sealant

Renew the sealant following the instructions for the test in question

5

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

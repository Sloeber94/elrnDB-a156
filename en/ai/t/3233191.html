With the car in motion, a smell is noted coming from the rear of the car, particularly after refuelling

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check for leaks from or damage to the fuel tank.

Continue to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Repair the fuel tank, or replace if necessary Op. 1040A14 fuel tank - r + r for replacement

1

CHECK AIR OUTLET FITTINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air vents. In particular ascertain:

  • fin position (they should open outward)
  • correct fitting

Continue to Step 2

fitting anomalies

Restore the fittings to the correct position Op. 5010C60 lh or rh rear air outlet -one- r r

2

CHECK TANK VENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the tank vent is positioned correctly

Continue to Step 3

Vent position incorrect

Reposition vent correctly Op. 1040A50

3

CHECK TANK FILLER FITTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fuel tank filler; in particular:

  • fitting compartment seal on wing
  • fitting condition
  • cap seal

Continue to Step 4

Filler or cap faulty

Repair fitting Op. 1040A30 fuel filler pipe - r + r

Repair cap Op. 1040A40 plug for fuel filler pipe - r + r

4

Problems met

With the car in motion, a smell of exhaust gas is noted coming from the rear area.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK AIR OUTLET FITTINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet fittings

In particular, ascertain:

  • fin position (they should open outward)
  • correct fitting

Continue to Step 2

fitting anomalies

Restore the fittings to the correct position Op. 5010C60 lh or rh rear air outlet -one- r r

2

CHECKING BOOT LID SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the boot lid seal. In particular, check the sealing strip for wrinkles

Continue to Step 3

Fitting of seal incorrect

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7025B66 boot compartment seal - r+r

3

CHECKING BOOT ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check tail-gate is correctly fitted. In particular, check the boot compartment/lid alignment. Ensure the sealing strip is in contact around the entire perimeter.

Continue to Step 4

Boot installation faulty

Refit the boot correctly Op. 7025B10 boot lid - square

4

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 5

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

5

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 6

Underbody plugs missing

Refit missing plugs

6

With the car in motion, a smell of exhaust gas is noted coming from the rear area.

Results of the tests

1

AIR VENTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet vents. In particular, ascertain:

  • Seal of fins (they should open outwards)
  • correct fitting

Continue to Step 2

fitting anomalies

Restore the fittings to the correct position ( Op. 5010C60 lh or rh rear air outlet -one- r r )

2

TAILGATE SEAL MATCHING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the seal matches the tailgate. In particular, make sure that the seal is not wrinkled.

Also check that no exhaust gases are penetrating through the openings in the lower part of the tailgate.

Continue to Step 3

Fitting of seal incorrect

Fit the sealing strip correctly

3

CHECK TAIL-GATE ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fitting of the tailgate. In particular, check the boot compartment alignment, ensuring that the sealing strip is in contact around the entire perimeter.

Continue to Step 4

Tailgate fitting faulty

Refit the tail-gate correctly

4

LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT SEALANT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the luggage compartment sealant is correct

Continue to Step 5

Luggage compartment sealant faulty

Restore the correct luggage compartment sealant

5

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs are fitted, in the luggage compartment housing

Continue to Step 6

Underbody plugs missing

Refit missing plugs

6

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

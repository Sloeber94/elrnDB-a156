Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control is working properly, but there is an unpleasant smell in the passenger compartment

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check that the unpleasant smells are not coming from the outside: they can be eliminated straight away by switching on the recirculation function

Continue to Step 1

Recirculation not working

See table L16 CLIMATE CONTROL RECIRCULATION NOT WORKING

1

CONDENSATION DRAINAGE PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the state of the condensation drainage pipe from the air conditioning unit

It should not be obstructed or dirty

Continue to Step 2

Pipe obstructed

Repair the drain pipe (see Assembly 5040B)

2

CHECK POLLEN FILTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pollen filter

It should be remembered that the pollen filter should be replaced about once a year.

Continue to Step 3

Filter obstructed

Replace the pollen filter Op. 5010B09 purifying filter (antipollen) filter element - replace

3

AIR SUPPLY DUCTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the air supply ducts: they should not be obstructed with dirt or foul smelling substances

Continue to Step 4

Vents malfunction

Wash the ducts (after removing the electric fan)

Restore the ducts (see Assembly 5010B)

4

HEATER RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the heater radiator: the outer fins should not be obstructed with dirt or other substances

Continue to Step 5

Radiator dirty or obstructed

Clean the radiator or replace it Op. 5040C50 air conditioner heater radiator - r+r

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

When it rains or after washing, water enters the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK THAT THERE IS NO PENETRATION THROUGH THE REARSCREEN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the sealant on the rearscreen See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 2

No sealant

Renew the sealant, following the instructions See Test 7035A windscreen

2

REARSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear windscreen. In particular, visually inspect:

  • the condition of the window
  • the flatness of the support surface

Continue to Step 3

Rearscreen faulty

Replace the rearscreen Op. 7035B14 rear window glass r r includes cleaning of housing

3

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When it is raining or after washing, water enters in the area of one of the rear doors

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING DOOR DRIP CHANNEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Checking the door drip channel In particular, ascertain:

  • no dirt, leaves etc. are present
  • corner area is properly fitted (the seal should not cover the air intake hole)

Continue to Step 2

Dirt in the drip channel

Clean the drip channel

Drip channel incorrectly fitted

Refit the drip channel properly Op. 7055A14 drip channels on door compartment (one), l/h or r/h - r+r

2

CHECK DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door seal In particular, check:

  • absence of wrinkles in the seal
  • condition of seal

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7010A34 rear side door compartment seal r r

3

CHECK DOOR ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door is properly fitted. In particular, ascertain:

  • door - compartment correctly aligned
  • seal correctly bonded to the door

Continue to Step 4

door incorrectly fitted

Refit the door properly Op. 7010A14 rear side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

4

CHECKING MOVING WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the moving glass seal In particular, ascertain:

  • the glass is properly closed
  • the glass channel is correctly positioned
  • the glass scraper is correctly positioned

Continue to Step 5

Channel incorrectly positioned

Refit the channel properly Op. 7010D18 rear side door descending window glass channel r r

Glass incorrectly fitted

Refit the window mechanism and moving glass correctly Op. 7010D10

Glass scraper incorrectly fitted

Refit the glass scraper correctly Op. 7010D26 rear side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r

5

CHECK DOOR DRAINAGE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the drainage on the lower part of the doors.

In particular, ascertain:

  • no dirt, leaves are present

Continue to Step 6

Dirt in drains

Clean the drains. If necessary refit correctly Op. 7010A46 lower rear side door seal r r

6

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

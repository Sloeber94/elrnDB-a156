Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When it is raining or after washing, water leaks in through the sunroof

Results of the tests

1

CHECK SUNROOF DRAINS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check sunroof drain pipes

In particular, ascertain:

  • condition of pipes
  • absence of dirt, leaves, etc.

Continue to Step 2

Dirt in drains

Clean the drains. If necessary refit correctly Op. 7030B38 sliding roof drainage pipes (water drain) - check

2

CHECK SUNROOF SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the sunroof seal. In particular, ascertain:

  • absence of wrinkles in the seal
  • condition of seal

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7030B22 glass seal - r+r with glass removed

3

CHECK SUNROOF ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check assembly of sunroof. In particular, ascertain:

  • correct sunroof - frame alignment
  • correct adherence of seal to roof

Continue to Step 4

Faulty fitting of the sunroof

Refit the sunroof correctly Op. 7030B17 sun-roof glass - fit

4

CHECK THAT BOLTS SECURING SUNROOF RUNNERS ARE SECURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the bolts fixing the runners to the sunroof frame are secure

Continue to Step 5

Bolts fitted incorrectly

Refit the runners correctly to the sunroof frame and check that they are secure

5

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

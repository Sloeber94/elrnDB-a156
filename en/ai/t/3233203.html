W02 CO EMISSIONS LOW, NOx EMISSIONS HIGH

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The CO percentage measured is very low, with a corresponding NOx percentage which is much higher than recommended.

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the engine management control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the injectors

Continue to Step 3

Connections faulty

Restore the connection of the solenoid valve with the control unit. See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Injectors faulty

Replace injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

3

Lambda sensor not working properly

CHECKING AIR CIRCUIT

CHECKING BLOW-BY CIRCUIT

All OK

Operation

Check the condition of the blow-by gas intake circuit. In particular, check that there are no obstructions in the separator or in the pipes

Continue to Step 6

Fault in the separator

Replace the separator Op. 1080D34 engine oil vapour separator - r + r

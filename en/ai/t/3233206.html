Operation

Restore the operation of the circuit; if necessary, replace the pipes (see Assembly 1080D)

Fault in the separator

CHECKING AIR CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the seal of the air supply circuit: in particular, make sure that there is no oil in the pipes

Continue to Step 4

Presence of oil in the pipes: penetration from the turbocharger

Clean the pipes again. Check and, if necessary, replace the turbocharger Op. 1064A10

Presence of oil in the pipes: leaks from a damage component in the circuit

Replace any damaged components (see Assembly 1048B)

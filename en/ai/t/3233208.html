X01 EXCESSIVE FUEL CONSUMPTION

Path

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

The engine appears to run smoothly, but the fuel consumption is higher than previously recorded during the life of the vehicle.

The consumption data in the vehicle technical documentation has been defined during the course of homologation tests in accordance with the directive in force. These tests simulate an urban cycle and an average extra-urban cycle and therefore may not be indicative as consumption depends very much on the style of driving.

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the engine management control unit:

  • in errors ambient
  • that there are no faults;
  • in parameters ambient
  • the plausibility of all the parameters, in particular:
  • engine temperature
  • ignition advances/poss. reduction for detonation
  • injection times
  • air mass
  • fuel consumption.

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Implausible parameters

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment, analyzing the causes that have led to the implausibility

X12 ENGINE OIL LEAKS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

An engine oil leak is seen with consequent dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary visual inspection to locate the component or the area giving rise to the leak.

If the visual inspection is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it and sprinkle talc over the parts concerned: carry out a road test to identify the leak

No leak located:

Continue to Step 1

Leak located

Move directly onto the step concerned

1

DRAIN PLUG CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the seal of the oil drain plug

Continue to Step 2

Drain plug tightening not correct

Restore the correct tightening Op. 1020A30 engine oil drain plug - replace

2

OIL SUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the oil sump
  • the seal of the sealant between the sump and crankcase
  • that the bolts fixing the sump are correctly tightened

Continue to Step 3

Oil sump damaged or distorted

Replace the oil sump Op. 1020A20 crank case oil sump - r + r clean seals for sealant application

No sealant or tightening incorrect

Restore the correct tightening Op. 1020A20 crank case oil sump - r + r clean seals for sealant application

3

OIL FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the oil filter
  • the seal and condition of the seal

Continue to Step 4

Oil filter damaged or distorted

Replace the oil filter and the seal Op. 1084B30 engine oil filter r r

4

OIL PRESSURE RELIEF VALVE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the oil pump pressure relief valve

Continue to Step 5

Valve stuck in closed position

Replace the oil pump Op. 1084B16 engine oil pump - r+r vehicles with a/c

5

ENGINE GASKET CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Gain access to the crankshaft and camshaft oil seals

Check their seal

Continue to Step 6

Crankshaft seals damaged

Replace the seals concerned (see ENGINE OVERHAUL, Subassembly 1024)

Camshaft seals damaged

Replace the seals concerned (see ENGINE OVERHAUL Subassembly 1036)

6

CORRECT OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'

Z51 RICH FUEL SYSTEM

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

-

Results of the tests

1

CANISTER VALVE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the canister valve is not stuck open

Continue to Step 2

Canister valve stuck open

Repair or, if necessary, replace the canister Op. 1080E20 active carbon filter for fuel vapours - r + r

Continue to Step 5

2

INTAKE AND EXHAUST SYSTEM LEAKAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the blow-by circuit, visually checking that there are no leaks from the engine sump to the intake circuit.

Also check, visually and by listening, that there are no leaks from the intake and exhaust systems

Continue to Step 3

Leaks are present

Eliminate leaks from the system. Replace faulty parts if necessary

Continue to Step 5

3

PETROL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Turn the key to MAR and check that fuel pump activates.

While the pump is active, check fuel pressure is not too high. Turn the ignition off and check that the pressure does not fall by more than 1 bar over a period of 15 minutes.

Continue to Step 4

High pressure: fuel pressure regulator setting not correct

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

The pressure falls by more than 1 bar in the established time: faulty injectors

Renew the correct seal for the injectors, if necessary replace them Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

Continue to Step 5

The pressure falls by more than 1 bar in the established time: leaks from the fuel pressure regulator

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

The pressure falls by more than 1 bar in the established time: leaks from the non-return valve on the fuel pump

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 5

4

UPSTREAM LAMBDA SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

See table Z56 UPSTREAM LAMBDA SENSOR SIGNAL ERROR starting from Step 3

  • that the terminals are not corroded;

Replace faulty components

Wiring faulty

2

REPLACING UPSTREAM LAMBDA SENSOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the upstream Lambda sensor

Search for the cause of the pollution in the sensor

The probable causes are:

  • contaminated petrol;
  • engine oil leaks;
  • improper use of fastenings at sensor connector.

Continue to Step 3

Use of contaminated petrol

Clean the tank and introduce suitable quality fuel.

Continue to Step 3

Engine oil leaks detected

See table X12 ENGINE OIL LEAKS

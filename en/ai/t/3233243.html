Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The bodyshell marking is stamped in the engine compartment, beside the right shock absorber top mounting. This marking comprises:

  • Vehicle type: ZAR 932.000;
  • Vehicle manufacturing serial number (chassis number).

For some versions/markets, the marking is partially covered by a guard.

To view the whole of the marking, remove the plug by unscrewing it anti-clockwise and lift the guard.

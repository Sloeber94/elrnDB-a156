Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When towing the vehicle, follow the laws in force locally Make sure that the gearbox is in neutral and follow the same instructions as for a normal vehicle with a manual gearbox.

If the gearbox cannot be placed in neutral, the vehicle should not be towed. Seek assistance from an Alfa Romeo Authorized Service Centre.

Do not start the engine whilst the vehicle is being towed.

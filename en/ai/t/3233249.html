Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When towing the vehicle, follow the laws in force locally Follow the instructions described previously.

If the vehicle is being towed, observe the following recommendations:If possible, transport the vehicle on a recovery truck.If one is not available, tow the vehicle with the drive (front) wheels raised off the groundIf the last solution is also not possible, the vehicle can be towed for less than 50 Km and at a speed not exceeding 50 Km/h.

The vehicle should be towed with the gearbox in N

Do not start the engine whilst the vehicle is being towed.

Not observing these instructions can lead to serious damage to the automatic transmission.

Vehicles with automatic transmission can only be towed for brief distances and at low speeds: if the vehicle has to be towed for a longer distance, this must be done with the drive wheels raised off the ground so that the gearbox is not driven.

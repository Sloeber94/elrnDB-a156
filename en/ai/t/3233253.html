Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The smooth operation and durability of every vehicle is closely linked to the way it is used and especially to the care taken over routine maintenance operations for which new criteria have been developed.

The interval between services is 20,000 km.

It is worth remembering that the vehicle also needs regular attention such as, for example, systematic checks, topping up the fluid levels, when necessary, and checking the tyre inflation pressure, etc.

It should be remembered that careful maintenance is definitely the best way of ensuring the performance of the vehicle and the safety specifications over a period of time, having regard for the environment and guaranteeing low running costs.

The Planned Maintenance services are recommended by the Manufacturer. Failure to carry them out can invalidate the warranty.

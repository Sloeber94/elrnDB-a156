Applies to: all engines; all production periods

For vehicles with annual mileage below 20,000 Km (e.g. about 10,000 km) there is an ANNUAL INSPECTION PLAN that includes the following:

  • Check condition/wear of tyres and, if necessary, adjust inflation pressure (including spare wheel).
  • Check operation of lighting system (headamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, luggage compartment, passenger compartment, glove compartment, instrument panel warning lights, etc.).
  • Check operation of windscreen wash/wipe, rearscreen wash/wipe and jet adjustment.
  • Check positioning/wear of front/rear wipers.
  • Check condition and wear of front disc brake pads.
  • Visually inspect condition of: engine, gearbox, transmission, pipes (exhaust, supply, fuel, brakes), rubber elements (boots, hoses, bushes, etc.), braking and fuel system flexible pipes.
  • Check battery charge.
  • Visually inspect condition of accessory drive belts
  • Check and, if necessary, top up fluid levels (engine coolant, braking system, windscreen washer, battery, etc.).
  • Change engine oil.
  • Replace engine oil filter.
  • Change active charcoal combination particle filter

(*) Tyres to which chains cannot be fitted.

(**) For the 3.2 V6 and 3.2 V6 Selespeed versions, tyres to which chains can be fitted with a reduced load inflation pressure of 2.3 bar (front and rear) and fully laden pressure of 2.6 bar (front and rear0.

When the tyre is warm, the pressure must be +0.3 bar higher than specified. Recheck that the value is correct with the tyres cold. When the tyre is warm, the pressure must be +0.2 bar higher than specified. Do not use air tubes on tubeless tyres.

Not only must the specified sizes be adhered to, but to ensure safe driving it is essential that the vehicle be fitted with tyres of the same make and model on all four wheels.

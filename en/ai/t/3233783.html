Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02

Description

The car is equipped with two dipped beam headlamps.

The dipped beam headlamps are switched on through the steering column switch unit by turning the special ring nut in the position after the side lights.

A special relay - on the right hand side, under the dashboard - keeps the dipped headlamps on with the main beam headlamps on.

The circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the right hand headlamp, one for the left hand headlamp.

The dipped beam circuit also enables the headlamp alignment device and rear fog lamps for operation.

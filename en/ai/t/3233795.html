Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02

Description

The car is equipped with two main beam headlamps.

The main beam headlamps are switched on:

  • when the dipped beam headlamps are on, by pressing the lever towards the dashboard (stable mode)
  • in "flash" mode, by pulling the lever towards the steering wheel (unstable mode)

The circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the right hand headlamp, one for the left hand headlamp.

A warning light in the panel (rev counter module) indicates that the main beam headlamps have been switched on.

The main beam headlamps circuit also provides the signal for keeping the dipped headlamps on when the main beam headlamps are on.

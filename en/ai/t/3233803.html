Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Description

The car is equipped with two main beam headlamps.

The main beam headlamps are switched on:

  • when the dipped beam headlamps are on, by pressing the lever towards the dashboard (stable mode)
  • in 'flash' mode, by pulling the lever towards the steering wheel (unstable mode)

The circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the right hand headlamp, one for the left hand headlamp.

A warning light in the panel (rev counter module) signals that the main beam headlamps have been switched on.

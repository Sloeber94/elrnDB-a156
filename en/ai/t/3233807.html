Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01

Description

The direction indicators and hazard warning lights delimit the outer dimensions of the car.

The left and right direction indicators are operated by raising or lowering the appropriate lever on the stalk unit.

The hazard warning lights (right and left direction indicators operated simultaneously) are turned on by the pressing the red button in the centre of the dashboard.

The direction indicators work with the ignition on, while for safety reasons, the hazard warning lights can be turned on at any time, as they are supplied directly from the battery.

The circuit for the direction indicators and hazard warning lights is protected by a single fuse.

Two warning lights in the panel (rev counter module), also intermittent, signal that the left and right direction indicators are turned on.

The red hazard warning light button contains two leds: the first green one is ignition-operated and lights up the specific symbol; The second red light comes on intermittently when the hazard warning lights are operated.

The hazard warning lights are also operated by the alarm system, which is connected directly to the power supply to the lights.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Description

The direction indicators and hazard warning lights delimit the outer dimensions of the car.

The left and right direction indicators are operated by raising or lowering the appropriate lever on the stalk unit.

The hazard warning lights (right and left direction indicators operated simultaneously) are turned on by the pressing the red button at the centre of the dashboard included in the climate control system controls.

The direction indicators work with the ignition on, while for safety reasons, the hazard warning lights can be turned on at any time, as they are supplied directly from the battery.

The circuit for the direction indicators and hazard warning lights is protected by a single fuse.

Two warning lights on the instrument panel (rev counter module), which also flash, show when the right or left direction indicator is on.

There is a red LED in the red hazard warning lights button that comes on intermittently when the hazard warning lights are switched on.

The hazard warning lights are also operated by the door opening remote control, which controls a special relay.

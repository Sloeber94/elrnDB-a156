Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Sedan

Description

The car is equipped with two reversing lights situated iin the centre of the tail-light unit (on the boot).

These are operated whenever reverse is engaged via a switch on the outside of the gear housing.

The reversing light circuit is supplied with the ignition key turned to MAR - fully independently of the other lights - by a line protected by a fuse in the junction unit.

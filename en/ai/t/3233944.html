Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Description

The lighting of the luggage compartment is timed according to a logic managed by the integrated services control unit, on the right hand side, under the dashboard:

When the boot is opened, a light is turned on for a timed interval of 20 minutes (this function ensures that the battery does not go flat if the car is left with the boot open).

Timer operation is suspended when the boot is closed.

A light inside the glove compartment is turned on - only with the ignition turned to MAR - when the compartment is opened, and goes off when the compartment is closed.

The light under the sunblind is turned on - only with the ignition turned to MAR (on) - when a switch on the blind is turned on.

Two fuses protect the integrated service control unit direct and ignition-operated supply lines.

The circuit for the glove compartment light is protected by the fuse for the supplies controlled by the ignition, at the side of the junction unit.

Numerous other interior instrument and control lights are present. These are turned on when the side lights are turned on. The lines are described and illustrated in the relevant component wiring diagrams.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Sedan

Description

The lighting of the luggage compartment (with two courtesy lights, one at the side and one in the top section) is timed according to a logic managed by the integrated services control unit in exactly the same way as described for the passenger compartment courtesy lights E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS .

A light inside the glove compartment is turned on - only with the ignition turned to MAR - when the compartment is opened, and goes off when the compartment is closed.

The two lights under the sun visor are switched on (only with the ignition turned on) by operating the switch.

The circuit for the boot light and the sun visor lights is protected by the same fuse as the courtesy lights, two fuses also protect the direct and ignition-controlled supply lines for the integrated services control unit.

The circuit for the glove compartment light is protected by the fuse for the supplies controlled by the ignition, at the side of the junction unit.

Numerous other interior instrument and control lights are present. These are turned on when the side lights are turned on. The lines are described and illustrated in the relevant component wiring diagrams.

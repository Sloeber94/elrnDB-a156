Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Description

All the instruments (dials and gauges) are lit up with the ignition switched ON.

In the 'night' conditions (side lights on), the brightness can be adjusted to 3 levels by an external button.

On the sport version with white dials, these are not illuminated when the ignition is switched on, only when the side lights are switched on.

This lighting logic is managed by a speedometer module, which operates the illumination of the rev counter module and the centre instrument.

The check control instrument is lit up directly by the electronic device inside.

The adjustment button is on the left of the actual instruments, together with the button for zeroing the milometer and the headlamp alignment adjuster.

The lighting circuit for all the instruments is protected by a special fuse, located at the side of the junction unit.

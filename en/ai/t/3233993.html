Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Description

The headlamp washer device is responsible for washing the front windscreen and the headlamp lens. The headlamp washer function is operated automatically, with the side lights on, when the windscreen washer is operated by a stalk lever to the right of the steering wheel.

The headlamp washer device consists of a detergent delivery pump and a nozzle in the bumpers that sprays a powerful jet onto the headlamp.

The headlamp washer circuit is protected by a specific fuse and regulated by an electronic timer device, located on the right under the dashboard.

The timer operates the headlamp washer pump in a series of half-second pulses.

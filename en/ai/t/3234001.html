Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

The rear window heater element, door mirror demister coils and the windscreen heater element absorb a considerable amount of energy. They must therefore be turned off once they have been working for a certain period of time.

The demisting of the heated rear windscreen and the rear view mirrors takes place with a timer, according to the following logic.

The system is turned on with:

  • the ignition in the ON position
  • engagement signal coming from the special button, in the air conditioning/heater control panel (or from the "rapid demisting" function of the actual air conditioner).

The resistances remain supplied for 20 minutes, then the power supply is cut off.

The power supply is also cut off under the following conditions:

  • ignition key in the OFF position;
  • disengagement signal from the button (or from the "rapid demisting" function);
  • after the initial 10 minutes of operation, if the battery voltage goes below 11.6 V (if the voltage goes back up above 13 V, then the supply is restored).

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Air Conditioner

Description

The power supply is also cut off under the following conditions:

  • The control button always has priority over the "rapid demisting" function: for example, if the heated rear windscreen is switched on automatically by the "rapid demisting" function and the button is pressed, the heated rear windscreen is deactivated.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Heater

Description

The power supply is also cut off under the following conditions:

  • The LED in the button stays on whilst the heated rear windscreen is activated.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

This control logic is managed by the integrated services control unit, located on the left under the dashboard. (the climate control/heater control unit only "houses" the control button and the LED, but does not affect the activation logic in any way).

The defrosting circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a specific relay located in the junction box.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Sedan

Description

A special relay controls the operation of the tailgate lock release actuator, which is operated by a remote control unit in the key (via a special button).

The boot release circuit is protected by a dedicated shunt fuse.

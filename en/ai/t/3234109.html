Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

The rear door electrically-operated windows can be operated by switches on the front driver's door or switches located on each of the rear doors.

Operation of the rear electrically-operated windows is manual (the window is raised/lowered only when the switch is pressed).

For safety reasons, a special lock switch on the front driver's door cuts off power to all switches to prevent accidental activation from the rear seats, e.g. when children are present.

The rear electric windows circuit is protected by two specific fuses and controlled by a relay which supplies other ignition-key operated services. The fuse is located inside the junction unit, and the relay is located on the right hand side of the facia.

The rear electrically-operated windows will work only when the key is turned to MAR.

The control switch symbols are lit when the key is turned to MAR.

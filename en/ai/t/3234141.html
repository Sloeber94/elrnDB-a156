Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Description

There are three ashtrays in the car: one is in the centre of the dashboard, and two are in the rear doors.

The front ashtray is lit up when the side lights are on (). it houses a standard power point; the ring round the power point is lit with the side lights on. The cigar lighter resistance is located in the socket, but it can be used for connection to other instruments or equipment (provided they operate at 12 V). To operate the cigar lighter, simply press the knob: after a few seconds it pops out automatically, ready for use.

The socket has a heat protection device: if devices with high energy absorption are connected, this protection cuts off the supply.

The current socket circuit is protected by a special fuse and is operated by the relay which is excluded when the ignition is switched on and is located in the junction unit.

One more power point is also present in the luggage compartament, with similar specifications to those of the cigar lighter; The power point circuits are protected by a specific fuse.

All power points are supplied only with the ignition turned on.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Sedan

Description

There are three ashtrays in the car: one is in the centre of the dashboard, and two are in the rear doors.

The front ashtray houses a standard type current socket; the ring nut for this socket is lit up when the side lights are switched on. The cigar lighter is located in the socket, but it can be used for connection to other instruments or equipment (provided they operate at 12 V). To operate the cigar lighter, simply press the knob: after a few seconds it pops out automatically, ready for use.

The socket has a heat protection device: if devices with high energy absorption are connected, this protection cuts off the supply.

The current socket circuit is protected by a special fuse and is operated by the relay which is excluded when the ignition is switched on and is located in the junction unit.

The sockets are only supplied when the ignition is switched ON.

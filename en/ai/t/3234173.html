Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Air Bag laterale

Description

On the version with RECARO seats, where Side Bags are fitted, the mechanical device for adjusting the lumbar support cannot be housed This adjustment is therefore carried out by a special electric motor operated by a switch on the outside of the actual seat.

The circuit is protected by a fuse controlled by the seat heater relay.

The lumbar adjustment can only be made with the ignition switched ON.

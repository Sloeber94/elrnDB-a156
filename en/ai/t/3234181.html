Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Description

On some versions the lumbar adjustment of the driver's seat backrest is carried out by a special electric motor and is controlled by a switch on the outside of the actual seat.

The circuit is protected by a fuse controlled by the seat heater relay.

The seat adjustment can only be made with the ignition switched ON.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02

Description

A device allows the headlamp beam to be adjusted, directly from the driver's seat, in accordance with the load. The headlamps are thus always aligned quickly and correctly.

The adjustment device consists of a motor mounted on each of the two headlamps which tilts them as appropriate, lowering the light beam if the car is heavily laden, and raising it if the load decreases.

A specific knob on the dashboard selects the four positions indicated:

  • position 0 - one or two persons on the front seats, full fuel tank, car fully equipped and in running order;
  • position 1 - five persons;
  • position 2 - five persons with luggage compartment full (about 50 kg);
  • position 3 - driver and 300 kg load, all stowed in boot.

The adjustment button is on the left of the actual instruments, together with the button for zeroing the mileometer and the instrument lighting dimmer switch; The control switches have symbols which are lit up when the side lights are on.

The device is enabled only when the dipped beam headlamps are on.

The headlamp adjustment circuit is protected by the same fuses as the dipped beam headlamps circuit, located in the junction unit.

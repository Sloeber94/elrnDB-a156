Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two GAS DISCHARGE headlamps.

For more details 5540 EXTERIOR LIGHTING .

The left headlamp is the Master control unit while the right acts as a Slave unit.

The master downloads the dipped beam command, car speed and - to manage the function - signals from two sensors (potentiometric) on the wheel axles that manage front and rear ride.

The control unit controls the adjustment motor - located in the headlamps - and tilts it by the required amount on the basis of input.

on the version with gas discharge headlamps, the light beam is adjujsted automatically and the two buttons on the left side of the panel are no longer active.

The device is enabled only when the dipped beam headlamps are on.

The electronics for the two headlamps are supplied by a fuse that also protects other power supplies controlled by the ignition.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: BOSE System and Navigator

Description

The radio system includes:

  • radio supply cables;
  • separate front power and tweeter speakers, located in the doors;
  • rear speakers, located in the doors;
  • leads connecting the radio to the speakers;
  • aerial built into the heated rear windscreen by means of a suitable electronic device and connected to the radio by means of a coaxial cable.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key turned to the ON position, or directly from the battery (which allows tuning settings, security codes, etc. to be stored).

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

In addition to the radio there is also a supply with the side lights on to light up the radio controls.

The following is also fitted, according to the trim level:

  • RADIO with CC or CD.E3510 RADIO
  • Standard CONNECT deviceE4091 CONNECT
  • Radio with NAVIGATORE4090 NAVIGATOR
  • CONNECT with NAVIGATORE4090 NAVIGATOR

A sophisticated 'Bose' HI FI system with special speakers and an outside amplifier is available on request.

The system has been developed ad hoc for the vehicle: the acoustic properties of the passenger compartment have been carefully designed so that the amplifier and the speakers are perfectly suited to the vehicle: the sound is excellent throughout the passenger compartment.

Unlike the system fitted as standard, the Bose system includes:

  • mid-range speakers in the front doors with tweeters incorporated
  • wide-range speakers in the rear doors
  • rear woofer speaker
  • high power amplifier

For further details see 5570T car radio and car telephone .

On the version with a NAVIGATOR, an interface connecting the radio navigator and the BOSE amplifier is required.

The other components (aerial, steering wheel radio controls, connection with CD player, etc.) are the same as those for the standard system E3510 RADIO .

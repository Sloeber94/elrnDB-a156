Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: CONNECT

Description

The radio system includes:

  • radio supply cables;
  • separate front power and tweeter speakers, located in the doors;
  • rear speakers, located in the doors;
  • leads connecting the radio to the speakers;
  • aerial built into the heated rear windscreen by means of a suitable electronic device and connected to the radio by means of a coaxial cable.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key turned to the ON position, or directly from the battery (which allows tuning settings, security codes, etc. to be stored).

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

In addition to the radio there is also a supply with the side lights on to light up the radio controls.

The following is also fitted, according to the trim level:

  • RADIO with CC or CD.
  • Standard CONNECT device (seeE4091 CONNECT)
  • Radio with NAVIGATOR (seeE4090 NAVIGATOR)
  • CONNECT device with NAVIGATOR (seeE4090 NAVIGATORandE4091 CONNECT)

Full radio specifications and operating instructions are given in the OWNER'S HANDBOOK.

The main radio controls are 'duplicated' on the steering wheel, allowing the driver to operate the radio without taking their hands off the steering wheel.

On versions with a Connect, an impedance adaptor is required for connecting the radio with the controls.

A multi-pole cable connecting the radio to the CD player, located in the boot, may be fitted on request.

The radio is equipped with an anti-theft system with a personalized security code which should be entered again if there is a break in the supply (e.g. if the battery is disconnected).

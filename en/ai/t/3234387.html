Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

During starting (ignition switched ON), the operation of all the instruments and warning lights is tested: all the warning lights and the LEDs are switched on for around 4 seconds to check their operation. In the case of the milometer (LCD), all the LCD segments are lit up for about 4 seconds to check that they are working.

This test function for the warning lights is managed by the electronic circuits inside the speedometer module which controls the test for the speedometer circuits and sends signals to the rev counter and to the auxiliary instrument and the check module.

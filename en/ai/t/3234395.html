Description

The CONNECT system is available on request: it is a Bosch RADIOPHONE radio personalized especially for ALFA ROMEO and includes the CONNECT device.

For more details 5570 ACCESSORIES .

This system includes:

  • GSM module and SIM-CARD reader integrated in the radio-telephone-navigator;
  • hands-free function via the radio speakers;
  • microphone located in the front courtesy light;
  • outside aerial.

Full details of the specifications and the operation of the RADIOPHONE are given in the 'OWNER'S HANDBOOK'.

The commands of the telephone function are grouped together on a conventional telephone keyboard (12 buttons), with in addition dedicated keys for emergency call.

On versions with steering wheel radio controls the commands for the telephone function can also be sent.

The antenna is mounted at the rear of the roof: it has two functions (GSM/GPS) as it also incorporates the GSM aerial if the navigation system is fitted. E4090 NAVIGATOR

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Heater

Description

The engine radiator cooling system consists of a fan activated at only one speed corresponding to a high engine coolant temperature.

The fan is operated by means of a specific relay located in the engine compartment near the battery.

The supply line for the fan is protected by a special maxifuse, whilst the circuit for energizing the relay is protected by the fuse which protects numerous other services controlled by the ignition. The fan therefore only comes on when the ignition is switched ON.

Description

The climate control system with heater unit is controlled by means of three knobs in the control panel in the centre of the dashboard:

  • the left knob mechanically controls the mixture flap between hot air (red) and cold air (blue);
  • the right knob, mechanically operated via a cable, controls the opening of the flaps which regulate the distribution of air flows;
  • the centre knob electrically controls the ventilation of the air at four different speeds: 1, 2, 3 and 4. In position 0, the fan is turned off but air still enters due to the dynamic effect of the car's motion.;
  • when pressed, the left button activates the air recirculation function by an electrical command; when the button is pressed, the relevant LED comes on. If the button is released, "dynamic" air flows in from the outside
  • the right button switches on the heated rear window; when the button is pressed, the relevant LED comes on.

( E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING ).

The control switches have ideograms which are lit up when the side lights are on.

For more details, 5020 HEATER

The system is controlled by means of a special relay located in the distributor duct unit.

The fan supply lines are protected by a special maxifuse and by the fuse which protects other supplies controlled by the ignition. The fan only operates when the ignition is switched ON.

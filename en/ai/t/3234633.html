Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

An automatic system manages passenger compartment climate control, i.e. temperature and ventilation with recirculation and air flow orientation.

A dedicated control unit manages system operation by controlling:

  • air temperature at outlets;
  • fan speed;
  • compressor activation (air cooling circuit);
  • air recirculation activation;
  • "rapid demisting" function;

The control unit sets the above parameters in order to adjust passenger compartment temperature to the required level. The system controls are located outside the control unit housing.

The control unit supplies information on interior and exterior temperature via special sensors.

After processing the information, it adjusts passenger compartment air intake speed by means of the fan motor and air temperature by means of a mixer actuator. It also controls interior air recirculation via a dedicated actuator.

When required, the ECU automatically activates the cooling circuit and dehumidifies the air by operating the air conditioner compressor. ( E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT ).

For more details, 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS .

The control unit supply lines are protected by a fuse in the junction unit and by the fuse which protects other supplies controlled by the ignition.

The fan supply lines are protected by a special maxifuse.

The system will only operate when the ignition is turned ON.

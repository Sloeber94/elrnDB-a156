Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00

Description

When required, the climate control system ECU automatically activates the cooling circuit and dehumidifies the air by operating the air conditioner compressor.

The left-hand key on the climate control panel enables the operation of the air cooling circuit; when the button is pressed, the relevant LED comes on.

The operation of the air cooling circuit is also enabled automatically (without pressing the button) when the ignition key is turned to the ON position if the temperature of the air at the vents is below the temperature of the outside air.

This key enables the operation of the air cooling circuit; but the actual switching on of the compressor is excluded at low external temperatures.

For more details 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS .

The compressor can also be deactivated by the 4-stage pressure switch or the engine management unit according to the engine management software, which is different for each engine version ( 1056 PETROL INJECTION SYSTEM ).

The compressor supply circuit is controlled by a specific relay, located in the engine bay close to the battery.

The relay contact line is protected by a special fuse and by the exclusion relay controlled by the ignition, located in the junction unit.

The energizing line is protected by the fuse which protects other supplies controlled by the ignition.

0010K80 80,000km service coupon - emission control versions

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Check that the tyre treads are not unevenly worn.

Some types of tyres are fitted with wear indicators; they have to be replaced as soon as these indicators become visible.

Do not cross-interchange the tyres.

Check that the tyre pressures are correct, including the spare tyre.

The tyre pressures should be checked on cold tyres.

Check that all the lights are working: headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, boot light, courtesy light, glove compartment light and instrument panel warning lights.

Check that the windscreen wash-wipe system is working correctly and if necessary adjust the jets.

Check that the windscreen and rear window wiper blades are correctly positioned and not worn.

Check operation of front disc brake pad wear indicator.

Check the condition and wear of the rear brake pads.

Visually check:

  • bodywork exterior
  • underbody protection
  • flexible and rigid sections of the pipes (exhaust - fuel system - brakes)
  • rubber components (dust excluders, hoses, bushes, etc.).

Check the adjustment of the handbrake lever travel 3380A04 handbrake - check travel .

Check exhaust emissions/fumes 1080A10 idling c.o. level - check with special equipment .

Replace the air filter cartridge 1048A14 air cleaner filtering element - replace. .

Visually check the evaporation control system (pipes and connectors).

Check and if necessary top up the brake-clutch fluid level Refer to op.. 0010T64 brake and or clutch fluid check level and top up as necessary .

Check and, if necessary, top up the power assisted steering fluid level 0010T71 power steering fluid check level and top up if necessary .

Check and if necessary top up the windscreen washer fluid level.

Check and if necessary top up the battery fluid level.

Check and if necessary top up the coolant level 0010T20 coolant change .

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Check and if necessary top up the Selespeed gearbox fluid level Refer to op.. 0010T47 hydraulic system fluid for gearbox with hydraulic selection check level and top up if necessary .

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Connect the Examiner or other diagnostic instruments to the diagnostic socket (select the relevant control unit).

Check the plausibility of all the parameters and if necessary carry out the available active diagnoses.

Check the manual gearbox oil level Refer to op.. 0010T41 MANUAL GEARBOX OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary .

Replace the engine oil and filter 0010T12 engine oil and filter replace .

Change the active charcoal combination particle filter 5010B09 purifying filter (antipollen) filter element - replace .

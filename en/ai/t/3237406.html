Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Engine oil is harmful to the skin: minimize contact of the oil with the skin; in case of contact wash with soap and water.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Remove the filler cap with the engine hot.

Withdraw the oil level dipstick.

Raise the car.

Undo the drain plug on the engine sump and drain the oil fully into a suitable container.

Be very careful whilst removing the drain plug; the oil could be very hot.

Do not disperse the oil into the environment, as indiscriminate dispersal of this product causes pollution.

Working under the vehicle, use the specific wrench to release the oil filter and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Clean the drain plug and screw it on, with its seal, to the specified torque.

Check that the engine oil level on the dipstick is correct.

The oil level should be checked with the vehicle on flat ground. If the oil level is higher than the Max mark, it could cause excessive evaporation of the oil and a loss of pressure.

Refit the filler cap, run the engine at idle speed for about 2 minutes, turn off the engine and wait a few minutes.

Check the oil level and that there are no leaks.

Remove the car from the ramps.

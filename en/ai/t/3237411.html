0010T40 hydraulic system fluid for gearbox with hydraulic selection check level and top up if necessary

.

The Selespeed system fluid level should be checked with the vehicle on a flat surface and the engine switched off and cold.

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

1. Detach the breather pipe (1a), remove the cap (1b) and check that the level is at the MAX reference on the dipstick on the actual cap.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

If the level is below the MAX mark, add fluid until the level is correct.

Retighten the cap and fully insert the breather pipe into the cap beak.

Turn the ignition to the OFF position.

Do not add fluid of different specifications from the fluid already in the gearbox.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

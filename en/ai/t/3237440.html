IF TOOL 1.871.003.100 IS AVAILABLE, REFIT THE CLUTCH UNIT AS DESCRIBED BELOW.

1. Fit the tool's centring device in the crankshaft.

Refit the clutch disc in its seating.

When fitting the clutch disc, make sure it is orientated as indicated by the words 'engine side' or 'gearbox side'.

Handle the clutch disc carefully. Do not touch the friction surfaces with the hands.

2. Refit the clutch cover in its seating.

Clean the protective anti-corrosion products or detergents from the friction surfaces of the clutch cover.

3. Fit the remaining part of the tool (3a) for fitting the clutch unit, by tightening the bolts (3b).

4. Turn the handwheel of the tool until the pins (4a) come into contact with the disc (4b).

5. Manually tighten the thre free bolts for securing the clutch unit.

6. Unscrew the handwheel of the tool to discharge the clutch mechanism's diaphragm spring.

Remove the tool.

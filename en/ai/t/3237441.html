1. Refit the intermediate drive shaft (1a) in its seating and tighten its bolts (1b) to the specified torque.

Make sure that the half shaft is clean before assembly, to avoid the entry of foreign bodies into the differential.

Take particular care not to damage the differential oil seal with the splined part of the half shaft. Insert the half shaft in a perfectly horizontal manner.

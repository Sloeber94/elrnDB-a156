1024B40 flywheel crown wheel - replacement for versions with manual gearbox

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Dismantling

Reassembling

Remove the ring gear from the engine flywheel using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Reassembling

Dismantling

Thoroughly clean and degrease the contact surfaces of the new ring gear and the flywheel.

Heat up the new ring gear evenly in an oven at a temperature of 80° C.

Fit the ring gear on the flywheel.

The bevel on the inner diameter of the ring gear must face the flywheel.

Leave to cool to ambient temperature without cooling artificially.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Dismantling

Reassembling

Remove engine flywheel crown wheel by using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Reassembling

Dismantling

Carefully clean and degrease contact surfaces of new crown wheel and flywheel.

Evenly heat new crown wheel in a furnace to a temperature of 120° - 140° C.

Fit crown wheel on flywheel.

Let it get cold at room temperature without forcedly cooling it.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Remove the ring gear from the engine flywheel using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean and degrease the contact surfaces of the new ring gear and the flywheel.

Heat the new ring gear evenly in an oven to a temperature of between 120° and 140° C.

Position the ring gear on the flywheel.

Leave to cool to ambient temperature without cooling forcibly.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

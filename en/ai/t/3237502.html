1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the right cylinder head camshaft caps (1b).

2. Remove the left cylinder head camshafts complete with oil seals.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the right cylinder head camshafts complete with new oil seals.

Fit the camshaft caps and fix them tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Check that the camshaft oil seals are positioned correctly in their seatings.

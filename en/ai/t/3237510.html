Removing

Removing

Refitting

Withdraw and remove, from its seating in the cylinder head, the hydraulic tappet to be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Lubricate with engine oil and fit a new hydraulic tappet in the seating in the cylinder head.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

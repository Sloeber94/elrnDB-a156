Refitting

Removing

Refit the fuel tank complete wtih multifunction valves and pipes.

Take care to position the pipes between body and fuel tank correctly.

Connect the fuel vapour separator pipe joints.

Connect the fuel filler pipe to the tank.

Fit the protection for the fuel filler pipe and fix it using the nuts and bolts.

Connect the evaporation control pipe joint.

Fit the fuel tank support belts and fix them using the bolts.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the fuel tank.

Fit the exhaust pipe heat shield.

1076B22 rear exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

Removing

.

The fuel tank is supplied by the Parts Dept. together with the float valves (which cannot be dismantled) and the immersed pump assembly complete with gauge. Therefore if the tank has to be replaced it is not possible to reuse the immsered pump assembly complete with gauge removed, but a new pre-assembled unit consisting of a tank - pump - float valves supplied by the Parts Dept. must be fitted.

Place the fuel tank back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

Take care to position the pipes between body and fuel tank correctly.

Connect the rapid attachment for the fuel vapour recovery pipe from the multipurpose valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Connect the fuel filler pipe from the tank.

Connect the rapid attachments for the fuel vapour recovery pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

Place the fuel fillter pipe protection back in its housing, secure it using the bands, then tighten the nuts and bolts.

Place the fuel tank support belts back in their housings and secure them using the nuts.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the fuel tank.

Place the rear suspension crossmember back in its housing using the hydraulic jack and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.

Removing

1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fuel .

3380A04 handbrake - check travel .

1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r .

7050B18 rear seat arm rest - r+r .

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Connector

Proceed with removal 7040B10 load platform (luggage platform) trim - r+r .

Proceed with removal 7050B18 rear seat arm rest - r+r .

Proceed with removal 7050A20 one-piece rear seat cushion - r r .

Proceed with removal 7050B10 l/h or r/h head restraint (one) of rear seat - r+r .

Proceed with removal 7050A19 rear seat squab - r r .

Proceed with removal 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r .

The fuel pump cannot be used when refitting because the fuel tank is supplied by the Parts Dept. together with the immersed pump assembly complete with gauge and float valves.

Drain the fuel from the tank using suitable equipment.

Remove the handbrake bush.

1. Lift up the handbrake lever (1a), loosen the adjustment nut (1b), then lower the lever.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

1. Disconnect the handbrake cables (1a) from the mounting bracket (1b) and from the fastenings on the fuel tank (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Proceed with removal 1076B22 rear exhaust silencer - r+r emission control versions .

Remove the exhaust pipe heat shield.

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the rear suspension crossmember.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and lower the rear suspension crossmember (2b), as necessary, using the hydraulic jack.

3. Position a hydraulic jack under the fuel tank.

4. Unscrew nuts (4a) and removethe fuel tank mount belts (4b).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and the bolts (1b) and remove the protection (1c) for the fuel filler pipe after having released it from the bands.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

1. Disconnect the rapid attachments for the fuel vapour recovery pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

1. Disconnect the fuel filler pipe from the tank.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel vapour recovery pipe from the multi-purpose valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Lower the hydraulic jack and remove the complete fuel tank.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Refitting

Removing

Place a new fuel tank assembly back in the housing using a hydraulic jack.

Take care to position the pipes between body and fuel tank correctly.

Connect the rapid attachment for the fuel vapour recovery pipe from the multipurpose valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Connect the fuel filler pipe from the tank.

Connect the rapid attachments for the fuel vapour recovery pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

Place the fuel fillter pipe protection back in its housing, secure it using the bands, then tighten the nuts and bolts.

Place the fuel tank support belts back in their housings and secure them using the nuts.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the fuel tank.

Place the rear suspension crossmember back in its housing using the hydraulic jack and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.

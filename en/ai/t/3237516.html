Refitting

Removing

Fit the fuel filler pipe cap and tighten it until the closing device can be heard.

Refitting

Removing

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the access flap to the fuel filler pipe cap.

2. Using the ignition key, turn it in an anti-clockwise direction and undo the fuel filler cap.

3. Remove the cap (3a) releasing it from the retaining device (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fasten the cap to the retaining device.

Tighten the cap in a clockwise direction until it clicks once or more.

Turn the ignition key in a clockwise direction and extract it.

Clean the fuel filler cap access flap.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

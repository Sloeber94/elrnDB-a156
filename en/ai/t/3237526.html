Refitting

Removing

Before fitting a new filter element, remove any impurities in the filter casing (by suction) that could fall into the clean air section of the circuit. Take care to position the filter element correctly so that the seal is in its housing and is not cut or damaged.

Fit a new element in the air filter.

Fit the air filter cover complete with air flow meter and fix it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter.

Connect the air supply hose to the chamber to the air flow meter.

- 1072B42 left hand or right hand inlet manifold (one) - r+r

Before any interventions on components of the fuel system, discharge the pressure in the system using tool N. 1.870.684.000 connected to the specific valve located on the fuel distribution manifold.

1. Disconnect the fuel inlet hose, then plug to prevent fuel escaping.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections from the injectors.

Discharge the fuel supply pressure ( 10 ENGINE - WARNINGS).

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel supply pipe from the complete fuel pipe.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections from the injectors.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the fuel pipe (3b) complete with injectors (3c).

Precautions to be taken for the rapid connector between the pipe connecting the right and left fuel manifolds.Avoid working with the engine hot under all circumstances;Never use tools such as pliers, screwdrivers, etc. under any circumstances;If it is released, rotate the rapid connector cover before acting on the release buttons;Check its condition (deformation/breakage of retaining lugs, release buttons) before reconnecting it;After re-engaging the rapid connector, check that it is correctly attached by pulling it;At the end of fitting, check that there are no fuel leaks by starting up the engine again;If there is damage to the rapid connector, replace the complete right pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Refitting

Removing

Place the fuel pipe, complete with injectors, back in its housing and fix it using the bolts.

Connect the electrical connections to the injectors.

Connect the rapid connector for the fuel supply pipe to the complete fuel pipe.

Proceed with refitting - 1072B42 left hand or right hand inlet manifold (one) - r+r .

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

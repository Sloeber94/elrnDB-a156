Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the retainers (1a) and remove the fuel injectors (1b) from the fuel manifold pipe.

2. Remove the O-rings from the fuel injectors.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit new O-rings on the fuel injectors.

Fit the fuel injectors on the fuel manifold pipe and fix them using the safety clips.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the press fit clip on the injectors and separate them from the single fuel manifold pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the injector in the housing on the single fuel manifold pipe, fit the press fit clip and repeat the operation for the other injectors.

Removing

Refitting

Place the vehicle on a ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the stop OFF, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

Proceed with the removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with the removal 1080B92 left upper lambda sensor r r .

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and the nuts (1b) and remove the heat shield for the left catalytic preconverter (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Refitting

Removing

Place the left catalytic preconverter heat shield back in its housing and secure it using the nuts and the bolt.

Proceed with the refitting 1080B92 left upper lambda sensor r r .

Proceed with the refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Connect the battery terminal (-).

Remove the car from the ramp.

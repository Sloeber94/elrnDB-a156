Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Let the engine reach operating temperature.

Insert the sensor of a suitable exhaust gas analyzer at least 30 cm into the end of the exhaust pipe.

If the shape of the end section of the exhaust pipe is such that the sensor cannot be fully introduced, a special extension pipe which guarantees the seal in the join area must be added.

With the engine idling, check that the CO content is within the recommended values.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Carry out the measurement will all the emission control devices activated and at the recommended idle speed.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Insert the sensor of a suitable exhaust gas analyzer at least 30 cm into the end of the exhaust pipe.

If the shape of the end section of the exhaust pipe is such that the sensor cannot be fully introduced, a special extension pipe which guarantees the seal in the join area must be added.

Wait until the exhaust gas analyzer pointers settle down.

With the engine idling, check that the CO content is within the specified values (see TECHNICAL DATA).

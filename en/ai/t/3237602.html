1080E59 Fuel vapour PIPE from INTERMEDIATE pipe to ACTIVE CHARCOAL FILTER - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the stop OFF, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

Proceed with the removal 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the removal 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove protection (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Disconnect the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate pipe to activated carbon filter, intermediate pipe side.

2. Release the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate pipe to the activated carbon filter from the band.

3. Undo bolts (3a) and move the windscreen washer reservoir (3b) to one side.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo nuts (1a) securing the activated carbon filter bracket (1b) and lower as necessary.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Disconnect the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate filter to activated carbon filter, activated carbon filter side, and remove.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate pipe to activated carbon filter and connect to the activated carbon filter.

Place the activated carbon filter mounting bracket in its housing and secure it using the nuts.

Place the washer fluid reservoir back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Secure the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate pipe to activated carbon filter to the band.

Connect the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate pipe to activated carbon filter, intermediate pipe side.

Place the protection back in its housing and secure it using the bolt.

Proceed with the refitting 7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the refitting 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R. (Right side).

Proceed with the refitting 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R. (Right side).

Connect the battery terminal (-).

Remove the car from the ramp.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

Removing

Refitting

Place the car on the lifting bridge.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

1. Unscrew cartridge oil filter support by ad-hoc tool and remove it.

Used engine oil and oil filter replaced contain dangerous substances for environment.

Put oil and filters into ad-hoc vessels envisaged for recovering these substances.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Moisten new filter gasket with oil and thoroughly tighten it by hand.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

Lower the car.

1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Using a suitable tool (1a), undo and remove the engine oil filter cartridge (1b).

Recover the engine oil which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Lubricate the gasket with engine oil and fit the engine oil filter, tightening fully by hand.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Top up the engine oil lubrication system 1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary .

Remove the car from the ramps.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Raise the car.

1. Remove the cartridge engine oil filter using a suitable tool.

Collect the engine oil in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

For versions prior to engine no. 1586000, replace the engine oil heat exchanger connector with a new unit supplied by Parts that is fitted with a flexible tapered washer in addition to the O-ring. Then tighten to the specified torque.

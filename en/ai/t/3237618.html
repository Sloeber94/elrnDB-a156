Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Undo the nut (1a), the bolt (1b) and remove the bracket (1c) supporting the engine oil cooling radiator and duct.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and disconnect the supply and return pipes (1b) from the engine oil cooling radiator.

Recover the engine oil which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the engine oil cooling radiator (1b).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the engine oil cooling radiator back in its housing and fix it using the nuts.

Connect the supply and return pipes to the engine oil cooling radiator and secure them with the relevant bolts.

Place the bracket for the engine oil cooling radiator and duct back in its seating and fix it using the bolt and nut.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system 1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary .

Remove the car from the ramps.

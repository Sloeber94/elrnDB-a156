Refitting

Removing

Place the cooling radiator engine oil supply and return pipes back in their housings.

Connect the engine oil supply and return pipes to the engine cooling radiator, engine oil filter mounting side and fix them using the connectors.

Place the bracket back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Place the bracket supporting the power steering pipes in its seating and secure it with the nut.

Place the cooling radiator engine oil supply and return pipes mounting bracket back in its housing and secure it using the nut.

Refit the front bracket supporting the engine oil/power steering pipes back in its seating and secure it with the nut.

Connect the supply and return pipes to the engine oil cooling radiator and secure them with the bolts.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system 1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary .

Remove the car from the ramps.

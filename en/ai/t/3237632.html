Refitting

1. Press the two rapid attachment springs by hand and disconnect the lower radiator hose.

Recover the engine cooling system coolant in a suitable container.

2. Loosen the band (2a) and disconnect the lower coolant outlet hose from the radiator (2b), thermostat side and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Supply the engine cooling system with the recommended fluid.

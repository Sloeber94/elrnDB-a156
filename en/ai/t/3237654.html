Fit a new cylinder head gasket.

ASTADUR type cylinder head gaskets are fitted.

The material from which these gaskets are made undergoes a polymerization process during the operation of the engine so that they become considerably harder during usage.

In order for the polymerization of the gasket to take place it is necessary to:

keep the gasket in its sealed container until fitting

not lubricate or get oil on the gasket and the contact surfaces.

Refitting

Removing

Fit a new cylinder head gasket on the cylinder block/crankcase, taking care that it is the same size as the one removed.

The cylinder head gasket is of the Astadur type.

These gaskets are made from a special material designed to undergo polymerization during the use of the engine. This means that they harden considerably during use.

In order for the polymerization process to take place it is necessary to:

- keep the gasket in its sealed nylon container until fitting

- not to take out of its container until ready to fit

- not lubricate or get oil on the gasket.

Position the cylinder head on the crankcase.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

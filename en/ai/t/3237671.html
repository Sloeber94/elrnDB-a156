For the front camshaft oil seal, inlet side proceed as follows:

1. Undo the bolts (1a) for the timing driven pulley, inlet side using the tool (1b) for anti-torque.

PROCEED AS FOLLOWS FOR THE INLET SIDE FRONT CAMSHAFT OIL SEAL.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) of the timing driven pulley, inlet side, using the tool (1b) for counter-torque.

Name

Country

1b

Counter-torque

1.860.856.000

2. Remove the timing driven pulley, inlet side.

Remove the front camshaft oil seal, inlet side.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during removal.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

PROCEED AS FOLLOWS FOR THE EXHAUST SIDE FRONT CAMSHAFT OIL SEAL.

1. Loosen the bolt (1a) for the exhaust side timing driven pulley using tool (1b) for counter-torque.

Name

Country

1b

Spanner for camshaft pulley rotation

1.860.831.002

2. Remove the exhaust side driven toothed pulley.

Remove the front camshaft oil seal, exhaust side.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during removal.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

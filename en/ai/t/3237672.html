3380A44 handbrake/linkage protection - r+r

Lift up the handbrake lever, loosen the adjustment nut, then lower the lever.

Raise the car.

1. Disconnect the handbrake cables (1a) from the mounting bracket and from the fixings on the fuel tank.

2. Remove the last section of the exhaust pipe (2a) and the heat shield (2b) underneath.

Position a hydraulic jack under the rear suspension crossmember and undo the bolts fixing it to the bodyshell, lower the jack and the crossmember as necessary and release the fuel tank.

Position a hydraulic jack under the fuel tank, then undo the nuts shown by the arrows and remove the fuel tank support rods.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector connecting the rear section of the fuel supply pipe to the front section.

Lower the fuel tank as necessary to release the pipe from the retaining bands on the actual tank, then remove the pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the rear section of the fuel supply pipe and fix it to the retaining bands on the tank.

Connect the connector securing the rear section of the fuel pipe to the front section.

Raise the hydraulic jack and position the tank, then fix it to the bodyshell using the support rods.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the tank.

Fit the heat shields and the end section of the exhaust pipe.

Reposition the rear suspension crossmember brining it in contact with the bodyshell through the hydraulic jack; fix it to the bodyshell tightening the fixing bolts to torque.

Complete operation 3380A44.

Fit the protective cover for the fuel pump.

Position the cover for the luggage compartment and secure it using the bolts fixing the luggage retaining hook fixing bolts.

Close the boot lid and remove the vehicle from the lift.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

3380A44 handbrake/linkage protection - r+r

Lift up the handbrake lever, loosen the adjustment nut, then lower the lever.

Raise the car.

1. Disconnect the handbrake cables (1a) from the mounting bracket and from the fixings on the fuel tank.

2. Remove the last section of the exhaust pipe (2a) and the heat shield (2b) underneath.

Position a hydraulic jack under the rear suspension crossmember and undo the bolts fixing it to the bodyshell, lower the jack and the crossmember as necessary to release the fuel tank.

Position a hydraulic jack under the fuel tank, then undo the nuts shown by the arrows and remove the fuel tank support rods.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector securing the rear section of the fuel return pipe to the front section.

Lower the fuel tank as necesary to release the fuel return pipe from the retaining bands on the actual tank, then remove the pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the rear section of the fuel return pipe and fix it to the retaining bands on the tank.

Connect the connector securing the rear section of the fuel return pipe to the front section.

Raise the hydraulic jack and position the tank, then fix it to the bodyshell using the support rods.

Remove the hydraulic jack from under the tank.

Fit the heat shield and the end section of the exhaust pipe.

Reposition the rear suspension crossmember bringing it into contact with the bodyshell using the hydraulic jack; fix it to the bodyshell tightening the bolts to torque.

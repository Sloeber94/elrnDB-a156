2. Disconnect the accelerator cable from the throttle body integral with the M.D.S.

3. Disconnect the injection/ignition wiring from the mounting bracket.

4. Undo bolts (4a) and remove the throttle body integral with the M.D.S. (4b) complete with the injection/ignition control unit (4c).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

When refitting, the learning procedure for the throttle body integrated with the M.D.S. must be repeated ( 1056B81 run electronic ignition/injection control unit (single) self-learning procedure ).

Fit the throttle body integrated with the M.D.S. complete with injection/ignition control unit and fix it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

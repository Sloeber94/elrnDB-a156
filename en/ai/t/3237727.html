Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the oil vapour recirculation pipe from the cam cover.

2. Loosen the band and disconnect the vacuum pipe from the air inlet hose to the throttle casing.

3. Loosen the bands (3a) and remove the throttle casing air inlet hose (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the throttle casing air inlet hose back in its housing.

Connect the vacuum pipe to the throttle casing air inlet hose and tighten the band.

Connect the oil vapour recirculation pipe to the tappet cover.

Proceed with refitting 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

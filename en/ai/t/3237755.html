Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 1076B18 rigid section of exhaust pipe between catalytic converter and silencer - r + r .

Proceed with removal 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

Proceed with removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with removal 1072D10 modular inlet manifold - r+r .

1. Undo the bolts securing the fuel vapour pipe from intermediate joint to fuel vapour solenoid to the modular inlet manifold.

2. Release the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate joint to the fuel vapour solenoid from the relevant fastening.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel vapour enabling solenoid valve to the ilet mounting bracket.

4. Loosen the band (4a) and remove the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate joint to fuel vapour solenoid (4b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate coupling to the solenoid valve back in its housing and fasten it using the band.

Tighten the bolts fixing the fuel vapour enabling solenoid valve to the inlet mounting bracket.

Secure the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate coupling to the solenoid valve to the fastening.

Tighten the bolts securing the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate joint to the solenoid to the modular inlet manifold.

Proceed with refitting 1072D10 modular inlet manifold - r+r .

Proceed with refitting 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with refitting 1016A40 ignition coil cover on cylinder head- r+r .

Proceed with refitting 1076B18 rigid section of exhaust pipe between catalytic converter and silencer - r + r .

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Remove the car from the ramps.

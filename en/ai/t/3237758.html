Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the engine oil filler cap.

2. Undo bolts (2a) and remove soundproofing cover (2b).

Place the engine oil filler plug back in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Remove the engine oil filler cap.

Place the soundproof cover back in its housing and secure using the relevant bolts.

Place the engine oil filler plug back in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

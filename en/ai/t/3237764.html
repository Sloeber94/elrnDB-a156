1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the connecting rod cap (1b).

2. Remove the bottom big end bearing shell.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Lower the vehicle.

1. Remove the connecting rod with the piston.

2. Remove the upper connecting rod half-bearing.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Fit the connecting rod - piston assembly and upper bearing shell (1a) using a suitable fitting tool (1b).

The connecting rod - piston assembly is fitted in the cylinder block so that the number stamped on the small end is facing the inlet side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Raise the vehicle.

Fit the connecting rod cap complete with half-bearing, and tighten the bolts to the specified torque.

The connecting rod cap is fitted so that the number on it is facing the same way as the one stamped on small end.

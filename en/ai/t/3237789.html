Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the lift.

Raise the car.

1. Remove the air supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit a new air supply sleeve between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger.

Lower the vehicle.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Loosen the bands (1a) and remove the air supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit in its seating the air supply duct between turbocharger and heat exchanger and tighten the bands.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Remove the car from the ramps.

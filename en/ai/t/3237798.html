1. Undo the nut (1a) and the bolt (1b) fixing the vacuum reservoir bracket (1c) and move it to the side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

Position something suitable under the regulator to collect any components which could drop during the stages which follow.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the pressure regulator to the pump.

Never grip the pressure regulator by the electrical connector.

2. Grip the outer casing of the regulator, partly extract it and, at the same time, turn it so that the fins in which the openings for the fixing bolts are located are positioned horizontally, then insert the blades of two screwdrivers by the fins and very carefully extract the pressure regulator.>

Do not use screwdrivers or other tools in the area between the regulator and the pressure pump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

1. After having removed the regulator, check that the high pressure seal (black or green), the anti-extrusion seal (1b) (white) and the low pressure seal (1c) (black) are present.

If the high pressure seal or any of the other seals have accidentally remained inside the pump casing, turn the ignition key to the ON position (with the engine switched off); the pump shaft will be operated and a small amount of fuel and the seal will come out this way.

Do not, under any circumstances, use any tools to extract the seals as this could damage the inner surface of the pump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Suction off any impurities inside the pump casing.

Before fitting, check the condition of the three seals and make sure they are correctly positioned.

Lubricate the outer surface of the three seals for the new pressure regulator using vaseline.

Do not, under any circumstances, lubricate the other surfaces of the pressure regulator.

Fit the pressure regulator in its housing on the pump, pressing gently and, at the same time, rotating the regulator until it is in contact with the surface of the pump.

Do not, under any circumstances, use a hammer or other tools for fitting the pressure regulator in position.m

Tighten the bolts fixing the pressure regulator to the recommended torque.

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r

Proceed with removal 1060F50 fuel return manifold - r r

Clean the high pressure pump using cold cleaning products and dry with compressed air.

The cold cleaning products should not penetrate inside the electrical connection.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the fuel pressure regulator.

Name

Connector

1

Fuel pressure regulator

N77

2. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel pressure regulator.

Check that the fixing bolts are not damaged, otherwise they must be replaced. Fixing bolts that are not damaged can be reused.

3. Rotate the pressure regulator, by hand, in an anti-clockwise direction and remove it.

Do not use any tools for removing the fuel pressure regulator from the pressure pump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Remove the dirt in the pressure pump opening and in the spaces on the surface.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Before refitting the fuel pressure regulator, check that the metal seal (1a), the black or green seal (1b) and the white support seal (1c) are intact and correctly housed.

If they are damaged, always replace the seals, the support seal and the fixing bolts.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Lubricate all the seals using clean diesel fuel.

Place the fuel pressure regulator back in its housing on the pressure pump.

Rotate the fuel pressure regulator by hand in an anti-clockwise direction and press until it is in contact with the matching surfaces.

Position the fuel pressure regulator correctly.

Do not use any tools to place the fuel pressure regulator back in its housing.

Tighten the bolts fixing the fuel pressure regulator to the recommended torque.

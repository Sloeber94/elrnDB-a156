1060F36 lines to injectors - r r

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Proceed as described in 1060F36 lines to injectors - r r bearing in mind that you are only working on one of the pipes in question.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

1. Undo the connectors (1a), (1b) and remove the pipe from the fuel manifold to the fuel injectors (2c).

Use a new pipe from the fuel manifold to the injectors when refitting.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit a new pipe from the fuel manifold to the injector and secure it using the connectors without tightening them.

2. Tighten the connector for the pipe from the fuel manifold to the injector, injector side, to the recommended torque using a suitable spanner.

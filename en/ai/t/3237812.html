Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the lift.

Raise the car.

Make sure that the ignition is off (STOP position), then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

1. Disconnect the condensed oil return pipe from the sump.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the engine rpm and timing sensor.

Name

Connector

-

Rpm sensor

K46

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the rpm and timing sensor (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the engine rpm and timing sensor and fix it using the bolt to the cylinder block/crankcase.

Connect the electrical connection for the engine rpm and timing sensor.

Connect the oil return pipe to the engine sump.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Lower the vehicle.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

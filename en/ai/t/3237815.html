Place the diesel filter water presence sensor in its housing.

To tighten the sensor, bring the seal in contact with the diesel filter, then rotate by a further 1/4 of a turn. This method ensures that it is tightened to the specified torque (about 0.1 daNm).

Refitting

Removing

Place the fuel filter water presence sensor in its housing.

To tighten the sensor, bring the seal in contact with the diesel filter, then rotate by a further 1/4 of a turn. This method ensures that it is tightened to the specified torque (about 0.1 daNm).

Connect the electrical connection to the water-in-fuel filter presence sensor.

Replace the complete fuel filter in its seating and secure it with its nuts to the engine compartment bulkhead.

Connect the electrical connection to the fuel pre-heating device.

Connect the electrical connection to the fuel temperature sensor.

Proceed with refitting 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r

Removing

Refitting

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1B

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the protective cover (1b).

2. Remove the diesel injection system relays.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the diesel injection system relays back in their housings.

Place the protective cover back in its housing and fix it using the nut.

Connect the battery terminal (-).

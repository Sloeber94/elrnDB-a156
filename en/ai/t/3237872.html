1084C38 engine oil delivery and return pipes to cooling radiator - r + r

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the engine oil return pipe from the cooling radiator (2b).

Recover the engine oil which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the engine oil return pipe from the cooling radiator back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Place the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator back in its housing and tighten the bolts.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system - 0010T11 engine oil check level and top up .

Remove the car from the ramps.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Proceed with removal 1076A46 heat shield for catalytic preconverter r r .

Proceed with removal 1076B70 exhaust pipe between pre-catalytic converter and catalytic converter - r r .

Proceed with removal 1080B40 catalytic pre-converter - r r .

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and disconnect the engine oil supply and return pipes to the cooling radiator (1b), radiator side.

Recover the engine oil which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the cooling radiator engine oil supply and return pipes (1b), engine oil filter mounting side.

2. Tighten the bolt securing the mounting bracket for the engine oil supply and return pipes to the cooling radiator.

3. Remove the cooling radiator engine oil supply and return pipes.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the cooling radiator engine oil supply and return pipes back in their housings.

Tighten the bolt securing the mounting bracket for the engine oil supply and return pipes to the cooling radiator.

Connect the engine oil supply and return pipes to the engine cooling radiator, engine oil filter mounting side and fix them using the nuts.

Connect the engine oil supply and return pipes to the engine cooling radiator, radiator side and fix them using the bolts.

Proceed with refitting 1080B40 catalytic pre-converter - r r .

Proceed with refitting 1076B70 exhaust pipe between pre-catalytic converter and catalytic converter - r r .

Proceed with refitting 1076A46 heat shield for catalytic preconverter r r .

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Refill the engine oil lubrication system - 0010T11 engine oil check level and top up .

Remove the car from the ramps.

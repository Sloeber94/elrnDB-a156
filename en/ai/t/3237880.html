Removing

Refitting

Undo the bolt and remove the driven pulley

Release and remove the camshaft oil seal in the upper cylinder head.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during removal. The operation of removing the oil seal destroys it, therefore it must always be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

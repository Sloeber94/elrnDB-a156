Removing

Refitting

Proceed with removal 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the pipe from engine oil vapour separator to filler, separator side.

2. Loosen the band and disconnect the pipe from engine oil vapour separator to filler, filler side.

3. Remove the engine oil vapour separator pipe to the filler (3a) after releasing it from the band (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit in its seating the pipe from the engine oil vapour separator to the filler, and secure it with the band.

Connect the pipe from the engine oil vapour separator to the filler, filler side, and tighten the band.

Connect the pipe from the engine oil vapour separator to the filler, separator side, and tighten the band.

Proceed with refitting 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r .

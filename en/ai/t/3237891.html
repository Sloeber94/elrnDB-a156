Removing

On this vehicle, in order to carry out the Removal-Refitting of the gearbox it is necessary to carry out the removal-refitting of the power unit 1004B10 power unit with manual gearbox - r + r and then proceed with the dismantling at the bench 1004D10 power unit (manual gearbox), removed, remove and re-build . The latter operation includes removal-refitting of the clutch.

Refitting

Removing

IF TOOL 1.871.003.100 IS AVAILABLE, REFIT THE CLUTCH DEVICE AS DESCRIBED BELOW.

1. Fit the tool locator in the crankshaft.

Refit the clutch plate in its housing.

When refitting the clutch plate, observe the fitting direction identified by the 'engine side' or 'gearbox side' wording.

Handle the clutch plate carefully. Do not touch the friction linings with your hands.

2. Refit the clutch holder.

Clean corrosion-proofing or detergents off the clutch holder friction surface.

3. Fit the remaining part of clutch installation tool (3a) by tightening tool (3b).

4. Turn the tool wheel to bring pins (4a) into contact with plate (4b).

5. Hand-tighten the three free bolts securing the clutch device.

6. Unscrew the tool wheel to unload the clutch mechanism diaphragm spring.

Remove the tool.

Drain the brake-clutch fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

1. Disconnect the rigid pipe from reservior to master cylinder, hose side.

2. Release the rigid pipe from reservoir to master cylinder from the fastenings on the engine bay bulkhead.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and lift up the brake fluid reservoir (3b), as necessary.

4. Disconnect the rigid pipe from the reservoir to the master cylinder on the reservoir side and remove it.

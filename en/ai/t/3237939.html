Refitting

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.

2127A20 cover of control lever/handle for manual gearbox with hydraulic selection - r r

1. Remove the trim.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection from the electro-hydraulic selection manual gear lever trim.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the electro-hydraulic selection manual gear lever trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Refitting

Removing

Fit the electro-hydraulic selection manual gear lever trim and fix it using the bolts.

Connect the electrical connection to the electro-hydraulic selection manual gear lever trim.

Place the trim back in its housing.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Refitting

Removing

.

If all the procedures described below and, in particular, the fitting order for the hydraulic unit and the securing of the gearshaft control bolt to the hydraulic actuation unit, are not carried out correctly this could adversely affect the operation of the system.

Before fitting the hydraulic unit on the gearbox, clean (blow through) any impurities on the gearbox shaft thread and/or on the actuation hydraulic unit housing.

Clean the threaded housing of the bolt securing the gearbox shaft to the hydraulic actuation unit, making sure that there are absolutely no shavings, grease, oil, lubricants, etc., so that the threaded housing in 'dry' (this condition is vital to guarantee the non-slip action of the bolt).

Manually check that the shaft slides freely in its housing and that there is grease on the end part of the shaft; if necessary renew it.

FITTING A NEW HYDRAULIC UNIT

1. Undo the cap (1a) on the new unit supplied by Parts and make sure that the seal (1b) is present.

2. Fit the hydraulic unit (2a) complete with bolt (2b) ( M6 x 110) in its seat. This cannot be fiitted later on because of space constraints.

Make sure that the hydraulic unit is against the gearbox.

3. Unscrew cap (3a) and remove the spacer tube (3b) fitted by Parts to lock the selection linkage inside the hydraulic unit during shipping.

Only carry out this operation after fitting the hydraulic actuation unit to the gearbox.

4. Fit the tool in the place of the spacer pipe and tighten fully.

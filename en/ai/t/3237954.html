Refitting

Removing

Place the electronic control unit for the electro-hydraulic gear selection system back in its housing after having connected the electrical connections.

Fit the mounting bracket for the electro-hydraulic gear selection system electronic control unit and fix it using the nuts.

Fit the protection for the electronic control unit for the electro-hydraulic gear selection system and fix it using the nut and the bolt.

Fit the cover under the dashboard on the passenger side and fix it using the nuts.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Refitting

Removing

Place the gear engagement position sensor for the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox back in its housing taking care to charge the potentiometer, then fasten it using the bolts.

The potentiometer is charged by engaging the sensor on the rear position signal relay drive shaft and making it rotate by ninety degrees in relation to the final fitting position.

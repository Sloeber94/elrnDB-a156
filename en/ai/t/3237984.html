2130A04 Automatic transmission FLUID - Check level and top up, .if necessary

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Q-System

Carry out a preliminary check of the automatic transmission fluid level when cold as follows.

Park the car on level ground and engage the hand brake.

With the engine idling, starting from the P position select all the other gears 2 or 3 times then return to the P position.

Withdraw the oil level dipstick, clean it, then insert it fully into its housing.

Withdraw the dipstick again and check that the oil level is between the MIN and MAX references on the side of the stick marked "COLD".

If the oil level is low, top up with the specified oil.

The final gearbox oil level check must be carried out with the engine and gearbox at normal operating temperature (gearbox oil temperature 70° - 80° C), so the car should be driven for an appropriate time before the check.

Carry out the final check of the automatic transmission fluid level when warm as follows.

Park the car on level ground and engage the hand brake.

With the engine idling, starting from the P position select all the other gears 2 or 3 times then return to the P position.

Withdraw the oil level dipstick, clean it, then insert it fully into its housing.

Withdraw the dipstick again and check that the oil level is between the MIN and MAX references on the side of the stick marked "HOT".

If the oil level is low, top up with the specified oil.

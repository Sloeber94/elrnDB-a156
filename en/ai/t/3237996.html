1048A10 air cleaner filtering element - replace

1. Undo the drain plug and drain the gearbox oil.

Recover the automatic transmission fluid in a suitable container.

2. Disconnect the quick-release fittings of the delivery and return pipes to the gearbox oil cooling radiator.

To disconnect the quick-release fittings, push the collar towards the gearbox and withdraw the pipe.

3. Undo nut (3a) and bolt (3b) then remove automatic transmission fluid temperature sensor cover bracket (3c).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Q-System

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove the gear selection solenoid assembly cover (1b).

The solenoid assembly securing bolts have different lengths: mark them during removal so they can be refitted correctly.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections (2a, 2b, 2c, 2d, 2e, 2f, 2g) for the gear selection solenoid assembly.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Q-System

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove the gear selection solenoid assembly (1b).

The bolts securing the solenoid assembly have different lengths: mark them during removal so they can be refitted correctly.

2. Recover the protection complete with gasket.

3. Remove gaskets.

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Q-System

Refitting

Removing

Place the gaskets back in their housing.

1. Place the solenoid assembly back in its housing, ensuring that you insert selector valve lever (1a) into the lever hole (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Q-System

Place the cover complete with gaskets back in its housing and tighten the bolts securing the gear selection solenoid assembly to the specified torque.

Connect the electrical connections for the gear selection solenoids.

Clean any residues of sealant from the solenoid assembly cover contact surface.

Apply a bead of silicone sealant 1.0 - 1.5 mm thick along the edge of the solenoid assembly cover, removing any sealant that may drip inside the cover itself.

Refit in its seating the gear selection solenoids assembly cover and tighten the bolts to the specified torque.

Removing

Refitting

1. Using a suitable tool, remove the differential left internal driveshaft.

Collect the manual gearbox oil that comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Remove the left differential shaft oil seal from the flange.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during removal.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1. Remove the clip securing the brake caliper to the bracket.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections for the brake pad wear cable.

3. Disconnect the hose connector from the brake caliper.

4. Undo the bolts (4a) and remove the front brake caliper (4b) complete with pads.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

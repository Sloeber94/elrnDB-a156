Position the vehicle on the ramp

4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1. Release the flexible brake caliper pipe from the mounting bracket.

2. Remove the clip securing the brake caliper to the bracket.

3. Remove the protective caps (3a), undo the bolts (3b) and move the front brake caliper (3c) away from the bracket.

4. Undo the bolts (4a) and remove the front brake caliper bracket (4b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

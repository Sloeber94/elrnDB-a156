Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Prevent brake-clutch fluid coming into contact with painted parts; If this should happen, wash immediately with water.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check that the fluid in the reservoir is on maximum level; if not, top up with the recommended fluid.

To top up the level, remove the reservoir cap without disconnecting the minimum level warning light switch connection.

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the split pin (1a) and withdraw the pin (1b) connecting the clutch pump to the clutch pedal.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and remove the clutch pedal (2b) complete with bush (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the clutch pedal complete with bush and secure it to the pedal assembly tightening the bolt to torque.

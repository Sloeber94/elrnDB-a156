Removing

Refitting

Proceed with the removal 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Proceed with the removal 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

1. Disconnect the brake servo single-acting valve complete with pipe from the brake servo.

2. Disconnect and remove the brake servo single-acting valve from the vacuum pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Connect the brake servo single-acting valve to the vacuum pipe.

Place the brake servo single-acting valve, complete with pipe, back in its housing on the brake servo.

Proceed with the refitting 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Proceed with the refitting 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods

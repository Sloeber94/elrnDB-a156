The letters that indicate to which caliper and to which brake upmp oulet they should be connected are stamped at each outlet on the control unit. RR = to the right rear brake (pipe marked with a light blue coloured band) LR = to the left rear brake (pipe marked with a green coloured band) RF = to the right front caliper (pipe marked with a yellow coloured band) LF = to the left front caliper (pipe marked with a white coloured band) MC1 = from the pump primary circuit/flange side (pipe marked with a black or pink coloured band when the pipe polyammide resin is black) MC2 = from the pump secondary circuit/front side (pipe marked with a brown coloured band).

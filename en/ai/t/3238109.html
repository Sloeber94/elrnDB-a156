Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

The VDC steering angle sensor is incorporated in the steering column switch unit and cannot be dismantled on its own. Therefore to remove it, proceed as described in 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag .

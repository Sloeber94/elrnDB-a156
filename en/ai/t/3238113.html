3380A04 handbrake - check travel

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Handbrake adjustment must be made only after replacing:

back brake pads

handbrake control ropes

back brake calipers

as backlash recovery due to wear is automatic.

Place the car on the lifting bridge.

Remove handbrake guard.

Lift the car.

Disconnect handbrake control ropes from brake calipers.

Pull srongly the foot brake pedal at least ten times.

Connect handbrake control ropes to brake calipers.

Lower the car.

Make handbrake control lever correspond to the third release on the serrated sector.

Tighten adjustment nut on handbrake lever until back wheels stop.

Check back wheels are free when lever is in the rest position.

Mount handbrake guard.

Take the car off the lifting bridge.

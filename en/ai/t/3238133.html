4130B14 Power assisted steering fluid reservoir CAP - R.R. - Check level and top up if necessary.

Path
Procedures > 41 STEERING > 4130 POWER ASSISTED STEERING > 4130B power steering hydraulic sytem > 4130B14 Power assisted steering fluid reservoir CAP - R.R. - Check level and top up if necessary.
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.0 JTS, 2.5 V6, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.0 JTS, 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-a156/en/#/3238133

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

1. Undo and remove the power assisted steering fluid reservoir cap and check that the fluid level corresponds to the upper reference on the dipstick in the actual reservoir cap.

The level should be checked with the vehicle on a flat surface and the engine switched off and cold.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

If the level is not within the recommended limtis, top up as follows.

Top up the power assisted circuit with the recommended fluid.

TUTELA GI/A

Bleed the power steering system as described below

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4-5 times.

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Position the vehicle on the ground, start the engine and carry out 4 - 5 full steering wheel turn cycles (right and left).

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Retighten the power assisted steering fulid reservoir cap.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

4130B52 heat exchanger pipe - r+r

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Drain the power steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

Disconnect the heat exchanger pipe oil inlet and outlet pipe.

1. Remove the heat exchanger pipe after removing it from the relevant retaining bands.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the heat exchanger pipe back in its housing and secure using the relevant bands.

Connect the oil inlet and outlet pipe to the heat exchanger pipe.

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

The power steering system is self-bleeding; to bleed, turn the steering wheel full lock left and right with the engine running and the car stopped.

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4-5 times.

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Position the vehicle on the ground and start up the engine.

Carry out 4 - 5 complete steering wheel rotation cycles (right and left).

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Proceed with removal 1084C40 Air DUCT for engine oil cooling radiator - R.R . .

1. Disconnect the rapid attachment coupling from the return pipe from the heat exchanger to the power steering fluid reservoir, heat exchanger side.

2. Disconnect the quick-release fitting on the return pipe from power steering box to heat exchanger, heat exchanger side.

Collect the power assisted steering fluid in a suitable container.

3. Undo the nut (3a) and remove the bracket (3b) for the heat exchanger fastening pipe.

4. Remove the heat exchanger pipe (4a) after releasing it from the fastening (4b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

4130B78 COOLING RADIATOR power assisted steering fluid INLET PIPE - R.R.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the cooling radiator power assisted steering fluid inlet pipe, radiator side.

Collect the power assisted steering fluid in a suitable container.

2. Loosen the band, disconnect the cooling radiator power assisted steering fluid inlet pipe, rigid pipe side, and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the cooling radiator power assisted steering fluid inlet pipe back in its housing, connect it to the rigid pipe and tighten the band.

Connect the rapid connector for the cooling radiator power assisted steering fluid inlet pipe, radiator side.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

Bleed the power steering system as described below

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4-5 times.

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Remove the car from the ramps.

Start the engine and carry out 4 - 5 full steering wheel turn cycles (right and left).

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Drain the power steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

7055B64 Engine compartment DUST shield in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R.

Pull up the left front wheel arch partly

1008A42 gear box left rigid mount - r r

1008B30 gearbox side mount flexible block - replace

1. Slacken the bands and remove the rubber pipe between the heat exchanger and the fluid tank rigid pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Loosen bolts (1a) and undo bolts (1b) securing the lower radiator beam to the body.

2. Disconnect the rapid connectors for the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger pipe side.

Collect the power assisted steering fluid in a suitable container.

3. Disconnect the rapid connectors for the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger, intermediate pipes side.

4. Release the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering system hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger from the fastenings on the lower radiator crossmember, then remove them.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering system hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger back in their housings and secure them to the lower radiator crossmember.

Connect the rapid connectors for the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering system hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger, intermediate pipes side.

Connect the rapid connectors for the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering system hydraulic circuit from/to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger pipe side.

Tighten the bolts securing the radiator bottom crossmember to the bodywork.

Proceed with refitting 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

4130B53 heat exchanger pipe - r r for rhd versions

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Drain the power steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Disconnect the flexible hoses connecting the power steering system to the heat exchanger pipe.

2. Loosen the nuts on the heat exchanger pipe mounting bracket.

3. Remove the heat exchanger pipe after releasing it from the attachment band.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Refitting

Removing

Refit the heat exchanger pipe in its seating and secure it with the relevant band.

Tighten the nuts on the heat exchanger pipe mounting bracket.

Connect the flexible hoses connecting the power steering system to the heat exchanger pipe.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

Bleed the power steering system as described below.

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off, carry out 4-5 complete steering wheel rotation cycles (left and right).

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Position the vehicle on the ground and start up the engine.

Carry out 4 - 5 complete steering wheel rotations (right and left).

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Drain the power steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

1. Slacken bands (1a) and disconnect the flexible hose (1b) from the heat exchanger pipe.

2. Undo nuts (2a) and remove the heat exchanger pipe (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Refitting

Removing

Place the heat exchanger pipe back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Connect the flexible pipes to the heat exchanger pipe and secure them using the bands.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

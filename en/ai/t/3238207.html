Refitting

Place the vehicle on the elevator bridge

Lift the vehicle.

1. Unscrew the nuts of the stabilizing bar rear suspension to its articulated joints.

2. Unscrew the screws (2a) and remove the stabilizing bar rear suspension (2b) with its supporting screw achors (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Raise the vehicle.

Remove the wheel hub cap.

Undo the bolts and remove the wheel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the wheel and fix it using the bolts without tightening them.

Lower the vehicle.

Tighten the bolts, which have a "Dacromet Plus" surface protection and can be recognized by their opaque grey colour, to the recommended torque.

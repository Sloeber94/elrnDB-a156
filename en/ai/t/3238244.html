Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged and that there are no cracks which would affect the seal.

Reposition the pipe and connect it to the connectors.

If necessary, lubricate the ends of the pipes with vaseline to faciliate refitting.

The heater water supply and return pipes have white or green coloured ends to prevent them from being incorrectly fitted after operations where they are both removed.

Tighten the pipe retaining bands.

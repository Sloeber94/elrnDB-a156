1. Working inside the cabin, open the clip securing the wiring to the heater.

2. Disconnect both electrical connections (2a (2b) to the heater.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the heater casing is not damaged.

Reposition the heater casing, complete with wiring ..

Tighten the bolts fixing the heater casing.

Release the wiring secured temporarily during removal.

Use clips to secure the wiring to the integrated servic control unit and relay bracket.

Connect the electrical connections to the integrated services control unit.

Refit the bracket.

Tighten the integrated services control unit and relay retaining bolts.

Fit the central bracket and secure with bolts.

Fasten the air bag wiring to the heater.

Fit the clips securing the heater wiring to the underfacia wiring.

Reconnect both heater electrical connections.

Secure the wiring clip to the heater.

5580C14 air bag ecu - r+r

Before working on the Air Bag system, follow the safety instructions for working on the system very carefully (See 5580C - Air Bag System - Precautions).

1. Working inside the passenger compartment, disconnect heater electrical connections (1a), (1b).

Name

Connector

1a

Dashboard/air conditioning-heater connection

D21A

Name

Connector

1b

Dashboard/air conditioning-heater connection

D21B

2. Open the clip securing the heater wiring to the underfacia wiring.

3. Release the air bag wiring from the heater and move.

4. Undo the fixing bolts and remove the bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Heater

1. Move the wiring and secure temporarily.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the heater casing.

3. Remove the heater casing, complete with wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Heater

Refitting

Removing

Check that the heater casing is not damaged.

Reposition the heater casing, complete with wiring.

Tighten the bolts fixing the heater casing.

Release the wiring secured temporarily during removal.

Position the bracket and fix it using the bolts.

Fasten the air bag wiring to the heater.

Position the wiring beneath the facia and do up the retaining collars.

Reconnect both heater electrical connections.

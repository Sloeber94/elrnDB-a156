Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

1. Disconnect the facia wiring harness clips.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections.

3. Disconnect the retaining bands.

4. Remove the heater case wiring harness.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Refit the air conditioner case wiring harness.

Connect the retaining clips.

Connect the electrical connections.

Connect the clips to the facia wiring harness.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Reposition the pipe.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors.

Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid.

Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Connect the pipe connectors.

Tighten the bolt securing the pipe connector to the condenser.

5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

1. Disconnect the connector (1a) for the pipe from the compressor (1b).

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

1. Disconnect the connector (1a) for the pipe from the condenser (1b).

2. Keep the pipe from the condenser to the compressor.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A refrigerant. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Position the pipe.

Connect the connector for the condenser pipe and tighten the bolt.

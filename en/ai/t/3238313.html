Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Removing

Refitting

Position the car on the lift.

Drain the coolant. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling

5540D10 l/h or r/h fog lamp (one) - r+r (right side).

1. Unscrew the screws fastening the pipe to the condenser.

2. Disconnect the pipe union from the condenser.

Plug the disconnected unions properly in order to prevent humidity and dirt from entering the system.

3. Unscrew the dehydrating filter support.

4. Release the dehydrating filter, together with its support, from the body.

5. Unscrew the pipe union from the filter.

6. Disconnect the union.

7. Remove the pipe.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or slitted.

Reposition the pipe.

Replace union O-rings.

Use green R134A coolantproof rings only.

Lubricate union threads with antifreezing oil.

Connect the pipe union to the filter and fasten the screw.

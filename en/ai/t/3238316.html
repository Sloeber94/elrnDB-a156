Removing

Refitting

Observe the following precaution.

Follow the recommended safety instructions (see subgroup 5040 - AIR CONDITIONER CASING AND COMPONENTS)

Place the vehicle on a lift.

7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R.

7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R.

Drain the coolant ( 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling ).

7055A43 central front grille - r r

7055A41 RIGHT FRONT GRILLE - R.R.

7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. Disconnect the pipe connector from the condenser.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

2. Disconnect the pipe from the intermediate connector.

3. Keep the pipe from the condenser to the connector.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A refrigerant. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Position the pipe.

Connect the pipe connectors and tighten the bolt and the nut.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the electronic governor is not damaged and, if necessary, conduct an electrical test on it (see 'Descriptions and Operation').

Reposition the electronic variable valve timing.

Tighten the screws.

Connect the electrical connection.

Position the bulkhead and fix it using the nuts.

Test the operation of the fan.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Air Conditioner

Removing

Refitting

Name

Connector

1

Air intake actuator

2. Unscrew the bolt.

3. Rotate the air recirculation and intake assembly and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the air recirculation and intake flap is not damaged.

Position and rotate the air recirculation and intake assembly and secure it using the bolt.

Connect the electrical connection for the air recirculation and intake motor.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the motor is not damaged.

Do not check the function of the motor by connecting it to 12V. Test the system, by means of Examiner or other diagnostic systems, at the end of the operation.

1. Position the motor.

To make easier the motor shaft connection, reconnect the electrical connection and rotate the motor by operating the control on the dashboard, until the lining up with the flap pin.

2. Screw the screws.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Remove motor (3a) by releasing outlet shaft (3b) from the operating pin of flap (3c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the motor is undamaged.

Do not test motor operation by connecting to the 12V supply. Once the operation is completed, test the system using an Examiner or another tester.

1. Position the motor.

To facilitate connection of the motor shaft to the motor, reconnect the electrical connection and turn the motor by operating the control on the facia until it is aligned with the flap pin.

2. Tighten the bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Air Conditioner

1056B82 injection/ignition system e.c.u. (one) - r + r

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

1. Disconnect the facia wiring harness retaining clips.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

3. Turn and remove sensors (3a), then disconnect electrical connections (3b).

4. Disconnect the retaining bands.

5. Remove the air conditioner case wiring harness.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Refit the air conditioning case wiring harness.

Do up the retaining clips.

Connect the sensor electrical connections, then fit into their seats and turn.

Connect the electrical connection.

Do up the facia wiring harness connecting clips.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

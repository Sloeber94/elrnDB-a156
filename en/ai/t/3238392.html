2. Disconnect the facia wiring electrical connection.

4. Disconnect the rear wiring electrical connection.

5. Disconnect the right headlamp alignment motor electrical connection.

6. Disconnect the electrical connection for the horn.

7. Disconnect the electrical connections from the electric windscreen and headlamp washer pumps.

8. Undo the bolt and remove the wiring clip.

9. Open the right wiring sleeve.

10. Disconnect the left headlamp alignment motor electrical connection.

11. Release the relays from the mounting.

12. Open the left wiring sleeve.

13. Disconnect the earth terminal.

14. Disconnect the electrical connections from the relay units.

15. Disconnect the electrical connections to the engine wiring.

1. Prise out the weather strip.

2. Disconnect fuel pipe (2a) from clamp (2b) and move.

3. Unscrew the bolt and remove the bracket.

4. Unscrew the bolts and move the power steering fluid reservoir.

6. Undo the bolts, move brake fluid reservoir (6a) and disconnect electrical connection (6b).

7. Remove the retaining buttons using the special tool.

Disconnect the electrical connection from the injection control unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6, 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

1. Connect a probe to the end of side flasher leads (1a) and winscreen wiper motor lead (1b).

This operation is necessary to make it easier to refit the wires in their seats.

Withdraw the electrical wires from the body and leave the probes in place.

2. Disconnect clips (2a) retaining front brake pad sensor right and left leads (2b).

3. Disconnect the electrical connections.

Remove or disconnect any other wiring retainers.

4. Prise out the rubber pad from the partition.

5. Remove the front wiring harness by withdrawing from the engine bay.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Refit the front wiring harness by inserting from the engine bay.

Refit the rubber pad in the partition.

Connect the electrical connection to the engine wiring.

Connect the various electrical connections.

Fit the retaining clips to the front brake pade sensor left and right wires.

Use the probes fitted during removal to insert the side flasher electrical wires and the windscreen wiper motor lead.

Secure the windscreen wiper motor lead using a retaining clip.

Connect the windscreen wiper motor electrical connection and refit the protective boot.

Position the upper flap of the partition panel.

Secure the front wiring harness using retaining clips.

Do up the retaining stud.

Fit the brake fluid reservoir, tighten the bolts and connect the electrical connection.

Fit the bracket and tighten the bolts.

Fit the power steering fluid reservoir and tighten the bolts.

Fit the bracket and tighten the bolt.

Secure the fuel pipe using a clamp.

Fit the weather strip.

Connect the electrical connections.

Connect the earth terminal.

Close the left wiring sleeve.

Secure the relays to the mount.

Connect the left headlamp alignment motor electrical connection.

Close the right wiring sleeve.

Fit the wiring clip and do up the bolt.

Connect the windscreen and headlamp washer pump electrical connections.

Connect the electrical connections for the horns.

Connect the right headlamp alignment motor electrical connection.

Connect the rear wiring electrical connections.

Connect the brake light switch electrical connection.

Connect the facia wiring electrical connection.

Connect the ignition switch electrical connection.

Connect the electrical connections between front wiring harness and junction unit / CODE control unit.

Fit the junction unit / CODE control unit mounting and tighten the retaining bolts.

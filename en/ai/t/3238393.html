Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner and Heater

1. Disconnect the electrical connections to the rear wiring harness and front wiring.

2. Disconnect the ignition electrical connection.

3. Disconnect the earth ring.

4. Release the wiring from the retaining clip.

5. Remove the wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Refit the underfacia wiring harness.

Secure the wiring harness using retaining clips.

Connect the earth ring.

Connect the electrical connections to to the various components.

Connect the electrical connections to the front and rear wiring.

Refit the junction unit - CODE control unit mount unit and do up the bolts.

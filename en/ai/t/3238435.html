5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

5530B10 battery - r+r

Release the air duct hose from the mounting bracket on the drip tray.

Remove the relay shield.

1. Release the relays from the battery drip tray and remove them.

2. Undo the nut fixing the air filter mounting bracket to the battery drip tray.

3. Undo the two fixing nuts (3a) and move the connections/electrical wiring mounting bracket (3b) aside.

4. Release the accelerator cable from the fastening on the bracket.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

1. Undo the upper bolts (1a) fixing the drip tray to the bodyshell and loosen the lower one (1b).

2. Remove the battery cage.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the drip tray in the battery housing.

Tighten the 4 bolts fixing the battery drip tray to the bodyshell.

Secure the accelerator cable to the fastening for the bracket on the drip tray.

Position the connections/electrical wiring mounting bracket and tighten the two fixing bolts.

Tighten the nut fixing the air filter mounting bracket to the battery drip tray.

Refit the relays on the tray with the shield.

Reattach the fastening for the air duct hose to the mounting bracket for the drip tray.

Removing

Refitting

5530B10 battery - r+r

Removing

Secure the electrical wiring to the fastenings and to the bands on the battery drip tray.

Place the relay mounting bracket in its housing and tighten the nuts.

Fit the battery acid collection tank with drainage pipe.

5530B10 battery - r+r

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

1. Open the bonnet lid then rotate the headlamp cover in an anti-clockwise direction and remove it.

2. For ease of access, disconnect the electrical connection from the main beam bulb.

3. Extract the bulb holder with the bulb.

(The bulb holder is press fit type.)

4. Extract the bulb from the bulb holder.

(The bulb is press fit type.)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Insert the bulb in the bulb holder.

Fit the bulb holder with the bulb in its housing.

Connect the electrical connection to the main beam bulb.

Check that the bulb is working.

Refit the lamp cover and rotate it in a clockwise direction.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Procedure for the direction indicator. For the main beam headlamps, see op. 5540B47.

Open the bonnet lid.

1. Rotate the direction indicator bulb holder in an anti-clockwise direction (bayonet fitting) and extract it.

2. Remove the bulb from the bulb holder (bayonet fitting).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition and the cleanliness of the lamp.

Fit the bulb in position and rotate it.

Fit the bulb holder with the bulb in position and rotate it.

Check that the direction indicators are working properly.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

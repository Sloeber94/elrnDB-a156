Refitting

1. Unscrew the screw.

2. Remove the frame (2a) and take it off the check clamps (2b).

3. Unscrew the screws.

4. Extract the speedometer (4a) from the support (4b) e move it.

5. Disconnect the electric connections (5a, 5b).

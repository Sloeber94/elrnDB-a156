5570T66 module (one) on steering wheel for radio control - r+r

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Removing

Refitting

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES ( 5580C air bag system - Precautions).

1. Undo the bolt (1a) keeping the washer (1b) still.

2. Move the horn contact slightly.

3. Disconnect the electrical connections.

4. Remove the switch holder surround (4a) releasing it from the retainers (4b).

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Refitting

Removing

Check that the radio controls on the steering wheel are not damaged.

Place the switch holder surround in position and engage the retainers.

Connect the electrical connection

Position the horn contact and tighten the bolt keeping the washer still.

Test the operation of the radio controls on the steering wheel.

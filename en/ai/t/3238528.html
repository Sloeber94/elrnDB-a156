Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before detaching and reattaching the Air Bag module, carry out the following preliminary operations:

- Turn the ignition key on "STOP" position and remove it;

- Disconnect and isolate the battery cables.

- Wait 10 minutes before proceeding.

OBSERVE ALL PRESCRIBED SAFETY REGULATIONS (see 5080C - Precautions). BE CAREFUL.

Place the Air Bag module in the special cabinet on a free surface and place the inflatable bag upwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Observe the following precaution.

Handle the Air Bag module very cautiously.

Make sure the battery is disconnected.

If a new module is installed, put the relative labels.

Remove the removable part of the adhesive labels from the new module, put it in the special dossier and write the car data (model, registration data, chassis number) and the serial number of the old module.

Together with the module a new adhesive plate is provided which has to be sticked on the one already present on the driver side door post. But it must be previously pierced on the correspondant month and the 10 years following the year in which the module is mounted. (for ex. 2007 will correspond to 1997)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Put the Air Bag module in its seat.

Screw the screws and block them to the prescribed couple.

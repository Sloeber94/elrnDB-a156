Before removing and refitting the sensor, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: - Turn the ignition off "STOP" and remove the key; - Disconnect the battery.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

7040H36 central trim lower element - r r

1. Unscrew both retaining bolts.

2. Withdraw the sensor from the bottom.

3. Disconnect the electrical connection and remove the sensor.

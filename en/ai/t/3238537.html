1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the cable loom.

2. Release the cable loom from the retaining bands.

3. Remove the wiring harness.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring loom is not damaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Position the cable loom.

Connect the electrical connection.

Secure the wiring loom with the retainers.

Refitting

Follow the warnings.

Before removing and refitting the Air Bag module cable loom, the following preliminary operations must be carried out:Place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it;Disconnect the battery leads and insulate.Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES ( 5580C air bag system - Precaustions).

7045A85 Front seat BACKREST COVER, left or right - R.R.

1. Using a drill, remove the rivets (1a) fixing the Air Bag module casing (1b).

2. Move the casing (1b) slightly and remove the Air Bag module cable loom (2a).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is intact and shows no signs of damage.

If a new wiring is being fitted, attach the labels as described below.

1. Detach the removable part (1b) of the adhesive label from the new wiring (1a). Place a copy of the label (1b) in a specific file containing the vehicle details (model, registration data, chassis number) and the serial number of the old backrest cover. This file should be kept for future checks.

2. Fit the original label (1b) at the bottom of the seat, where there are already the equivalent labels for seat and side Air Bag module.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: GTA

Place the cable loom and the Air Bag module casing in position.

Fasten the casing using the rivets.

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the striker bolts.

2. Remove the striker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the striker is undamaged.

Fit the striker.

1. Screw in the retaining bolts without tightening.

2. Use the bolt slots to move the striker into the correct position.

Fit shims if necessary in order to adjust the striker accurately.

Permitted adjustment movements are indicated by arrows.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tighten the retaining bolts.

Check the striker and catch fit together properly and ensure the door is aligned when closed.

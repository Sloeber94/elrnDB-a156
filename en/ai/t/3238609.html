Refitting

Removing

Use a new lining.

Thoroughly clean any adhesive residues from the fitting area using heptane.

Bathe the fitting area in soapy water.

1. Remove the protection from the adhesive side of the lining, then place it in position.

The soapy water facilitates the sliding needed for the correct positioning.

2. Using a sponge, press on the lining to remove any air bubbles.

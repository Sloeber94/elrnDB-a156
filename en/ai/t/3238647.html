Refitting

1. Disconnect the door opening link from the outer handle side.

2. Unscrew the bolts

3. Remove the outer handle.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the outer handle and the handle mechanism are undamaged.

Position the outer handle.

Do up the bolts.

Connect the door opening link.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Removing

Check that the outer handle is undamaged.

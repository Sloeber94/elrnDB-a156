7040G36 BULB HOLDER FOR LOAD CARRYING COMPARTMENT lighting on tailgate - R.R.

1. Release the covers for the lights, moving them in the direction of the arrow.

2. Remove the bolt covers.

3. Unscrew the panel retaining bolts.

4. Release the inner retainers using the appropriate tool.

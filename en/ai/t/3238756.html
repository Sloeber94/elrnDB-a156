7030B17 sun-roof glass - fit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Move the sun roof to the closed position and open the curtain sunshade..

1. Slacken the six bolts (1a) securing the sun roof window (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Fit a 4 mm spacer (1a) between the rear of the glass panel and the wall of the hatchway in the roof.

2. Tighten the rear right and left bolts (2c).

3. Adjust the front right part (3a) of the window to 1 mm lower than the roof's outer profile (as shown in the figure), then tighten the front right bolt (2a).

4. Loosen the right and left bolts (2c).

5. Adjust the rear right part (5a) of the window to 1 mm higher than the roof's outer profile (as shown in the figure), then tighten the right bolts (2c) and (2b).

6. rpeat points 3. and 5. for the left side of the window.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check that the sun roof works properly and slides into its various positions.

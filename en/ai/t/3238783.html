1. Remove the sealant strip (1a) from the side window co-moulded strip (1b) using pliers, as shown in the figure. The remaining sealant does not have to be removed completely; simply reduce it to a thickness of between 0.25 and 1 mm. Take care not to damage the pressed seal (1b) on the perimeter of the window.

2. Carefully clean with compressed air the surface of the window's moulded seal and thoroughly degrease with heptane.

Check that the window is undamaged.

If sticking takes place 60 minutes after the cleaning of the pressed seal, the surface of the pressed seal must be treated with the special activator and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fixing the glass.

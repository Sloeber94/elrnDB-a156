Removing

Refitting

1. Open the flap and remove the rod sliding trims.

2. Press the flap rods inwards, release them from the internal retainers and extract them.

3. Undo the bolts.

4. Remove the door.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the door is undamaged.

Refit the flap fixing it using the bolts.

Inser the flap rods in their housings.

Fit the rod sliding trims.

Removing

Refitting

1. Release the cover from the retainers pulling it from the bottom.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lid and retainers are undamaged.

Refit the cover fitting it in its housing and checking that the retainers are correctly fastened.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Removing

Refitting

1. Rotate the centre lock and release it.

2. Release the cover from the retainers extracting it from the bottom.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover, the retainers and the centre lock are not damaged.

Refit the cover fitting it in its housing and checking that the retainers are correctly fastened.

Rotate the centre lock and engage it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the boot lid and undo the bolts and luggage retainer.

2. Remove the load compartment trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trim is undamaged.

Refit the load compartment trim.

Tighten the bolts and luggage retainer.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the tailgate and remove the moving cover.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the mounting ring.

3. Remove the mounting ring.

4. Loosen the bolt fixing the mounting ring.

5. Remove the luggage compartment fixed cover.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trim is undamaged.

Fit the trim.

Position the removed mounting ring.

Tighten the bolts fixing the mounting rings.

Position the load carrying compartment moving cover.

Removing

Refitting

1. Release the switch holder trim from the sun roof (1a), releasing the retainers (1b).

2. Release the switch from the trim.

3. Recover the trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trim is not damaged.

Fit the switch in the trim.

Fit the switch holder trim and engage the retainers.

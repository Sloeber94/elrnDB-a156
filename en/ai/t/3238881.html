- 7045A84 Front set BACKREST COVER - R.R. with seat removed

1. Undo the bolts fixing the inner joint to the cushion support.

2. Recover the inner plate and nuts.

3. Undo the rear nuts shown for the cushion support.

4. Loosen the front nut shown.

5. Disconnect the linkage.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts fixing the inner joint to the backrest.

2. Release and recover the plate and counter-plate.

3. Remove the inner joint (3a) releasing it from the threaded pins (3b) and from the transmission rod (3c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the inner joint is not damaged.

Position the joint and engage it with the threaded pins and the transmission rod.

Position the backrest and place the counter-plate and the plate in position.

Tighten the nuts fixing the inner joint to the backrest.

Connect the linkage.

Tighten the nuts fixing the cushion support.

Position the inner plate and tighten the joint fixing bolts.

Check that the backrest angle adjustment device is working properly.

Removing

Refitting

1. Unscrew the screws.

2. Remove the collar badge.

3. Remove the metal points.

4. Remove the cover (4a) of the headrest from the padding and take the cover (4b) made of cloth not clothed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Make sure that the covers are not damaged.

Place the cover made of cloth not clothed on the padding and place the cover of the headrest.

Shape the cover on the padding.

Install the metal points.

Reinstall the collar badge.

Screw the screws.

1. Lift up the seat cover and upholstery covering the seat backrest adjustment motor slightly.

2. Undo the lower motor fixing bolts.

3. Disconnect the electrical connections.

4. Remove the motor, releasing the pinion from the seat backrest frame toothed wheel.

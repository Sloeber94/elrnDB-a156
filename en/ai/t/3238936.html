7050B10 l/h or r/h head restraint (one) of rear seat - r+r

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Set the head restraint in the raised position.

2. Press the right retainer and raise the head restraint to the limit of its travel.

3. Press both the right and left retainers and lift the head restraint until it comes out.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

4. If necessary, prise off and withdraw the head restraint tube.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the head restraint and retainers are undamaged.

If removed, fit the head restraint tube.

Refit the head restraint in the tubes.

Press both right and left retainers and insert the head restraint fully in the tubes.

Check that the head restraint height adjustment system is working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Removing

Refitting

1. Raise the head restraint to the limit of its travel.

2. Press the retainers and remove the head restraint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. If necessary, release the retainer (1a) and remove the head restraint tube (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Refitting

Removing

Check that the head restraint is intact and shows no signs of damage.

If removed, place the tube in its seating and engage the retainer.

Re-insert the head restraint in the tubes until they are home.

Press the retainers and insert the head restraint fully in the tubes.

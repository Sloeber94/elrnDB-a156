7050B18 rear seat arm rest - r+r

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Press the retainers (1a) in the direction of the arrows and remove the armrest (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the armrest is not damaged.

Press the retainers and refit the armrest in position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Removing

Refitting

1. Lower the armrest.

2. Remove the armrest (2a), releasing it from the retainers (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Refitting

Removing

Check that the armrest is not damaged.

Fit the armrest and engage the retainers.

Raise the armrest.

Refitting

1. Slightly heat the lettering.

2. Detach the lettering using a plastic spatula.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lettering is undamaged.

Use heptane to clean the area of the bodywork to which the lettering is to be refitted.

Apply a piece of adhesive paper to the outer edge of the lettering in order to hold the parts in place.

Apply the lettering after removing the adhesive protection. Ensure it is correctly positioned.

Remove the adhesive paper applied previously to the outside of the lettering.

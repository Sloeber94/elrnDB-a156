Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the cover (1a) by using a screwdriver with thin blade and insert it in the proper crack (1b).

2. Remove the check rod from the hole and take the cover.

Be careful not to damage the painting of the bumper .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Make sure that the cover is not damaged.

Insert the rod in the hole.

Place the cover into its seat.

Refitting

Remove the wheel.

1. Unscrew the screws.

2. By using the proper tool, remove the supplementary wheel loop.

Removing

Make sure that the wheel loop is not damaged.

Place the supplementary wheel loop.

Screw the screws.

Install the wheel again.

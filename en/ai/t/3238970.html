Before disjoin and release pretention rods again, strictly keep to the following preliminary operations:Place ignition key on "STOP" position and remove it.Disconnect and insulate battery cables.Wait 10 minues before acting.KEEP TO ALL THE PRESCRIBED SAFETY RULES (SEE 7065A Precautions).

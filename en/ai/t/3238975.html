Refitting

Removing

Check that the seat belt is undamaged.

If the seat belt has been subjected to great stress, e.g. following an accident, it must be replaced as a complete unit together with its anchorages and the retaining bolts. Even if the seat belt does not show signs of visible defects, it may have lost its strength properties.

1. Fit the seat belt.

2. Tighten the winder retaining bolt.

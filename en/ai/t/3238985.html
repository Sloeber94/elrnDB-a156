Removing

Refitting

1. Unscrew I' sun blind fixing screw.

2. Remove I' sun blind (2a) by releasing it from stop (2b).

3. Remove and lift I' grab handle badge.

4. Unscrew I' grab handle fixing screw.

5. Remove l' grab handle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check l' sun blind and its grab handle are not damaged.

Position l' grab handle.

Tighten l' grab handle fixing screw.

Put l' grab handle badge.

Reassembly l' sun blind by blocking it in stop.

Tighten l' sun blind fixing screw.

Check movement regularity of l' sun blind.

Refitting

Rotate the engine through 180° on the overhauling stand.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the oil sump (1b) complete with fixed single drive belt tensioner (1c) and rpm and timing sensor (1d).

To release the sump, tap it gently with a wooden or resin hammer, then use a screwdriver to lever against the specific projections on the sides of the sump.

Dismantling

Reassembling

Place the pressure pump mounting in a vice fitted with protective jaws.

1. Stop the rotation of the pressure pump pulley using two bolts.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the safety flange (2b).

3. Undo the pressure pump pulley nut.

Undo the two bolts fitted previously to stop the rotation of the pressure pump pulley.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

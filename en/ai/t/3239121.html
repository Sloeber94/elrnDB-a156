Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is OFF (STOP position), then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the injector.

Name

Connector

-

Injector

N70

Using a multimeter, check that the injector winding resistance corresponds to the recommended value.

Value

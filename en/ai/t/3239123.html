1056BC Check intake air temperature sensor operation

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is OFF (STOP position), then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the intake air temperature sensor.

Name

Connector

-

Air temperature sensor

K42

Using a multimeter, check that the air intake temperature sensor resistance is: 8100 - 10700 Ohm at -10° C; 2300 - 2700 Ohm at +20° C; 300 - 360 at +80° C.

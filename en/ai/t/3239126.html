Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the hot film air flow meter.

Name

Connector

-

Air flow meter

K41

1, Air temperature sensor 2, 14 Volt supply 3, Earth 4, 5 Volt reference voltage 5, Output voltage (signal)

Check the air flow meter curve by measuring the voltage between pins 3 and 5 in accordance with the air flow rate.

Check that the nominal resistance of the hot film air flow meter corresponds to the recommended value.

Measurement

Value

-

Nominal resistance (ohm)

Pin 1-3

2000 +/- 5%

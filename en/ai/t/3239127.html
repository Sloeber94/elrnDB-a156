Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is OFF (STOP position), then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the engine coolant temperature sensor.

Name

Connector

-

Engine temperature sender unit

K36

Check that the nominal resistance of the engine coolant temperature sensor corresponds to the recommended value.

A 'dual NTC' post-modification engine coolant temperature sensor has been adopted.

The nominal resistance is measured between pins B1 and B2.

Measurement

Value

-

Winding resistance (ohm)

at +20°C

860 +/- 10

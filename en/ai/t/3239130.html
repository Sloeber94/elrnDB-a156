1056BX MAP/flow meter signal plausibility

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments).

Let the engine warm up and reach the following conditions: engine warm (temperature above 85° C), throttle position less than 20% (or 15°), fan off.

Check that the air flow rate is within the permissible limits.

Engine speed (rpm):

  • Nominal value 850
  • Minimum 810
  • Maximum 890

Air flow rate (kg/h):

  • Nominal value 10
  • Minimum 6
  • Maximum 14

Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments).

Let the engine warm up and reach the following conditions: engine warm (temperature above 85° C), engine idling, fan off and lever in position N (only for versions with automatic transmission).

Check that the air flow rate is within the permissible limits.

Air flow rate (kg/h):

  • Nominal valuve 16 (18*)
  • Minimum 8
  • Maximum 24 (28*)

(*): version with automatic transmission

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the fuel vapour recovery solenoid valve.

Name

Connector

-

Fuel vapour recovery solenoid

L10

Check that the resistance for the winding corresponds to the recommended value.

Measurement

Value

-

Winding resistance (ohm)

26 +/- 4

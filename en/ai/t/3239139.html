Applies to: engines: 2.5 V6; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal (refer to op. 5530B10 battery - r+r ).

Disconnect the electrical connection from the butterfly casing actuator.

Name

Connector

-

Throttle body actuator

N75

Check, using the multimeter, that the butterfly casing actuator resistance is 1.25 Ohm ± 20% between pins 2 and 3.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Measure the pump supply voltage at the pump connector.

Measure the pump absorption current positioning the ammeters pliers zeroed and set to 200mv on the pump supply cable on the fuse box. E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Check that the supply voltage to the pump on the pump connector is 12V.

Measure the pump absorption current by positioning the ammeter clip for the Examiner on the pump supply cable (on the fuse box or directly on the pump inlet cable. E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The current measured should be between 2 and 10 Ampere.

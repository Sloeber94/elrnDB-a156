1064BC Variable geometry turbocharger actuator leakage test

Path

Kind
test / check procedure
Applies to
engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Visually inspect that whilst the engine is being started (key in AVV position) the turbocharger actuator connecting rod (turbine stator vane positioning device) moves by about 10 mm in the direction of the actuator.

With the engine switched off, connect connector EX09 with transducer EX06 to the section of pipe between the actuator and the solenoid valve.

In the instrument ambient, select the intake pressure measurement pressure gauge.

Start the engine and let it idle, then wait until the pressure level stabilizes.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

The pressure reading should be between 450 and 500 mmHg.

The graphic acquisition of the pressure can be made by selecting the scale 2000 - 4000 mmHg.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10v; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

The pressure reading should be between 250 and 350 mmHg.

The graphic acquisition of the pressure can be made by selecting the scale 2000 - 4000 mmHg.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Visually inspect that whilst the engine is being started (key in START position) the turbocharger actuator connecting rod (turbine stator vane positioning device) moves by about 10 mm in the direction of the actuator.

CHECK USING EXAMINER

With the engine switched off, connect connector EX09 with transducer EX06 to the section of pipe between the actuator and the solenoid valve.

In the instrument ambient, select the intake pressure measurement pressure gauge.

Start the engine and let it idle, then wait until the pressure level stabilizes.

The reading must not exceed 450 - 550 mmHg.

The graphic acquisition of the pressure can be made by selecting the scale 2000 or 4000 mmHg.

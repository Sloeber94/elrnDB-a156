SPECIFCATIONS

OPERATION

The air chamber is made from aluminium and receives pressurized air, cooled by the intercooler and conveys it directly to the cylinder head.

The spiral inlet manifolds give the air the necessary (swirl) rotary motion for correct combustion.

The excess pressure sensor 1060G pressure pump electric control , connected to the engine management control unit, is fitted on the chamber.

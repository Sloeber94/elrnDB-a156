Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.0 JTS, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

The power steering system, supplied as standard on all versions, is driven by a pump which, apart from its lightness characteristics - the effort on the steering wheel is 4 Nm in steering from a standstill - offers very silent operation.

The system has also been designed to minimize hydraulic pulsing which can sometimes generate noise.

The setting has been chosen to give a balance between light steering from a standstill and a sensation of road grip when driving at speed.

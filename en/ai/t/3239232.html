The gauges and warning lights on the instrument panel are clustered into four different modules.

  • speedometer
  • revs counter
  • fault display
  • central module

The first three are located behind the steering wheel, the fourth in the middle of the facia

The modules fitted to different versions and engine versions differ only in the type of gauge scale.

Some warning lights may not be connected in certain specifications and versions.

All the instruments (dials and gauges) are lit up with the ignition switched ON.

In the 'night' conditions (side lights on), the brightness can be adjusted to three levels by an external button.

Some specifications come in a sport version with white dials, these are not illuminated when the ignition is switched on, only when the side lights are switched on.

SPEEDOMETER

The module includes

  • electronic speedometer
  • mileometer and trip counter
  • outdoor temperature gauge
  • warning lights

1, Electronic speedometer 2, Mileometer/outdoor temperature gauge 3, Water in diesel warning light 4, Seat belt warning light 5, Brake pad wear warning light 6, Glow plug warning light 7, Passenger Air Bag disabled warning light

The speedometer signal comes from the ABS control unit; there are three 'repeater' outputs of the signal for other systems; only two are used for the electronic fuel injection and climate control system.

The gauge display is produced by an electronic circuit inside the module.

The milometer consists of an LCD which simultaneously displays the total and trip mileage.

An external key is used to reset the trip counter and display outdoor temperature.

If the battery is disconnected, the trip meter is zeroed.

External temperature (in ° C) can be displayed instead of mileage by adopting the following procedure:

  • press the button for longer than 2 seconds to reset the trip counter
  • press the button for less than 2 seconds to display outdoor temperature
  • if outdoor temperature is lower than + 3 ° C, the system automatically switches to the temperature display, which flashes and shows an ice symbol to indicate the danger of an icy road surface. This message remains for 10 seconds and is repeated after 20 seconds.

This message can be cancelled by pressing the button and switching to the trip counter display.

The outdoor temperature level comes from the climate system control unit (see 5540 EXTERIOR LIGHTING )

Because an outdoor temperature signal is not produced (in versions with heater), the temperature is not shown on the outdoor temperature display.

Resetting mileage

The total mileage counter can be reset only once - during the first 200 km - following handling during production or prior to delivery to the Customer.

In the case of a new instrument, the mileage reading is preceded by an 'F' (e.g. 'F00199'). After 200 km, the letter F disappears automatically and the reading becomes '000201'.

If the reset button is pressed for longer than 10 seconds before 200 km, the letter H disappears and the counting restarts from '000000'. This operation can only be carried out once!

Warning lights

The following warning lights are present on the speedometer:

  • seat belt

The warning light comes on for about 15 seconds when the ignition is turned on, regardless of whether the seat belts are done up or not, in order to remind the driver of this requirement.

In a version available in some markets, the warning light goes off when the driver's seat belt has been done up.

- brake pad wear

When the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out

- presence of water in diesel filter (JTD only): the warning light comes on if the specific sensor detects the presence of water in the diesel filter.

- glow plug preheating (see 10 ENGINE )

- passenger air bag disablement (see 5580C air bag system )

Warning light test

The module internal electronic circuits also manage a warning light test function for all modules: upon start-up (key turned on), all the warning lights and leds are turned on for about 4 seconds to check they are working

In the case of the mileometer (LCD), all the lcd segments are turned on for about 4 seconds to check they are working.

Selespeed transmission speed display

The display in the rev counter indicates the gear engaged: N, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, R.

When the ignition is turned on, all display segments and the wording CITY are lit. The gear engaged is indicated after about one second.

Selespeed transmission buzzer

The buzzer on the back of the rev counter comes on for up to 20 seconds when:

  • reverse is engaged (R);
  • the clutch overheats (e.g. during setting off);
  • the car moves in the opposite direction to the speed selected (e.g. car moves forward with reverse engaged);
  • the engine is stopped - ignition turned off - with the transmission in neutral (N);
  • the lever is not released immediately after a gear change (manoeuvre extended for more than 10 seconds);
  • the system detects a fault. N.B. the failure warning light comes on at the same time;
  • the system switches automatically from a gear to neutral (N); this occurs when:
  • the engine does not start;
  • the pedals are not operated - accelerator or brake - for at least 50 seconds
  • the brake pedal is held down for longer than 10 minutes

the driver's door is opened and neither pedal is pressed

Selespeed failure warning light

This red warning light located in the rev counter comes on in the case of faults or critical driving conditions (e.g. clutch overheating).

When the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on with a fixed light for 4 seconds, then goes off to indicate that the SELESPEED system has begun to work correctly.

If the warning light flashes, a fault has been detected in the system.

The warning light comes on even when the lever is not released immediately after a gear change (manoeuvre extended for longer than 10 seconds): the warning light goes off when the lever is released.

When a door or lid is opened, the LED comes on together with the whole car symbol.

Warning lights

Air bag failure warning light

When the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on, controlled by the relevant control unit, for about 4 seconds as a self-test; if no faults are found, it goes out after that period (see 5580C air bag system )

The warning light also comes on in the case of a faulty connection or if the circuit connecting warning light and control unit is short circuited to earth. The warning light is 'smart', i.e. its inner control circuit checks that the connection is correct.

Abs failure warning light

When the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on, controlled by the ABS control unit, for about 2 seconds as a self-test; if no faults are found, it goes out after that period (see 3340A abs check/adjustment devices )

The warning light also comes on in the case of a faulty connection or if the circuit connecting warning light and control unit is short circuited to earth. The warning light is 'smart', i.e. its inner control circuit checks that the connection is correct.

Low brake fluid level and handbrake warning light

when the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on, controlled by the ABS control unit, for about 2 seconds as a self-test; if no faults are found, it goes out after that period.

- if it comes on, during driving, together with the ABS failure warning light, it indicates a fault in the EBD braking system (see 3340A abs check/adjustment devices ).

if, on the other hand, it comes on by itself, the brake fluid level is low or the handbrake is on.

Code failure warning light

- the warning light comes on upon a command from the Code control unit for 1 second when the ignition is turned, then it goes off to indicate that the key has been recognised and the system is efficient (see 5580E anti-theft device )

CENTRAL MODULE

The module includes:

  • fuel gauge, with reserve warning light
  • engine coolant temperature gauge, with maximum temperature warning light
  • analogue clock

1, Fuel level gauge 2, Reserve warning light 3, Analogue clock 4, Engine coolant temperature gauge 5, Maximum temperature warning light

The fuel level is displayed by an electronic circuit inside the module, which operates the gauge and also the warning light for the reserve (about 7 litres).

The internal software 'dampens' the signal to prevent fluctuations.

When the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out.

If it flashes and stays on, a fault has been detected in the connection or the level gauge.

The coolant temperature gauge is controlled directly by a sensor on the engine.

The maximum temperature warning light is controlled by a sensor on the engine with another signal, independent of the gauge.

During starting, when the ignition is turned on, the maximum temperature warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out.

The analogue clock consists of a face with hour and minute hands. It is electronic with a quartz oscillator. It is adjusted by means of a button inside the dial.

The engine oil temperature is acquired through an analogue input. To determine the value to be displayed, the average of 8 samples taken is calculated and then shown using 'bars' -: the value measured and the information displayed correspond in the following way:

  • 0 Bars: temperature between -40ºC and 60ºC
  • 1 Bar: temperature between 60ºC and 80ºC
  • 2 Bars: temperature between 80ºC and 100ºC
  • 3 Bars: temperature between 100ºC and 120ºC
  • 4 Bars: temperature between 120ºC and 140ºC
  • 5 Bars: temperature above 140ºC

Fault display

When a malfunction occurs, ideograms relating the fault/function diagnosed are displayed and, at the same time, there is a description of the fault and messages describing any action to be taken by driver or 'warning messages' (e.g. 'SWITCH OFF THE ENGINE, DO NOT CONTINUE', 'GO TO THE WORKSHOP', etc.).

There is also an intermittent acoustic warning lasting about 2 seconds (

When a fault occurs, the Infocenter is at the maximum brightness level. If the INFO button is pressed an icon appears in the special area and the entire display is lit up at the same brightness level as before the problem occurred.

All the other information is not displayed (date / outside temperature...)

The display of the faults follows a logic that depends on the priority (0, 1 or 2) assigned to it and, in any case, involves an acoustic signal.

Priority 0 fault: the screen is displayed in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for an indefinite period, interrupting any display shown previously.

The display cycle can be interrupted in two ways:

  • by removing the cause of the malfunction that produced the problem;
  • by the driver pressing the INFO button. In this case, only the symbol remains in the icon area and it stays there until the cause of the problem has been solved.

Priority 1 and 2 faults. They are displayed on the screen in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for 20 seconds and then disappears;

To prevent false fault signals at the Infocenter, a plausibility check is carried out at the actual Infocenter on the information received from the instrument panel.

In the case of simultaneous faults, the order of precedence for their display is priority 0 faults, then priority 1 faults and lastly priority 2 faults. The information corresponding to the various faults is displayed in rolling mode for 5 seconds at a time.

When a malfunction occurs, ideograms relating the fault/function diagnosed are displayed and, at the same time, there is a description of the fault and messages describing any action to be taken by driver or 'warning messages' (e.g. 'SWITCH OFF THE ENGINE, DO NOT CONTINUE', 'GO TO THE WORKSHOP', etc.).

When a fault occurs, the Infocenter is at the maximum brightness level. If the INFO button is pressed an icon appears in the special area and the entire display is lit up at the same brightness level as before the problem occurred.

All the other information is not displayed (date / outside temperature...)

The display of the faults follows a logic that depends on the priority (0, 1 or 2) assigned to it and, in any case, involves an acoustic signal.

Priority 0 fault: the screen is displayed in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for an indefinite period, interrupting any display shown previously.

The display cycle can be interrupted in two ways:

  • by removing the cause of the malfunction that produced the problem;
  • by the driver pressing the INFO button. In this case, only the symbol remains in the icon area and it stays there until the cause of the problem has been solved.

Priority 1 and 2 faults. They are displayed on the screen in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for 20 seconds and then disappears;

To prevent false fault signals at the Infocenter, a plausibility check is carried out at the actual Infocenter on the information received from the instrument panel.

In the case of simultaneous faults, the order of precedence for their display is priority 0 faults, then priority 1 faults and lastly priority 2 faults. The information corresponding to the various faults is displayed in rolling mode for 5 seconds at a time.

During starting, when the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out to show the system is working properly.

If the warning light begins to flash and stays on, the automatic transmission management system is faulty.

If the warning light stays on with a fixed light, or if it comes on with a fixed light during driving, the transmission fluid is overheating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: VDC

central module

The module includes

  • fuel gauge, with reserve warning light
  • engine coolant temperature gauge, with maximum temperature warning light
  • analogue clock

1 - Fuel level gauge 2 - Reserve warning light 3 - Analogue clock 4 - Engine coolant temperature gauge 5 - Maximum temperature warning light

The fuel level is displayed by an electronic circuit inside the module, which operates the gauge and also the warning light for the reserve (about 7 litres).

The internal software 'dampens' the signal to prevent fluctuations.

During starting, when the ignition is turned on, the warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out.

If it flashes and stays on, a fault has been detected in the connection or the level gauge.

Fuel refilling should not take place in the following two circumstances:Ignition key in ON position (during the refillng),Engine running (during the refilling)In addition to compromising legal SAFETY aspects, the instrument is not allowed to recognize the refilling condition.In these conditions it is made impossible for the gauge to indicate the actual level of fuel in the tank for periods of 20-30 minutes or more after the refilling.

The coolant temperature gauge is controlled directly by a sensor on the engine.

The maximum temperature warning light is controlled by a sensor on the engine with another signal, independent of the gauge.

During starting, when the ignition is turned on, the maximum temperature warning light comes on for about 4 seconds as a self-test, then goes out.

The analogue clock consists of a face with hour and minute hands. It is electronic with a quartz oscillator. It is adjusted by means of a button inside the dial.

Display at key-on

The Alfa logo is shown on the display for 2 seconds at the key-on.

The check stage then begins and is shown by the message 'CHECK IN PROGRESS'

On diesel versions, at the end of the Check stage, the 'oil level' information is displayed for 3 seconds.

When the check stage is over and the result is positive, the message 'CHECK OK' appears.

During the diagnostic cycle ('CHECK IN PROGRESS' screen) if the following is verified: 'Insufficient engine oil pressure' and/or 'Inertia switch operated' then there is an immediate display, whilst in the case of all other faults the complete display of the 'CHECK IN PROGRESS' must be waited for and only then will the display of the fault take place.

At the end of the entire display cycle at the key-on, the standard screen is proposed (last activated before the key-off). It remains displayed until a function that needs to be shown on the display is activated.

Display of engine oil level information (diesel versions)

The display of the oil level information at the key-on is achieved as follows: at the end of the Check stage the message 'ENGINE OIL LEVEL' is shown for 3 seconds at the top of the display and the amount of oil in the engine is shown at the bottom by six 'bars' lighting up/going off.

When all the 'bars' are on, the oil level is maximum.

The 'bars' gradually going off indicates that the oil level is decreasing.

If the oil level is below the minimum level, the 'bars' are all switched off. In this case the 'minimum oil level' message will be displayed. If the oil level is above the maximum value, all the 'bars' will be lit up. The message 'MAXIMUM OIL LEVEL' is displayed for 3 seconds.

Display of dimmed light adjustment function

The driver can adjust the brightness of the Infocenter display by pressing the INFO '+' and INFO '-' buttons. The brightness cannot be reduced to zero.

The 'Nightime' and 'Daytime' brightness can be adjusted: with the side lights on, the message 'Nightime Brightness Adjustment' is shown in the top part of the display and there is an indicator with bars that switch on/off at the bottom of the display.

If the side lights are not on, the message 'Daytime Brightness Adjustment' appears and there is an indicator with bars that switch on/off at the bottom of the display.

Display of the speed limit function

A speed limit can be set (with a range of between 30 and 250 km/h in 5 km/h steps); if this limit is exceeded, the symbol, the message 'SPEED LIMIT EXCEEDED' and the warning 'SLOW DOWN' will be displayed accompanied by an acoustic warning.

This procedure is only carried out once after the speed limit is exceeded and can only be repeated if the speed of the vehicle decreases to at least 5 km/h below the limit set and then increases again exceeding the limit once more.

Display of winter tyres function

The driver can use this function to set a speed limit if snow tyres are fitted on the vehicle. In this way it is possible to signal if the vehicle is travelling too fast at a speed that might adversely affect the performance of the tyres.

There is a choice of three speeds: (160, 190, 210 km/h).

If the speed set is exceeded, the message 'WINTER TYRE LIMIT EXCEEDED' is displayed with a warning message 'SLOW DOWN'.

Display of doors open function

The DOORS OPEN signal is activated as soon it is verified that one or more of the doors is open and it remains until all the doors are completely shut.

The screen comprises a symbol in the warning lights area, the message 'INCOMPLETE CLOSURE' and an indication of which door is open, e.g. 'LEFT FRONT DOOR'.

In the case of a BOOT OPEN problem, a screen with this specific message is displayed.

Information for the service function is displayed

The 'Service' function which signals the need to have the vehicle serviced when the distance or time parameters are approaching the recommended interval.

The values calculated are: distance 20,000 Km and days (365): the distance is provided by the instrument panel through the speedometer repeater and the time is calculated using the internal clock.

When the next service is nearly due, the message 'SERVICE DUE IN' is displayed at the key-on following by the number of kilometres or days until the next service is due.

The service information is supplied in 'km' (or miles) or in 'days' depending on which comes first.

Zeroing the 'Service' figures.

After having carried out the servicing operations for the specific interval, the distance and the service time can be zeroed (restored to 20,000 Km and 365 days). Proceed as follows: with the ignition ON and the vehicle stationary, keep the '+' and '-' buttons in the climate control system control panel pressed for more than 8 seconds.

Display of the trip computer function

The TRIP computer is a specific function that makes it possible to show certain information relating the operational state of the vehicle on the instrument panel display:

  • Distance travelled (km)
  • Instant consumtion and Average consumption (km/l or l/100km)
  • Range (km)
  • Average speed (km/h)
  • Journey time (hh:mm)

Each piece of information appears individually on the screen.

Each time the battery is reconnected, all the TRIP figures are zeroed.

The counting/updating of the TRIP information only takes place with the engine running, when the engine speed is above 300 rpm.

The zeroing through a manual resetting operation (steering column switch unit SET button) for one of the TRIP figures involves resetting all the others (with the exception of the RANGE and the INSTANT CONSUMPTION) after which each figure is then counted independently. Resetting the 'general' TRIP also resets the TRIP B, whilst resetting the TRIP B is independent.

The resetting takes place automatically:

  • when the 'journey distance' information reaches the limit equal to 9999.9 (km or miles)
  • when the journey time reaches the total limit of 99.59
  • each time afterthe battery is reconnected
  • of TRIP B 2 hours after key-off (NOTE: the TRIP B function can be excluded from the Setup Menu).

Engine oil temperature display (gta only)

The engine oil temperature is acquired through an analogue input. To determine the value to be displayed, the average of 8 samples taken is calculated and then shown using 'bars' -: the value measured and the information displayed correspond in the following way:

  • 0 Bars: temperature between -40ºC and 60ºC
  • 1 Bar: temperature between 60ºC and 80ºC
  • 2 Bars: temperature between 80ºC and 100ºC
  • 3 Bars: temperature between 100ºC and 120ºC
  • 4 Bars: temperature between 120ºC and 140ºC
  • 5 Bars: temperature above 140ºC

If the temperature exceeds 150ºC for 2 minutes, then the fault message 'OIL OVERHEATING' is displayed plus the warning 'REDUCE DEMANDS'

Fault display

When a malfunction occurs, ideograms relating the fault/function diagnosed are displayed and, at the same time, there is a description of the fault and messages describing any action to be taken by driver or 'warning messages' (e.g. 'SWITCH OFF THE ENGINE, DO NOT CONTINUE', 'GO TO THE WORKSHOP', etc.).

There is also an intermittent acoustic warning lasting about 2 seconds (

When a fault occurs, the Infocenter is at the maximum brightness level. If the INFO button is pressed an icon appears in the special area and the entire display is lit up at the same brightness level as before the problem occurred.

All the other information is not displayed (date / outside temperature...)

The display of the faults follows a logic that depends on the priority (0, 1 or 2) assigned to it and, in any case, involves an acoustic signal.

Priority 0 fault: the screen is displayed in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for an indefinite period, interrupting any display shown previously.

The display cycle can be interrupted in two ways:

  • by removing the cause of the malfunction that produced the problem;
  • by the driver pressing the INFO button. In this case, only the symbol remains in the icon area and it stays there until the cause of the problem has been solved.

Priority 1 and 2 faults. They are displayed on the screen in accordance with the following logic:

  • fault message (top left);
  • warning message (under the previous message);
  • fault symbol (in the designated area)

This screen remains for 20 seconds and then disappears;

To prevent false fault signals at the Infocenter, a plausibility check is carried out at the actual Infocenter on the information received from the instrument panel.

In the case of simultaneous faults, the order of precedence for their display is priority 0 faults, then priority 1 faults and lastly priority 2 faults. The information corresponding to the various faults is displayed in rolling mode for 5 seconds at a time.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

The car earth points are subdivided according to the wiring to which they are connected, as follows:

  • battery negative wiring C1 - C10 C ;
  • crossmember wiring C11 ;
  • front wiring C10 A -C10 - BC15 ;
  • engine wiring (petrol version) C40 A - C40 B - C40 C ;
  • dashboard wiring C20A - C50 (up to 06/02);
  • rear wiring C20B - C30 - C31- C50 (from 06/02);
  • rearscreen negative wiring (saloon) C32 ;
  • ABS wiring C1 B - C11 B - C12 ;
  • air conditioner wiring C16 .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Alarm

Functional description

The circuit is controlled by the hazard warning lights switch H20, incorporated in the climate control system controls M70 .

The intermittent device is located on the steering column switch unit H5. It is connected to earth at pin 7 of connector A and receives a supply signal at pin 7 of connector D through switch H20:

  • when the hazard lights are not on, the supply is controlled by the ignition (INT/A) from fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B.
  • when the hazard warning lights are switched on, the supply is direct from the battery through fuse F9 of the junction unit B1 and maxifuse J/B1of fuse B99.

The intermittent device produces a signal which supplies the switch for the direction indicators on the steering column switch H5 and the switch H20 .

The operation, in one direction or another, of the steering column switch lever H5 supplies the right direction indicators ( F11 , F21 , F31 ) or the left ones ( F10 , F20 , F30 ).

At the same time the warning light, located in the rev counter module E15 , comes on.

Pressing the switch H20 closes three contacts which simultaneously supply all the lights (right branch and left branch) close and the red LED in the actual switch which lights up the symbol.

All the hazard warning lights are also operated by the alarm system control unit M30 (pins 1 and 2 of connector B), which is connected directly to the light power supply See E7015 ALARM .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Functional description

The switch for the reversing lights I20 is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT) by the line for fuse F1 for the junction unit B1 .

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and sends the supply to the reversing lights in the centre rear light cluster on the left F35 and on the right F36 , via the junction unit B1 (pin 9 connector D and pins 6 and 7 of connector G).

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Q-System and Station Wagon

Functional description

Reversing light switch I20 is not present. Pins 8 and 9 of the automatic transmission solenoids unit L50 , connector A, are connected to the reverse gear sensor and send the signals for switching on the reversing light in the same way as for other versions.

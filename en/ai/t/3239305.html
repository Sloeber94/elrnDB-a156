Functional description

The timed supply for the front ( G10 ) and rear ( G25 and G26 ) courtesy lights arrives from the integrated services control unit M40 , pin 16 of connector C.

Control unit M40 is supplied by an ignition-operated supply (INT) at pin 7 of connector B from the line protected by fuse F4 of the supplementary fuse box B98B and directly by the battery. (maxifuse J/B2 of B99 ) via fuse F8 of the junction unit B1 (pin 8 of connector B).

The control unit sends a timed signal according to the door unlocking and door open signals which arrive:

  • at pin 15 of connector C: unlocking from the receiver for the remote control M35 E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING.
  • at pin 16 of connector B: unlocking of the lock of one of the front doors N50 and N51 E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING.
  • at pins 2 and 3 of connector B: opening of one of the front doors N50 and N51 (left and right respectively);
  • at pins 7 and 13 of connector C: opening of one of the rear doors N55 and N56 (left and right respectively);

The courtesy lights are then switched on via a direct earth signal (permanently activated) or a signal from the integrated services control unit M40 , from pins 1 and 3 of connector C .

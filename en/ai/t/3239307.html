Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Sedan

Functional description

The boot opening switch I11 sends an earth signal, with the boot open, to integrated services control unit M40. (pin 14 of connector C) and simultaneously to boot light G40 .

The supply for the control unit M40 is controlled by the ignition (INT) by the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 (pin 7 of connector B) and directly from the battery. (maxifuse J/B2 of B5 ) up to 2/99 or of B99 from 2/99.) through fuse F8 of junction unit B1 (pin 8 of connector B).

When the signal that the boot is open arrives, the control unit M40 sends the timed supply signal, from pin 2 of connector C, which operates the boot light G40 .

The supply for the glove compartment light G30 is controlled by the ignition (INT/A) by the line for fuse B10 up to 2/99 or fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99, and is activated by an earth signal from the glove compartment switch I15 .

The supply for the sun visor light G32 is controlled by the ignition (INT) by the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 and is activated by the switch inside the actual light.

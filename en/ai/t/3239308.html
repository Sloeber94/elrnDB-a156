Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Functional description

The boot opening switch I11 sends an earth signal, with the boot open, to the integrated services control unit M40 (pin 14 of connector C) and, at the same time, to the boot light G40 and the light on the tail-gate G41. .

The supply for the control unit M40 is controlled by the ignition (INT) by the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 (pin 7 of connector B) and directly from the battery (maxifuse J/B2 of B99 ) via fuse F8 of junction unit B1 (pin 8 of connector B).

When the signal that the boot is open arrives, the control unit M40 sends the timed supply signal, from pin 2 of connector C, which operates the boot light G40 and tail-gate light G41 .

The supply for the glove compartment light G30 ignition-operated (INT/A) from the line protected by fuse F6 of additional fuse box B98 , and is activated by an earth signal from the glove compartment switch I15 .

Sun visor lights G31 and G32 are controlled by the ignition (INT) by the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 and is activated by the switch inside the actual light.

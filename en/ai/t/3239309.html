Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Functional description

The boot opening switch I11 sends an earth signal, with the boot open, to integrated services control unit M40. (pin 14 of connector C) and simultaneously to boot light G40 and to tailgate light G41 .

Control unit M40 is supplied by an ignition-operated supply (INT) - pin 7 of connector B from the line protected by fuse F4 of supplementary fuse box B98B and directly by the battery. (maxifuse J/B2 of B99 ) via fuse F8 of the junction unit B1 (pin 8 of connector B).

When the signal that the boot is open arrives, the control unit M40 sends the timed supply signal, from pin 2 of connector C, which operates the lights G40 and G41 .

The supply for the glove compartment light G30 ignition-operated (INT/A) from the line protected by fuse F6 of additional fuse box B98B , and is activated by an earth signal from the glove compartment switch I15 .

Sun visor lights G31 and G32 are controlled by the ignition (INT) by the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 and are activated by the switch inside the actual lights.

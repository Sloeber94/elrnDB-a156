Functional description

The supply for the speedometer E10 (pin 10 of connector A) is controlled by the ignition (INT) by fuse line F4 of the additional fuse box B98B .

Similarly, the rev counter E15 (at pin 15 of connector A) and the centre instrument E20 (pin 1.).

An earth signal from instrument light dimmer control H10 reaches pin 6 of connector B of E10 . This also receives a side light activated signal, at pin 5 of connector B E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS .

According to the logic processed by the internal electronic device, the speedometer E10 sends the lighting signal to the other instruments: to the centre instrument E20 (pin 7) from pin 12 of connector A; and to the rev counter E15 (3 of connector B) from pin 4 of connector B.

The switch H10 has ideograms lit up when the side lights are switched on through the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 .

The zeroing signal for the trip meter reaches pin 12 of connector B of E10 located in H10 . E4021 SPEEDOMETER

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02

Functional description

The horn system is supplied through the horn relay R2 , located in the junction unit B1 from the direct line through the fuse F13 - always for B1 - and the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 up to 2/99 or B99 from 2/99.

The coil of the relay R2 is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed; these switches are connected via the clock spring to the stalk unit H5 . The relay thereby supplies the high tone horn P10 and the low tone horn P11 .

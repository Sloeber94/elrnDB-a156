Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay R2 , located in the junction unit B1 from the direct line from the battery through the fuse F13 - always for B1 - and the maxifuse J/B1 for B99 .

The relay coil R2 is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed; these switches are connected via the clock spring D47. The relay supplies the high tone horn P10 and the low tone horn P11.

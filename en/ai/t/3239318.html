Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Functional description

The special switch for the steering column switch unit H5 controls the operation of the rearscreen wash/wipe.

The stalk unit H5 is supplied at pin 3 of connector D from the ignition-operated line (INT/A) of the relay R1 protected by the fuse F14 : both are contained in the junction unit B1 .

When operated, the rearscreen wash/wipe switch for the steering column switch unit H5, sends control signals from connector D: from pin 1 to the rearscreen wiper motor N16 ; from pin 2 to the rearscreen wiper control unit M79 , pin B7 .

Control unit M79 controls system operation via the sequence described previously, via a signal from pin C6 for rear wiper motor N16 .

When operated, the windscreen washer switch on the steering column switch unit H5 sends two signals from pin 8 and pin 9 of connector A, the first to windscreen wiper control unit M79 (pin B4), the second is directly connected to pump N22 .

The motor is operated upon a command from winscreen wiper control unit M79 , pin C2, in the opposite manner to that described for the washer function (when the motor is operated in reverse, it sends fluid to the back instead of to the front).

The control unit also receives a signal - pin A5 - from reversing sensor I20E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS to operate the rear wiper automatically.

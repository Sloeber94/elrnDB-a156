Functional description

The heated rear windscreen relay R3 , located in the junction unit B1 , controls the simultaneous activation of the demisting of both the rear windscreen P55 and the external rear view mirrors P60 and P61 .

The relay R3 is supplied by the direct line from the battery through the fuse F11 - always for B1 - and the maxifuse J/B1 for B99

The coil for relay R3 energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98 .

An earth signal coming from the integrated services control unit M40 (pin 9 of connector B) energizes the coil of the relay R1 , with the supply consequently sent to resistors P55 , P60 and P61 . (the control circuit of the heated rear window P55 passes through the aerial P22 control unit. ( E3510 RADIO )

The supply circuit of the heated rear window P55 passes through a special interference filter in versions with predisposition for a radio.

The control unit M40 is supplied when the ignition (INT) is switched on from the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 (pin 7 of connector B).

When an earth command is received (pin 14 of connector B) coming from the climate control unit M70 (heated rear windscreen on button pressed), according to the control logic described previously, a command is sent which energizes the coil o f relay R3 (pin 9 of connector B).

At the same time as the activation of the resistors, a signal is sent (from pin 11, of connector B of M40 ) to light up the LED in the control button which is located in the climate control unit M70 .

- Version with Heater. When an earth command is received (pin 14 of connector B) coming from the heater H80 (heated rear windscreen on button pressed), according to the control logic described previously, a command is sent which energizes the coil of the relay R3 (pin 9 of connector B). At the same time as the activation of the resistors, a signal is sent (from pin 11, of connector B of M40 ) to light up the LED in the control button which is located in H80 .

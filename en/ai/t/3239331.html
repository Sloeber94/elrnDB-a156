Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

The two-way switch controlling mirrors H30 operates electrical motors located in the right and left electrically-operated door mirrors P60 and P61 respectively.

The switch is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) at pin 7 of H30 , by the line for fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B ; Pin 1 is earthed.

Operating the switch in one direction or the other, sends one of the two motors a positive and an earth signal, thereby determining the direction of rotation. The motors which adjust the vertical position are connected to pin 5 of the mirrors P60 and P61 , the motors which adjust the horizontal position are connected to pin 3. According to the selector position, either the left motors (output signals from pins 4 and 9 of H30 ) or the right motors (output signals from pins 12 and 2 of H30 ) are connected; the common supply comes out of pin 8 and is connected to pin 5 of the motors.

The switch H30 is lit up when the side lights are switched on by means of the line coming from the stalk unit switch H5 (pin 5).

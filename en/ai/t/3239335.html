Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The integrated services control unit M40 which manages the operation of the central locking.

The direct supply for the control unit M40 (pin 8 of connector B) arrives directly from the battery via the line protect by maxifuse J/B2 (of B99) and fuse F8 of the junction unit B1.; pin 13 of connector B provides the reference earth.

The receiver of the remote control M35 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 and directly from the battery via the line of the maxifuse J/B1 (of B99) and fuse F10 of the junction unit B1.

The control unit M40 receives the following inputs:

  • commands coming from the front locks N50 and N51 for locking (pin 15 of connector B) or for unlocking (pin 16 of connector B) the doors;
  • signals on the state of the door locks coming from the four geared motors N50, N51, N55 and N56, respectively at pins 3 and 2 of connector B and 3 and 7 of connector C. See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS .
  • ( only with remote control) the signals from the remote control M35 receiver for locking (pin 6 of connector C) or for unlocking (pin 15 of connector C);

In accordance with the strategy described above, the control unit M40 controls the locks: the signals for the motors of the front doors (N50 and N51) and rear doors (N55 ed N56) come from pins 4 and 5 of connector B.

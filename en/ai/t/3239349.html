Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the circuit for the rear electric windows (as well as the sun roof and heated seats: see E3060 SUN ROOF e E3075 HEATED SEATS ). .

The coil of the relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse B10 up to 2/99 or fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99, while the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 (of B5 up to 2/99 or B99 from 2/99).

This supply passes through the rear electric window fuses B17 and B18 up to 2/99 or F2 and F3 of B98B from 2/99, which guarantee the protection of the circuit, and arrives at the switches on the driver's door ( H45 left and H46 right). The supply of the switches is therefore controlled by the ignition (INT/A) and they are connected to earth C20 (in this way they are also lit up when the ignition is switched on).

When these switches are operated, they send command signals (positive and negative) to the switches in the rear doors and from there to the (left) N65 and right N66 motors: reversing the signals according to the contact which is closed determines the direction of rotation of the actual motor.

The same supply controlled by the ignition (INT/A) also reaches the switches in the rear doors H53 and H54 , passing through the safety switch H55 , but only if it is in the go ahead position. If this is not the case, the supply to the rear door switches is stopped.

As with the front switches, when the rear switches are activated, they send command signals (positive and negative) to the (left) N65 and (right) N66 motors: reversing the signals according to the contact which has been closed, determines the direction of rotation of the actual motor.

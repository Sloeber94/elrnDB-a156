Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Sedan

Functional description

The power socket / cigarette lighter P50 is supplied (at pin 2) by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the relay R1 protected by the fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

Pin 3 of socket P50 provides the earth from C20 .

The ring nut for the power socket is lit up (at pin 1) when the side lights are on by means of the line coming from the steering column switch unit H5 switch and is protected by the controls illumination fuse B11 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse unit B98B from 2/99.

The same supply line with the side lights on also lights up the ashtray light G35 .

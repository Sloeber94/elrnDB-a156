Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the heating circuit for the seats as well as the electric rear windows and sun roof: See E3060 SUN ROOF , See E3051 ELECTRIC REAR WINDOWS .

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01

Functional description

The coil for relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse B10, whilst the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B5.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The coil of the relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B, while the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B99.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01

Functional description

By pressing the switch on the seat the resistors for the heaters in the left and right seats, 040 and 041, connected in parallel, are supplied, until a thermal contact inside the heaters disconnects the resistors when the temperature exceeds a certain limit.

This supply passes through the heated seats fuse B20 , which guarantees the protection of the circuit and reaches the switches in the seats through connection D70 (left seat) and D71 (right seat): the supply for the two devices is therefore controlled by the ignition (INT/A) and they are connected to earth C20 .

When the resistors are on, an LED signals the operation of the device.

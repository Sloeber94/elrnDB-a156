Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the heating circuit for the seats as well as the electric rear windows and sun roof E3060 SUN ROOF ,

The coil for relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse F6 of supplementary fuse box B98 B, whilst the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B99.

This supply passes through fuse F5 of supplementary fuse box B98 C , which guarantees the protection of the circuit and reaches the switches in the seats through connection D70 (left seat) and D71 (right seat): the supply for the two devices is therefore controlled by the ignition (INT/A) and they are connected to earth.

By pressing the switch on the seat the resistors for the heaters in the left and right seats, 040 and 041, connected in parallel, are supplied, until a thermal contact inside the heaters disconnects the resistors when the temperature exceeds a certain limit.

When the resistors are on, an LED signals the operation of the device.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Air Bag laterale

Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the heating circuit for the seats as well as the electric rear windows and sun roof: See E3060 SUN ROOF , See E3051 ELECTRIC REAR WINDOWS , See E3075 HEATED SEATS ).

The coil for relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse F6 of supplementary fuse box B98 B, whilst the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B99.

This supply passes through fuse F5 of supplementary fuse box B98 C which guarantees the protection of the circuit and reaches the switch on the seat via the connection D70: the supply for the device is therefore controlled by the ignition (INT/A); and is also connected to earth C20 .

When the switch on the seat is pressed, command signals (positive and negative) are sent to the motor N38 : reversing the signals according to the contact which is closed determines the direction of the rotation of the actual motor.

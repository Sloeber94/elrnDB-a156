Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the heating circuit for the seats as well as the electric rear windows and sun roof E3060 SUN ROOF .

The coil for relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse F6 of supplementary fuse box B98 B, whilst the supply at the relay contact arrives from the battery, through the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B99.

This supply passes through fuse F5 of supplementary fuse box B98 C which guarantees the protection of the circuit and reaches the switch on the seat via the connections D70B and D71B : the supply for the device is therefore controlled by the ignition (INT/A); and is also connected to earth.

When the switch on the seat is pressed, command signals (positive and negative) are sent to the motors N32 (driver's side) and N33 (passenger side): reversing the signals according to the contact which is closed determines the direction of the rotation of the actual motor.

Functional description

The headlamp alignment device in H10 is supplied at pin 3 by a line coming from the circuit for the dipped beam headlamps, protected by fuse F5 of the junction unit B1 ; this line only supplies voltage to the regulator when the dipped beam headlamps are switched on. E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS this line provides voltage to the regulator only when the dipped beams are on.

Pin 4 of device H10 is earthed, whilst pin 2 sends the signal to adjust the motors N30 (left) and N31 (right), through a resistive divider which alters the output voltage depending on which of the four positions has been selected.

Motors N30 and N31 contain a transducer that establishes actual motor travel on the basis of the voltage of an adjustment signal received by pin 2 from device H10 . The motors are supplied through pins 3 from the line (part of the dipped beam circuit) that supplies device H10 , while pins 1 are earthed.

The headlamp aglinment device H10 is lit up (at pin 1) when the side lights are switched on by means of the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 and is protected by fuse F1 of the additional fuse box B98B .

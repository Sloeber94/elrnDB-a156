Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Functional description

The two electronics inside the headlamps F10A (left) and F11A (right) are supplied - pin 6 - by the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B ; pin 14 of F10A and F11A are earthed.

Pin 8 of F10A and F11A are connected to one another (serial communication line between MASTER and SLAVE), whilst pin 13 of the control unit located in headlamp F10A receives the speedometer signal from instrument E10 .

Headlamp F10A incorporates the headlamp alignment function: the two sensors (potentiometers) that record ride at the front K1 and the rear K2 are connected: a supply and reference earth are sent from pins 9 and 10 of F10A : the signals proportional to the load return to pins 11 and 12; according to these signals the headlamp electronics automatically adjust the light beam by means of a special motor. The left headlamp motor is adjusted directly, whilst the right one is adjusted via the serial line between F10A and F11A .

The dipped headlamps control signal reaches F10A and F11A at pin 1 E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS .

The diagnostic line for connector R10, pin 12, leaves pin 7 of F10A and F11A.

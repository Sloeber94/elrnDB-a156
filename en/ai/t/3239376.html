Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Functional description

The radio P20 is supplied directly (pins 4 and 7 of connector A) by the battery via the line for fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 and the maxifuse J/B1 of B99 .

It receives a supply when the ignition is switched on (INT) (pin 3 of connector A) via the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 .

With the side lights switched on, the supply reaches pin 6 of connector A by means of the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 , protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B ; the radio controls are therefore lit up when the side lights are switched on.

Pin 8 of connector A, on the other hand, is earthed ( C20 ).

Pin 5 of connector A is connected to the control unit P22 which controls the aerial built into the spoiler. The control unit P22 is then connected to the radio by means of the specific aerial lead.

Connector B sends out cables carrying signals to the speakers:

  • pins 5 and 6 to the left front speakers P35 and P45 ;
  • pins 3 and 4 to the right front loudspeakers P36 and P46 ;
  • pins 7 and 8 to the left rear speakers P30 and P40 , and to the subwoofer P92 ;
  • pins 1 and 2 to the right rear speakers P31 and P41 , and to the subwoofer P92 ;

The subwoofer P92 has its own supply which comes from the line protected by the fuse F8 of the junction unit B1 , and is connected to pin 5 of connector A of P20 .

The cable connected to the CD player P75 comes out of connector C of P20 .

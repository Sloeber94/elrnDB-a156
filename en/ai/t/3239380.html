Functional description

The radionavigator P20 is supplied directly (pin 4 of connector A) by the battery via the line for fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 and the maxifuse J/B1 of B99 .

It receives a supply when the ignition is switched on (INT) (pin 7 of connector A) via the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 .

With the side lights switched on, the supply reaches pin 6 of connector A by means of the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 , protected by the controls illumination fuse F1 of the additional fuse box B98 B ; the radionavigator controls are therefore lit up when the lide lights are switched on.

Pin 8 of connector A, on the other hand, is earthed ( C20 ).

Pin 5 of connector A is connected to the control unit P22 which controls the aerial built into the heated rear windscreen. The control unit P22 is then connected to the radio by means of the specific aerial lead.

On the version with a NAVIGATOR, an interface P132 connecting the radio navigator and the BOSE amplifier P70 is required.

The amplifier P70 is supplied directly by the battery via the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 C .

It is then connected by a special line to the radio P20 - pin 6 of connector C - which sends the signal to switch on the amplifier.

The interface P132 also receives the amplifier on signal - at pin 6 - from pin 6 of connector C; pin 7 of connector B is earthed.

Pins 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 11 and 12 send out signals to the 'Bose' HIFI system amplifier P70 which, in turn, is connected to the speakers: P35 , P40 , P41 and P36 . Control signals from connector C of P20 reach pins 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5; these signal are together in a suitably screened five-pole cable.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: BOSE System and Navigator and Sedan

Functional description

The rear woofer speaker P92 is outside the amplifier located in the rear parcel shelf.

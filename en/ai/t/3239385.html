Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The connection D75 receives a supply directly (pin 6) from the battery via the line for fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 and maxifuse J/B1 of B5 up to 2/99 or of B99 from 2/99.

It receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) (pin 8) via the line for fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 .

The supply with the side lights switched on reaches pin 7 via the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 and protected by the controls illumination fuse B11 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99

Pin 5 of connection D75 is earthed, while pins 9 and 10 supply the signal to the loudspeaker P35 , connector B, while connector A is for the radio ( E3510 RADIO ).

Lastly, pin 1 is connected to the radio P20 (MUTE function).

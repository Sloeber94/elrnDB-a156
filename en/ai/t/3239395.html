Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

The supply for the rev counter module E15 reaches pins 10 and 16 of connector A directly from the battery. (maxifuse J/B2 of B99 ) via fuses F2 and F3 of junction unit B1 ; this supply is used by the check device for the exterior lights.

Rev counter module E15 also receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 15 of connector A from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B . Pin 14 of connector A is earthed.

The engine rpm signal, which is different for each engine type, comes from the engine management control unit M10 to pin 6 of connector B.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 10v, 2.4 JTD 20v; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

The water in diesel filter sensor K31 is supplied by the line protected by fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98 A , and sends and earth signal to pin 7 of connector B of the speedometer E10 when a certain level of humidity is detected in the diesel filter.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

The supply to the speedometer module E10 reaches pin 9 of connector A directly from the battery (maxifuse J/B1of B5 ), via fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 ; the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 10 of connector A from the line protected by fuse B11 .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The supply to the speedometer module E10 reaches pin 9 of connector A directly from the battery (maxifuse J/B1of B99 ), via fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 ; the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 10 of connector A from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B .

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

Pin 1 of connector A is earthed.

The ABS control unit M50 sends the speedometer signal to pin 11 of connector A of E10 .

The speedometer signal is sent again from pin 15 of connector A to the engine control unit M10 , which differs for the various versions and from pin 16 of connector A towards the climate control unit M70 .

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Smart gearbox and Right hand drive

Functional description

The same signal is sent from pin 14 of connector A to the automatic transmission control unit M54 .

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

Pin 7 of connector A of E10 receives the signal for the outside temperature from the climate control unit M70 .

The zeroing signal for the trip meter reaches pin 12 of connector B from H10 .

The switch H10 has ideograms lit up when the side lights are switched on through the line coming from the switch for the steering column switch unit H5 . E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING .

The earth signals which provide information on the state of wear of the brake pads, coming from sensors K20 and K21 reach pin 11 of connector B.

On a version available on some markets, pin 5 of connector B of E10 receives the earth signal from the switch l45 , located on the driver's seat anchorage, which indicates that the belt is fastened.

For all the other warning lights, refer to the diagrams relating to the components to which they refer.

Functional description

The water in diesel filter sensor K31 is supplied by the line of maxifuse EFI of B99 , and sends an earth signal to pin 13 of connector A of the speedometer module E10 when a certain level of humidity is detected in the diesel filter.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The supply for the check module E30 reaches pin 2 directly from the battery. (maxifuse J/B1 of B5 up to 2/99 or of B99 from 2/99 ) through fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 ; the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 1from the line protected by fuse B11 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99. Pin 3 is earthed.

Pins 8, 9, 11 and 10 of instrument E20 are connected to the switches at the locks of the front doors (connector C of N50 and N51 ) and the rear doors (connector B of N55 and N56 ), respectively and signal if any of the doors is open.

The boot opening switch I11 is connected to pin 12.

The signals for the handbrake switch I40 and brake fluid level sensor K25 both reach pin 13 of E30 .

For all the other warning lights, refer to the diagrams relating to the components to which they refer.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02, da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

The supply for the centre module E20 arrives directly from the battery. (maxifuse J/B1 of B99 ) to pin 2 via fuse F10 of junction unit B1 ; the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 1 of the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B .

Pin 3 is earthed.

The standard engine temperature sender unit K36 sends a signal proportional to the temperature of the engine coolant (pin 1) to pin 10 of E20 . The minimum temperature signal, on the other hand, arrives at pin 9 of E20 from the engine control unit M10 .

Pins 4 and 5 of the centre instrument E20 receive signals (positive and negative) coming from the fuel gauge N40 located in the fuel pump unit.

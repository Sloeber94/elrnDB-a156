Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The supply for module E63 arrives directly from the battery. (maxifuse J/B1 of B99 ) to pin 1 via fuse F10 of junction unit B1 ; the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 3 of the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B .

Pin 11 is earthed.

The control buttons, incorporated in the climate control system M70 controls, are connected to pins 16, 19 and 20. A serial line also connects module E63 - from pin 13- to the climate control system M70 .

The SET button, located in the steering column switch unit ( H5 connector E) is connected to pin 15.

Pins 4, 8, 5 and 9 of instrument E63 are connected to the switches at the locks of the front doors (connector C of N50 and N51 ) and the rear doors (connector B of N55 and N56 ), respectively and signal if any of the doors is open. Similarly, the opening of the boot is signalled by switch I11 to pin 6.

The signal indicating the operation of the inertia switch I50 reaches pin 10 of E63 .

A serial line connects module E63, from pin 7, with speedometer module E10 See E4021 SPEEDOMETER with this line used to send information concerning the warning lights (for the repetition of the signal at the actual INFOCENTER) for the engine oil level, speedometer signal, etc.

The engine temperature signal is sent again at pin 17 to module E20E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

The engine oil pressure signal is sent again at pin 18 to module E30E4022 CENTRE INSTRUMENT (CHECK) .

Module E63 has its own diagnostic serial line - from pins 2 and 12 - which is connected to a specific diagnostic socket, R57 .

Functional description

The oil temperature sensor K29 provides a signal at pin 14 of E63 for display purposes.

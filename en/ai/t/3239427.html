Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

When the ignition key for the switch H1 is turned to the extreme position (AVV) [START], the solenoid winding (pin 50) of the starter motor A20 is supplied.

This supply comes from the battery via the line of maxifuse IGN of B99 .

Pin 30 of A20 , the actual starter motor, receives the voltage coming directly from the battery A1 .

The direct current generated by the alternator A10 (pin B+) is sent to the battery A1 through motor A20 . When the alternator is not turning and therefore not recharging the battery, an earth signal is sent from pin D+ to check module E30, pin 7.

Module E15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 13 from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B: when this signal arrives, the 'recharging' warning light comes on.

When the engine has been started up this signal becomes 12 V and the warning light goes out.

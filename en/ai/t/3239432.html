Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00; only with equipment: Heater

Functional description

The supply for the fan N11 arrives directly from the battery from the line for the VENT maxifuse of B6 up to 2/99 or FAN 1 of B99 from 2/99.

The fan thus operates when it receives a control earth.

The relay J30 receives a supply at its coil from the line controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuse B25 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

It is energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10 .

If the engine coolant reaches a high temperature, the control unit M10 sends an earth signal to the relay J30 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth which operates the fan N11 .

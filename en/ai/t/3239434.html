Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS ; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Functional description

The supply for the fan N11 arrives directly from the battery from the line for the VENT maxifuse of B6 : the fan therefore works when it receives an earth command.

The relays J30 and J31 , specific to the cooling system, receive a supply at their coils from the line controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuse B25 .

They are energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10 .

If the engine coolant reaches the first temperature stage, or the 4 stage pressure switch K10 (pin 3) signals to the control unit M10 (pin 44) that the first pressure stage has been reached, then the control unit M10 sends an earth signal (from pin 26) to the 1st speed fan relay J30 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal which, via the additional resistor O10 , operates the fan N11 which rotates at the first (slow) speed.

If the engine coolant reaches a higher temperature or the 4-stage pressure switch K10 (pin 6) signals to the control unit M10 (pin 43) that a higher pressure stage has been reached, the control unit M10 sends (from pin 25) an earth signal to the 2nd speed fan relay J31 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal directly to the fan N11 which rotates at the maximum speed.

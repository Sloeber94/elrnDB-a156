Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00; only with equipment: Heater

Functional description

The supply for fan N11 arrives directly from the battery from the line for the VENT maxifuse of B6 up to 2/99 or FAN 1 of B99 from 2/99: the fan therefore works when it receives an earth command.

The relays J30 and J31 , specific to the cooling system, receive a supply at their coils controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuse B25 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

They are energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10 .

If the engine coolant reaches a first temperature level, the control unit M10 sends (from pin 23 of connector B) an earth signal to the fan 1st speed relay J30 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal which, via the additional resistor O10 , operates the fan N11 which rotates at the first (slow) speed.

If the engine coolant reaches a higher temperature, the control unit M10 sends (from 45 of connector C) an earth signal to the fan 2nd speed relay J31 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal directly to the fan N11 which rotates at the maximum speed.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Functional description

The supply for the two fans N11 and N12 arrives directly from the battery from the lines of the two maxifuses FAN1 and FAN2 of B99 : the fans operate when they receive an earth command.

The relays J30 , J31 and J32 , specific to the cooling system, receive supply to their coils from the direct line (EFI) protected by fuse F5 of the additional fuse box B98A .

They are energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10 .

If the coolant temperature reaches the first temperature stage, or the 4-stage pressure switch K10 (pin 3) indicates to control unit M10 - pin 42 of connector A - that the first pressure stage has been reached, control unit M10 sends an earth signal to 1st speed relay J30 from pin 90 of connector A, which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth which, via the additional resistors O10 and O12 , operates both fans N11 and N12 which rotate at the first (slow) speed.

If the engine coolant reaches a higher temperature level, or the 4 stage pressure switch K10 (pin 6) signals to the control unit M10 - pin 81 of connector A - that a higher pressure level has been reached, then the control unit M10 sends an earth signal, pin 69 of connector A, to the two 2nd speed fan relays J31 and J32 which energize the coils. The two relays then send an earth directly to the two fans N11 and N12 which rotate at maximum speed.

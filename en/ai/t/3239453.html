Applies to: engines: 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

The engine control unit M10 monitors and controls the entire electronic ignition and injection system.

The direct supply for the system arrives from the battery from the line for the maxifuse EFI of B5 up to 2/99 or from B99 from 2/99.

The control unit M10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 18 of connector A, via the line protected by fuse B35 up to 2/99 or from fuse F5 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) reaches pin 8 of connector A, via the line protected by fuse B25 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

Main injection relay J10 controls the entire system: this is excited by a control signal (earth) received from pin 31 of connector A of control unit M10 and then sends power:

  • to pins 7 and 17 of connector A of the control unit, via the line protected by the fuse B41 up to 2/99 or fuse F3 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99;
  • to injectors N70 , to air flow meter K41 , to fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10 , to variable geometry control solenoid L15 , to variable valve timing actuator N76 and Lambda probe K40 ; (all these lines are protected by fuse B40 up to 2/99 or fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99).

The fuel pump relay J15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition via fuse B25 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

It is energized by a control signal (earth) from pin 3 of connector A of control unit M10 and supplies power to fuel pump N40 , which is earthed through the inertia switch I50 , which cuts off the circuit in the case of a crash and so prevents the dangerous spilling of fuel.

It also supplies power to the ignition coils A30 .

The engine control unit M10 receives signals from the various sensors in order to monitor all engine operating parameters.

Rpm sensor K46 provides information on engine speed via a frequency signal sent to pins 22 and 32 of connector B of control unit M10 . These two signals are very low intensity and are therefore shielded.

Timing sensor K47 is supplied from pin 29 of connector B of control unit M10 and sends a signal of corresponding frequency to pin 23 of control unit connector B.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 TS; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

Engine temperature sensor K45 receives a reference earth from pin 1 of connector B of control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the coolant temperature to pin 5 of control unit connector B.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 TS; production: da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01, da 02/01 a 02/02, da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

Engine temperature sender unit K36 receives a reference earth from pin 1 of connector B of control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the coolant temperature to pin 5 of control unit connector B. The same sender unit also supplies information to the temperature gauge and warning light ( E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() ).

Applies to: engines: 1.8 TS; all production periods; only with equipment: Mechanical gearbox

Functional description

Hot Lambda probe K40 provides control unit M10 with information on the correct composition of the air-fuel mixture. The signal is sent to pin 30 of connector B of M10 , while pin 21 provides a reference earth signal. Probe K40 is heated by a resistor in order to ensure it works efficiently even when cold. The resistor is supplied by relay J10 and receives an earth signal from pin 8 of connector B of control unit M10 .

Knock sensor K50 provides information on knock status via a frequency signal sent to pin 2 of connector B of control unit M10 . It receives a reference earth from pin 11 of connector B. The signals are very low intensity and are therefore shielded.

Air flow meter K41 (supplied from relay J10 ) receives a reference voltage from pin 1 of connector A and sends pin 20 of connector A a signal proportional to air flow. K41 also contains an air temperature sensor: the sensor reference earth is supplied by pin 29 of connector A of M10 , while an air temperature signal is received by pin 30 of connector A.

Control unit M10 controls the opening of individual injectors N70 ,via duty cycle signals produced by pins 6 (cyl. 1), 17 (cyl. 2), 16 (cyl. 3) and 7 (cyl. 4) of connector B of M10 .

Injectors N70 receive a power supply to enable opening from main relay J10 .

Control unit M10 also controls coils A30 via control signals (earth) for the coil primary windings, while the secondary winding sends a pulse to the spark plugs: from pins 38 and 10 of connector B for cylinder pair 1-4 and from pins 9 and 37 of connector B of M10 for cylinders 2-3.

The primary windings for coils A30 receive the supply for enabling the opening from the main relay J10 .

The idle actuator N74 regulates the idle air flow via the butterfly. It receives a supply and reference earth from pins 1 and 29 of connector B of control unit M10 : pins 4 and 13 of connector B are connected to the potentiometer which detects the position of the butterfly; pins 26 and 35 of connector B control the butterfly actuator.

Fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10 allows fuel vapours to pass through to the engine intake where they join the mixture entering the combustion chamber. The valve L10 , supplied by the main relay J10 , is opened by the control unit when the engine is under load via a duty-cycle signal from pin 34 of connector B of M10 .

Variable geometry solenoid L15 is controlled from pin 33 of connector B of control unit M10 .

The timing variator N76 mechanically controls the inlet timing advance: it is controlled by pin 24 of connector B of the control unit.

Control unit M10 is connected to the ALFA ROMEO CODE control unit via a line from pin 16 of connector A ( E7010 CODE ).

The control unit is equipped with a self-test system that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 : this receives signals from pin 11 of connector A of control unit M10 through the tester line. The self-test system also produces the signal for the "injection failure" warning light in the instrument E15 , which comes from pin 12 of connector A of the control unit M10 .

The module E15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 15 of connector A from the line protected by fuse B11 up to 2/99 or from fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99; when this earth signal arrives, the "fuel injection fault" warning light comes on.

The speedometer signal (vehicle speed) reaches pin 27 of connector A of control unit M10 ( E4021 SPEEDOMETER ).

The control unit M10 sends from pin 2 of connector A a signal proportional to the engine rpm ( E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT ).

Control unit M10 is connected to the air conditioning system via pins 19 and 31 of connector A. This allows engine idling speed to be adjusted to increased engine load whenever the compressor is activated, or to turn off the compressor in the case of high speeds or high engine loads ( E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT ).

The control unit also controls the engine cooling system: pins 4, 13, 34 and 35 of connector A control activation of the relevant fan ( E5020 ENGINE COOLING ).

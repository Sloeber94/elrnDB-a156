Functional description

The engine control unit M10 monitors and controls the entire electronic ignition and injection system.

The system is supplied directly by the battery via a line protected by the maxifuse EFI located on the battery, connector B99 C .

The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) arrives from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

The control unit M10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 15 of connector A, via a line protected by fuse F5 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

The supply controlled by the ignition reaches pin 47 of connector A, via a line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

Main injection relay J10 controls the entire system: this is excited by a control signal (earth) from pin 31 of connector A of control unit M10 and then sends a power supply to pins 16, 32 and 48 of connector A of the control unit via a line protected by fuse F3 of supplementary fuse box B98 A. to air flow meter K41, fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10, variable geometry contol solenoid L15, variable valve timing actuator N76 and lambda sensors K15, K16 and K17; (all these lines are protected by fuse F2 of B98 A ). to the injectors N70 and the coils A30 , via the line protected by fuse F3 , located in the unit B98 A .

the fuel pump relay J15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition via fuse F4 , of the additional fuse box B98 A .

It is energized by a control signal (earth) from pin 30 of connector A of control unit M10 and supplies power to fuel pump N40 , which is earthed through the inertia switch I50 , which cuts off the circuit in the case of a crash and so prevents the dangerous spilling of fuel.

On the control unit, the earth point C40 C is located, which connects to the engine earth C40 A .

The engine control unit M10 receives signals from the various sensors in order to monitor all engine service conditions.

Rpm sensor K46 provides information on engine speed via a frequency signal sent to pins 10 and 59 of connector B of control unit M10 . These two signals are very low intensity and are therefore shielded.

Timing sensor K47 is supplied from pin 51 of connector B of control unit M10 , receives a reference earth from pin 26 of connector B and sends a signal of frequency corresponding to the timing to pin 42 of control unit connector B.

Engine temperature sender unit K36 riceives a reference earth from pin 26 of connector B of control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the coolant temperature to pin 25 of control unit connector B. The same sender unit also supplies information to the temperature gauge and warning light E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

The three Lambda probes K15, K16 and K17 provide the control unit M10 information concerning the correct composition of the air-fuel mixture in three different positions of the exhaust: two are upstream of the precatalyzers, the third is upstream of the main catalyzer.

The probe K15 , on the right precatalyzer, sends a signal to pin 23 of connector B of M10 , while pin 55 supplies the reference earth. Probe K15 is heated by a resistor in order to ensure it works efficiently even when cold. The resistor is supplied by the main relay and receives an earth signal from pin 34 of connector B of control unit M10 .

The probe K16 , on the left precatalyzer, sends the signal to pin 39 of connector B of M10 , while pin 7 supplies the reference earth. The probe K16 is heated with a resistor, supplied by the main relay, and receives an earth signal from pin 2 of connector B of control unit M10 .

The probe K17 downstream of the main catalyzer sends the signal to pin 28 of connector B of M10 , while pin 60 supplies the reference earth. The probe K17 is heated with a resistor, supplied by the main relay, and receives an earth signal from pin 50 of connector B of control unit M10 .

Knock sensor K50 provides information on knock status via a frequency signal sent to pin 22 of connector B of control unit M10 . It receives a reference earth from pin 54 of connector B. The signals are very low intensity and are therefore shielded.

Air flow meter K41 (supplied by the relay J10 ) receives a reference voltage from pin 63 of connector A of the control unit, and sends to pin 57 of connector A a signal proportional to the air flow. K41 also contains an air temperature sensor: the sensor reference earth is supplied by pin 25 of connector A of M10 , while an air temperature signal is received by pin 54 of connector A.

Accelerator pedal K55 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers (a main one and a safety back-up). The first receives power and earth from pins 56 and 24 respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 8 of the same connector. The second receives power and earth from pins 7 and 25 respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 40 of connector A.

Pin 27 of connector A of M10 receives the 'pedal pressed' signal coming from the brake lights switch I30, with the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 or, depending on the version, from fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B.

Pin 59 of connector A of M10 receives the signal coming from the clutch switch I31.

The control unit M10 converses with the ABS control unit M50 via the CAN line - pins 12 and 29 of connector A: the latter, on versions with VDC, signals any irregularities on the ground, distinguished via the speed sensors installed on the individual wheels via steering sensor K58 to ABS control unit M50. In this way the engine management control unit can distinguish between variations in engine torque due to irregularities on the road surface and those due to misfire in the combustion chambers.

Via this CAN line, the control unit M10 also receives the speedometer signal (vehicle speed).

Control unit M10 controls the opening of individual injectors N70, via duty cycle signals produced by pins 19 (cyl. 1), 52 (cyl. 2), 35 (cyl. 3) and 3 (cyl. 4) of connector B of M10 .

The control unit M10 also controls the coils A30 by means of (earth) signals for the coil primary windings, whilst the secondary winding sends a pulse to the spark plugs: from pins 16, 31, 30 and 14 of connector B of M10 for cylinders 1,2, 3 and 4 respectively.

Throttle body actuator N75 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers connected in parallel: they control throttle opening via a stepper motor.

The motor receives information from pins 17 - 49 and 1 - 33 of connector B of M10 . Pin 58 of connector B sends a power supply to both potentiometers, pin 26 supplies an earth signal to the potentiometers, while pins 24 and 41 of the same connector receive signals from the throttle body actuator N75 .

Fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10 allows fuel vapours to pass through to the engine intake where they join the mixture entering the combustion chamber. Valve L10 is opened by the control unit when the engine is under load via a duty-cycle signal from pin 5 of connector B of M10 .

Variable geometry solenoid L15 is controlled from pin 4 of connector B of control unit M10 .

Variable valve timing system N76 controls intake advance mechanically; it is controlled by pin 36 of control unit connector B.

The control unit M10 is connected to the ALFA ROMEO CODE control unit via a line from pin 3 of connector A E7010 CODE .

The control unit is equipped with a self-diagnostic system that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 : this receives signals from pin 53 of connector A from control unit M10 through the tester line. The self-test system also produces the signal for the 'EOBD' warning light in the instrument

Control unit M10 sends out a signal proportional to engine rpm E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT from pin 35 of connector A.

The minimum engine temperature signal is sent from pin 34 of connector A to the auxiliary instrument pin 22 receives, from E20, a 'fuel consumption' signal See E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

A speedometer signal is sent to instrument E10 from pin 52 of connector A See E4021 SPEEDOMETER .

Control unit M10 is connected to the air conditioning system via pins 6 and 13 of connector A. This allows engine idling speed to be adjusted to increased engine load whenever the compressor is activated, or to turn off the compressor in the case of high speeds or high engine loads E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT .

The control unit also controls the engine cooling system: pins 9, 41, 50 and 62 of connector A control activation of the relevant fan E5020 ENGINE COOLING .

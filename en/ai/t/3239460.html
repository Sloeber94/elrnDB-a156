Functional description

The engine control unit M10 monitors and controls the entire electronic ignition and injection system.

The system is supplied directly by the battery via a line protected by the maxifuse EFI located on the battery, connector B99 C .

The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) arrives from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

The control unit M10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 70 of connector A, via a line protected by fuse F5 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

The supply controlled by the ignition reaches pin 67 of connector A, via a line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 A .

The main injection relay J10 controls the entire system: this is excited by a control signal (earth) received from pin 25 of connector A of control unit M10 and then sends power: to pins 3, 5 and 6 of control unit connector A; the air flow meter K41 , fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10 , variable geometry control solenoid L15 , variable valve timing actuator N76 and Lambda sensors K15 , K16K17 and K18 ; (all these lines are protected by fuse F2 of B98 A ). to the coils A30 , via the line protected by fuse F3 of B98 A ; to high pressure pump N77 , again via the line protected by fuse F3 of B98 A .

the fuel pump relay J15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition via fuse F4 , of the additional fuse box B98 A .

It is energized by a control signal (earth) from pin 28 of connector A of control unit M10 and supplies power to fuel pump N40 , which is earthed through the inertia switch I50 , which cuts off the circuit in the case of a crash and so prevents the dangerous spilling of fuel.

Pins 1, 2 and 4 of connector A of M10 are earthed.

The engine control unit M10 receives signals from the various sensors in order to monitor all engine service conditions.

Rpm sensor K46 supplies information on engine speed via a frequency signal sent to pins 40 and 55 of connector B of control unit M10 .

Timing sensor K47 is supplied from pin 26 of connector B of control unit M10 , receives a reference earth from pin 21 of connector B and sends a signal of frequency corresponding to the timing to pin 41 of control unit connector B.

Fuel pressure sensor K83 is supplied from pin 26 of connector B of control unit M10 , receives a reference earth from pin 21 of connector B and sends a signal of frequency corresponding to the timing to pin 24 of control unit connector B.

Engine temperature sender unit K36 riceives a reference earth from pin 57 of connector B of control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the coolant temperature to pin 9 of control unit connector B. The same sender unit also supplies information to the temperature gauge and warning light E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

The four Lambda probes K15, K16,K17 and K18 provide the control unit M10 information concerning the correct composition of the air-fuel mixture in four different positions of the exhaust: two (linear) are upstream of the precatalyzers, and two (planar) are downstream of the catalyzers.

Linear sensor K15 sends signals to pins 53, 52 and 74 of connector A of M10 , while pin 75 supplies the reference earth. Probe K15 is heated by a resistor in order to ensure it works efficiently even when cold. The coil is supplied by the main relay, and receives an earth signal from pin 73 of connector A of control unit M10 .

Linear sensor K16 sends signals to pins 79, 58 and 80 of connector A of M10 , while pin 57 supplies the reference earth. Sensor K16 is heated by a heating coil in order to ensure it works efficiently even when cold. The coil is supplied by the main relay, and receives an earth signal from pin 29 of connector A of control unit M10 .

Planar sensor K17 sends a signal to pin 77 of connector A of M10 , while pin 76 supplies the reference earth. The sensor K17 is heated by a heating coil supplied by the main relay and receives an earth signal from pin 49 of connector A of control unit M10 .

Planar sensor K18 sends a signal to pin 55 of connector A of M10 , while pin 56 supplies the reference earth. Sensor K18 is heated with a heating coil, supplied by the main relay, and receives an earth signal from pin 94 of connector A of control unit M10 .

The knock sensor K50 makes it possible, through a frequency signal sent to pin 44 of connector B of control unit M10 to have information concerning knock conditions: it receives a reference earth from pin 59 of connector B.

Air flow meter K41 (supplied by the relay J10 ) receives a reference voltage from pin 17 of connector A of the control unit, and sends to pin 10 of connector A a signal proportional to the air flow. K41 also contains an air temperature sensor: the sensor reference earth is supplied by pin 16 of connector A of M10 , while an air temperature signal is received by pin 10 of connector A.

The brake servo vacuum sensor K105 intervenes by reducing the load as appropriate so as to restore more efficient braking: the sensor is supplied from pin 26 of connector B of control unit M10 , it receives a reference earth from pin 21 of connector B and sends a signal of frequency corresponding to the vacuum to pin 42 of control unit connector B.

Accelerator pedal K55 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers (a main one and a safety back-up). The former receives power and earth from pins 83 and 59 respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 61 of the same connector. The second receives power and earth from pins 81 and 82 respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 60 of the same connector.

Pin 27 of connector A of M10 receives the 'pedal pressed' signal coming from the brake lights switch I30, with the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 or, depending on the version, from fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 B; it receives a pedal released signal from I30 at pin 20 of connector A.

Pin 23 of connector A of M10 receives the signal coming from the clutch switch I31 .

The control unit M10 dialogues with the ABS control unit M50 via the CAN line - pins 64 and 86 of connector A: this line, on versions with VDC, passes through steering sensor K58 to reach ABS control unit M50: the latter signals any irregularities on the ground, distinguished via the speed sensors installed on the individual wheels. In this way the engine management control unit can distinguish between variations in engine torque due to irregularities on the road surface and those due to misfire in the combustion chambers.

Control unit M10 controls the opening of individual injectors N70, via appropriate signals produced by pins 1 and 33 (cyl. 1), 16 and 46 (cyl. 2), 31 and 18 (cyl. 3) and 48 and 2 (cyl. 4) of connector B of M10 .

Fuel pressure regulator N77 is controlled by control unit M10 from pin 5 of connector B.

The control unit M10 also controls the coils A30 by means of (earth) signals for the coil primary windings, whilst the secondary winding sends a pulse to the spark plugs: from pins 54, 37, 39 and 38 of connector B of M10 for cylinders 1,2, 3 and 4 respectively.

Throttle body actuator N75 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers connected in parallel: they control throttle opening via a stepper motor.

The motor receives information from pins 15 and 30 of connector B of M10 . Pin 14 of connector B sends a power supply to both potentiometers, pin 29 supplies an earth signal to the potentiometers, while pins 13 and 28 of the same connector receive signals from the throttle body actuator N75 .

Fuel vapour recovery solenoid L10 allows fuel vapours to pass through to the engine intake where they join the mixture entering the combustion chamber. Valve L10 is opened by the control unit when the engine is under load via a duty-cycle signal from pin 60 of connector B of M10 .

Variable geometry solenoid L15 is controlled from pin 6 of connector B of control unit M10 .

Variable valve timing system N76 controls intake advance mechanically; it is controlled by pin 36 of control unit connector B.

The control unit M10 is connected to the ALFA ROMEO CODE control unit via a line from pin 72 of connector A E7010 CODE .

The control unit is equipped with a self-diagnostic system that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 : this receives signals from pin 87 of connector A from control unit M10 through the tester line. The self-test system also produces the signal for the 'EOBD' warning light in the instrument E10 , which comes from pin 89 of the control unit M10 .

Control unit M10 sends out a signal proportional to engine rpm E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT from pin 66 of connector A.

The minimum engine temperature signal is sent from pin 71 of connector A to the auxiliary instrument E20 ; pin 8 receives, from E20, a 'fuel consumption' signal E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

A speedometer signal is sent to instrument E10E4021 SPEEDOMETER from pin 68 of connector A.

Control unit M10 is connected to the air conditioning system via pins 7 and 40 of connector A. This allows engine idling speed to be adjusted to increased engine load whenever the compressor is activated, or to turn off the compressor in the case of high speeds or high engine loads E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT .

The control unit also controls the engine cooling system: pins 45, 24, 69 and 93 of connector A of M10 to control the activation of the relevant fan E5020 ENGINE COOLING .

Cruise control management signals leave from pins 41, 43, 44, 21 and 91 of connector A of M10.E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

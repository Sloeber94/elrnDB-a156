Functional description

The engine control unit M10 monitors and controls the entire electronic injection system.

The supply for the engine control unit M10 is controlled by the ignition (15/54) at pin 58 by the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A .

The relay J10 controls the system supply: it is supplied by the battery, from the line for maxifuse EFI of B99 ; pin 13 of the control unit M10 sends the enablement signal which excites relay J10 and sends power to the control unit, directly to pin 5 and, via the line of fuse F3 of the additional fuse box B98A, to pin 4.

The relay J15 is also supplied by the battery, from the line for maxifuse EFI of B99; pin 24 of control unit M10 sends the signals which energize the relay, which provides the supply to the fuel pump N40, which is connected to earth through the inertia switch I50. In case of impact, this cuts off the circuit to prevent the danger of fuel delivery. Pins 1, 2 and 3 of M10 are earthed.

The diesel preheating relay J17 is supplied by the battery via the line protected by the GLOW maxifuse of B99 or fuse B39, and controls the operation of the diesel heating resistor O20; a signal of the control unit M10 (from pin 111) deactivates the system once heating has taken place.

Control unit M15 , responsible for controlling preheating of heater plugs A40 , is supplied directly by the battery via a line protected by the GLOW maxifuse of B99 . It is supplied - connector C - by the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A, and exchanges the command and control signals with pins 22 and 62 of M10 which controls the timer. Connector B is connected to the heater plugs A40.

The engine control unit M10 receives signals from the various sensors in order to monitor all engine service conditions.

The rpm sensor K46 provides information concerning the engine speed through a frequency signal sent to pin 100 of the control unit M10 . It receives an earth signal from pin 99 of the control unit. Both signals are weak and are therefore suitably shielded by pin 101.

Timing sensor K47 is supplied from pin 102 of control unit M10 . It receives a reference earth from pin 104 of the control unit M10 and sends a frequency signal corresponding to the timing to pin 103.

The engine temperature sensor K36 , receives a reference earth from pin 85 of the control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the temperature of the engine coolant to pin 84.

The same sender unit also supplies information to the temperature gauge and warning light E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

The air flow meter K41 receives a general power supply from the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A ; a reference voltage is received from pin 88 of M10 . It is supplied by the control unit from pin 97 of M10 , and sends to pin 89 a signal proportional to the air flow. K41 also contains an air temperature sensor which sends an air temperature signal to pin 86 of M10 .

Accelerator pedal K55 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers (a main one and a safety back-up). The first receives power and earth from pins 78 and 76 respectively of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 77. The second receives power and earth from pins 81 and 79 respectively of M10 and sends a signal to pin 80.

Fuel temperature sensor K81 fitted on the return manifold measures the temperature of diesel leaving the injectors. It receives a reference earth from pin 83 of M10 and sends the fuel temperature signal to pin 82.

The supercharging sensor K82 measures the turbocharger pressure. It is supplied by pin 93 of M10 and receives a reference earth from pin 95. The signal is sent to in 94 of the control unit.

The fuel pressure sensor K83 is responsible for providing the injection control unit with a feedback signal for governing pressure and injection duration. It receives the power and reference earth from pins 90 and 92 of M10 ; it then sends the pressure signal to pin 91.

The fuel pressure regulator N77 has the task of keeping the value of the fuel pressure in the accumulator constant. The two control signals come from pins 108 and 109 of the control unit M10 .

The EGR solenoid L30 controls exhaust gas recirculation. It is supplied from the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A and is controlled by a signal of pin 16 of M10 .

The turbine variable geometry control solenoid L36 governs the operation of the turbine in accordance with engine load; it is supplied by the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A and is controlled by a signal from pin 17 of M10 .

The solenoid valve on the throttle casing L62 shutters and closes the throttle, for example when the engine is switched off; it is supplied by the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A and is controlled by a signal of pin 15 of M10 .

Pin 59 of M10 receives the signal coming from the brake lights switch I30, with the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from fuse F1 of the junction unit B1, or, depending on the version, from fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 B.

Pin 62 of M10 receives an earth signal coming from the clutch pedal switch l31 .

The injectors N70 receive a power supply to enable opening from pins 117 and 118 of M10 .

The engine management control unit sends commands to the injectors N70 from pins 119, 120, 121, 114 and 115 respectively for cylinders 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5.

Control unit M10 - pins 10 and 9 - communicates with ABS control unit M50 via the CAN line: this line, on versions with VDC, passes through steering sensor K58 and reaches ABS control unit M50.

The rev counter module E15 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B ; when the earth signal arrives from pin 21 of M10 , the 'injection failure' warning light comes on.

The rev counter module E15 also receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B; when the earth signal arrives from pin 14 of M10, the 'heater plugs' warning light comes on.

The coolant temperature signal is sent to the auxiliary instrument from pin 110 of M10E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

The control unit M10 is connected to the ALFA ROMEO CODE control unit M20 via a specific line from pin 11; in this way if the ALFA ROMEO CODE unit does not detect a correct code, it does not issue enablement and the engine is not started.

The control unit is equipped with a self-diagnostic unit that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 via pin 48.

The control unit M10 sends a signal from pin 47 proportional to the engine speed E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT .

The control unit M10 is connected to the air conditioning system through pins 18 and 60. This makes it possible to adjust the engine idle speed as the load increases each time the compressor is switched on or to switch off the compressor in the case of high engine speed or load conditions E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT .

The control unit also controls the engine cooling system: pins 19, 20, 51 and 52 control the engagement of the relevant fans E5020 ENGINE COOLING .

Cruise Control management signals leave pins 23, 53, 54, 55, 56 and 57 of M10 See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL .

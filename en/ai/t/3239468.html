Functional description

The engine control unit M10 monitors and controls the entire electronic injection system.

The supply for the engine control unit M10 is controlled by the ignition (15/54) at pin 28 by the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A.

The relay J10 controls the system supply: it is supplied by the battery, from the line for maxifuse EFI of B99 ; pin 72 of the control unit M10 sends the enablement signal which excites relay J10 and sends power to the control unit, directly to pin 5 and, via the line of fuse F3 of the additional fuse box B98A, to pin 1.

The relay J15 is also supplied by the battery, from the line for maxifuse EFI of B99; pin 91 of control unit M10 sends the signals which energize the relay, which provides the supply to the fuel pump N40, which is connected to earth through the inertia switch I50. In case of impact, this cuts off the circuit to prevent the danger of fuel delivery. Pins 2, 4 and 6 of connector A of M10 are earthed.

The diesel preheating relay J17 is supplied by the battery via the line protected by the GLOW maxifuse of B99 or fuse B39, and controls the operation of the diesel heating resistor O20; a signal from the control unit M10 (from pin 68) deactivates the system once heating has taken place.

Control unit M15 , responsible for controlling preheating of heater plugs A40 , is supplied directly by the battery via a line protected by the GLOW maxifuse of B99 . It is supplied - connector C - by the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A, and exchanges the command and control signals with pins 52 and 93 of M10 which controls the timer. Connector B is connected to the heater plugs A40.

The engine control unit M10 receives signals from the various sensors in order to monitor all engine service conditions.

Rpm sensor K46 supplies information on engine speed via a frequency signal sent to pins 12 and 27 of connector B of control unit M10.

Timing sensor K47 is supplied from pin 11 of connector B of control unit M10. It receives a reference earth from pin 20 of connector B of control unit M10 and sends it a signal of frequency corresponding to the timing of pin 50 of the same connector.

The engine temperature sensor K45, receives a reference earth from pin 41 of the control unit M10 and supplies a signal proportional to the temperature of the engine coolant to pin 58 of connector B.

The air flow meter K41 receives a general power supply from the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A ; a reference earth is received from pin 44 of connector B of M10. It is supplied by the control unit from pin 29 of connector B of M10, and sends pin 42 of connector B a signal proportional to the air flow. There is also an air temperature sensor inside K41 which sends an air temperature signal to pin 37 of connector B of M10.

Accelerator pedal K55 is fitted with two built-in potentiometers (a main one and a safety back-up). The former receives power and earth from pins 30 and 45, respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a corresponding signal to pin 9. The latter receives power and earth from pins 46 and 8, respectively of connector A of M10 and sends a signal to pin 31.

The fuel temperature sensor K81 measures the temperature of the diesel entering. It receives a reference earth from pin 10 of connector A of M10 and sends the fuel temperature signal to pin 11.

The supercharging sensor K82 measures the pressure and temperature in the intake chamber, downstream of the turbocharger. It is supplied from pin 13 of connector B of M10 and receives a reference earth from pin 23 of connector B. The signal corresponding to the pressure value is sent to pin 40 of connector B of the control unit. There is also an air temperature sensor inside K82 which sends a signal to pin 53 of connector B of M10.

The fuel pressure sensor K83 is responsible for providing the injection control unit with a feedback signal for governing pressure and injection duration. It receives power and a reference earth from pins 28 and 8 respectively of connector B of M10; it then sends the pressure signal to pin 43.

The fuel pressure regulator N77 has the task of keeping the value of the fuel pressure in the accumulator constant. The two control signals come from pins 4 and 34 of connector B of the control unit M10.

The EGR solenoid L30 controls exhaust gas recirculation. it receives a supply from the line for fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A; , and is controlled by a signal from pin 60 of connector B of M10.

The turbine variable geometry control solenoid L36 governs the operation of the turbine in accordance with engine load; it is supplied by the line of fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A and is controlled by a signal from pin 7 of connector A of M10.

The solenoid valve on the throttle casing L62 shutters and closes the throttle, for example when the engine is switched off; it receives a supply from the line for fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98A; and is controlled by a signal from pin 59 of connector B of M10.

The engine management control unit sends the command to the injectors N70 from pins 16-47, 2-31, 1-46, 17-33 and 18-48 of connector B of M10 for cylinders 1, 2, 3 , 4 and 5, respectively.

Pin 59 of M10 receives the signal coming from the brake lights switch I30, with the supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 or, depending on the version, from fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98 B.

Pin 79 of M10 receives an earth signal coming from the clutch pedal switch I31.

Control unit M10 - pins 61 and 62 - communicates with ABS control unit M50 via the CAN line: this line, on versions with VDC, passes through steering sensor K58 and reaches ABS control unit M50.

The speedometer module E10 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B; when the earth signal arrives from pin 71 of connector A of M10, the 'EOBD' warning light comes on.

The rev counter module E15 also receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B; when the earth signal arrives from pin 92 of connector A of M10, the 'heater plugs' warning light comes on.

The coolant temperature signal is sent to the auxiliary instrument from pin 94 of connector A of M10 See E4023 DASHBOARD CENTRE AUXILIARY EQUIPMENT() .

Control unit M10 is connected to the ALFA ROMEO CODE control unit M20 via a line from pin 47 of connector A; in this way if the ALFA ROMEO CODE unit does not detect a correct code, it does not issue enablement and the engine is not started.

The control unit is equipped with a self-diagnostic unit that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 via pin 25 of connector A.

Control unit M10 sends from pin 48 of connector A, a signal proportional to the engine rpm See E4020 REV COUNTER INSTRUMENT ; a consumption signal leaves pin 27 of connector A for the speedometer E4021 SPEEDOMETER ,

The control unit M10 is connected with the air conditioning system through pins 29 and 54. This makes it possible to adjust the engine idle speed as the load increases each time the compressor is switched on or to switch off the compressor in the case of high engine speed or load conditions E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT .

The control unit also controls the engine cooling system: pins 69, 42, 81 and 90 control the engagement of the relevant fans E5020 ENGINE COOLING .

Cruise control E5060 CRUISE CONTROL function management signals leave pins 80, 49, 77, 78, 56 and 38 of connector A of M10.

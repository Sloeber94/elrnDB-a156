Functional description

The relay J35 controls the heater fan circuit.

The supply at the relay contact comes from the battery through the line for the A/C maxifuse up to 2/99 or of B99 from 2/99, while the coil energizing is controlled by the ignition (INT/A) from the line protected by fuse B10 up to 2/99 or fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99.

The relay J35 supplies the control knob H81 : this controls the fan at the different speeds through the regulator O30 .

The voltage control system varies according to the position of the knob and passes or does not pass through the resistors of the regulator O30 determining the four different voltage signals for the fan N85 which sends outside or recirculation air to the duct/distributor unit at 4 different speeds.

The regulator O30 has a thermal contact which protects against dangerous overvoltages.

The intake of outside air is controlled by the motor N82 which operates the flap between the two extreme positions of "dynamic air" and "recirculation".

The activation of the motor is controlled by the button located in the controls H80 : this switch receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT/A) from the line protected by the fuse B10 up to 2/99 or fuse F6 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99.

and is connected to earth: when the supply and earth are reversed, the motor's direction of rotation is reversed. With the recirculation switched on, the LED in the button for H80 also comes on.

The controls unit H80 has ideograms which are lit up when the side lights are on by means of the line coming from the steering column switch unit H5 protected by fuse B11 up to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98B from 2/99.

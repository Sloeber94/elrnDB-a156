Functional description

The relay J20 controls the compressor engagment circuit.

The relay receives a supply from the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the relay R1 protected by fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The coil receives a supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) from the line of fuse B25 ; the relay is thus energized by an earth signal coming from the engine management control unit M10 : in this way the air conditioning compressor L20 engagement electromegnet is operated through a voltage of 12 V.

The climate control control unit M70 (pin 7 of connector A) sends the signal for enabling the engagement of the compressor to the 4 stage pressure switch K10; if the pressures in the circuit are correct, the signal leaves the pressure switch and reaches the engine control unit M10 (pin 40).

According to its internal control logic, the control unit M10 sends the effective command to engage the compressor (from pin 32) which energizes the relay J20 .

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods

Functional description

The relay J20 controls the compressor engagement circuit.

The relay receives a supply from the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the relay R1 protected by fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The coil is supplied by the line from fuse F2 of the additional fuse box B98 A , of the main injection relay J10 ; the relay is energized by an earth signal coming from the engine management control unit M10 : in this way the air conditioning compressor engagement electro-magnet L20 is operated through a voltage of 12 V.

Climate control unit M70 ( pin 7 of connector A) sends a compressor enablement signal to four-stage pressure switch K10 . If circuit pressures are correct, the signal leaves the pressure switch and reaches engine control unit M10 - pin 6 of connector A. According to its internal control logic, the control unit M10 sends - from pin 13 of connector A - the command to engage the compressor which energizes the relay J20 .

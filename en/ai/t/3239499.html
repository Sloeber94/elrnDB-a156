Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

Functional description

The relay J20 controls the compressor engagment circuit.

The relay receives a supply from the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the relay R1 protected by fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The coil is supplied by the line from fuse F2 for the additional fuse box B98 A for the main injection relay J10 therefore the relay is energized by an earth signal coming from the engine management control unit M10: in this way the air conditioning compressor L20 engagement electro-magnet is operated through a voltage of 12 V.

Climate control unit M70 ( pin 11 of connector A) sends a compressor enablement signal to four-stage pressure switch K10. If circuit pressures are correct, the signal leaves the pressure switch and reaches engine control unit M10 - pin 40 of connector A. According to its internal control logic, the control unit M10 sends - from pin 7 of connector A - the command to engage the compressor which energizes the relay J20.

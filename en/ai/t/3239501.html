Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 03/98, da 04/98 a 02/99, da 03/99 a 01/00, da 02/00 a 01/01

Functional description

The CODE control unit M20 is supplied at pin 3 by the battery from the line for the maxifuse EFI of B5 to 2/99 or B99 from 2/99, through the line protected by fuse B35 to 2/99 or by fuse F5 of supplementary fuse box B98A from 2/99.

The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) reaches pin 8 from the line protected by shunt fuse B25 to 2/99 or fuse F4 of the additional fuse box B98A from 2/99.

Pin 4 is earthed.

The line leading from pin 6 is a serial line leading to engine management unit M10 , which is responsible for identifying the key and exchanging codes.

The control unit is equipped with a self-diagnostic unit that may be used by connecting the unit to connector R10 : this receives signals from pin 5 of control unit M20 through the tester line.

The self-test system also produces the signal for the "CODE failure" warning light, located in the check instrument E30 , which comes out of pin 2 of the control unit M20 .

The check module E30 receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 1 from the line protected by fuse B11 to 2/99 or from fuse F4 of supplementary fuse box B98B from 2/99: when this earth signal arrives, the "CODE failure" warning light comes on.

A second control unit connector is connected via two leads to the aerial, fitted coaxially to the ignition key, in order to detect key insertion and rotation.

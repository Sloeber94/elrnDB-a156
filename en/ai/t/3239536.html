Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02

Functional description

The diagnostic socket R10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 16 by the line protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 .

Pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

The pins are connected to the ABS control unit M50 (pin 11) E7020 ABS .

Pin 3 is connected to airbag control unit M60 (pin 9) E7030 AIRBAG .

Pin 9 is connected to the climate control unit M70 (pin 8 of connector B) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING .

Pin 11 is connected to alarm control unit M30 (pin 6 of connector A) and remote control receiver M35 (pin 2) E7015 ALARM .

Pin 8 is connected to Code control unit M20 (pin 5) E7010 CODE .

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS, 2.5 V6; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Functional description

Pin 7 is connected to the engine management control unit M10 (pin 53 of connector A) E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Functional description

pin 7 is connected to the SELESPEED control unit M54 , which shares its test line with engine management unit M10E7050 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION - Functional description .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 02/01 a 02/02; only with equipment: Q-System

Functional description

pin 7 is connected to AISIN electronic automatic transmission management unit M55 , which shares its test line with engine management unit M10E7050 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION - Functional description .

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v , 2.4 JTD 10v; production: da 02/01 a 02/02

Functional description

Pin 7 is connected to engine management control unit M10 (pin 48 of connector A) E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT .

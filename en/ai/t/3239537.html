Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

The diagnostic socket R10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 16 by the line protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 .

Pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

The pins are connected to the ABS control unit M50 (pin 11) E7020 ABS .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

Pin 3 is connected to airbag control unit M60 (pin 9 connector A) See E7030 AIRBAG .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

Pin 3 is connected to Air Bag control unit M60 (pin 6 connector A) See E7030 AIRBAG .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03

Functional description

Pin 9 is connected to the climate control unit M70 (pin 13 of connector A) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING .

Pin 11 is connected to alarm control unit M30 (pin 6 of connector A) and remote control receiver M35 (pin 2) E7015 ALARM .

Pin 8 is connected to Code control unit M20 (pin 5) E7010 CODE .

Pin 7 is connected to engine management control unit M10 .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Functional description

Pin 12 is connected to the two electronic units inside the headlamps F10A (left) and F11A (right).

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Functional description

Pin 7 is also connected to the SELESPEED control unit M54 , which shares its test line with engine management unit M10E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX .

Applies to: all engines; production: da 03/02 a 09/03, da 10/03; only with equipment: Q-System

Functional description

Pin 7 is connected to AISIN electronic automatic transmission management unit M55 , which shares its test line with engine management unit M10E7050 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION - Functional description .

WARNINGS

All the operations should be carried out taking great care to avoid injury to persons.

The use of special Alfa Romeo tools is recommended for certain procedures. The use of these tools is vital for working safely.

The safety regulations in force for workshops should be observed. Where necessary, the Manual contains specific warnings to prevent dangerous situations from arising.

For the use of chemical products, stick to the warnings on the safety card which the supplier must give the consumer (in Italy in accordance with D.M. No. 47/1992).

WARNINGS

All the operations should be carried out taking great care to avoid damage to the vehicle.

Suitably protect those parts of the engine which, having remained uncovered after dismantling, allow the entry of dust or foreign bodies, using adhesive tape or clean rags.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 TS , 1.8 TS; all production periods

WARNINGS

The engine compartment contains numerous moving parts, components at high temperature and live electrical wires.

The following precautions should be scrupulously observed when carrying out operations in the engine compartment:

  • Switch off the engine and let it cool down.
  • No smoking.
  • No naked flames.
  • Ensure that there is a fire extinguisher nearby.

WARNINGS

During each refitting, replace the gaskets, oil seals, spring washers, safety plates, self-locking nuts and all components which have deteriorated.

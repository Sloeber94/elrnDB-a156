Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

All the operations should be carried out taking great care to avoid injury to persons.

The use of special Alfa Romeo tools is recommended for certain procedures. The use of these tools is vital for working safely.

Follow the safety instructions in force for workshops. Where necessary, the Manual contains specific warnings to prevent dangerous from arising.

For the use of chemical products, stick to the warnings on the safety card which the supplier must give the consumer (in Italy in accordance with D.M. No. 47/1992).

WARNINGS

All the operations should be carried out taking maximum care and paying attention to avoid damage to the vehicle.

Protect those parts of the engine which, having remained uncovered after dismantling, could allow dust or foreign bodies to enter, using adhesive tape or clean rags.

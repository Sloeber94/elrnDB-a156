Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v ; all production periods

WARNINGS

The engine compartment contains numerous moving parts, components at high temperature and live electrical cables.

The following precautions should be scrupulously followed when carrying out operations in the engine compartment:

  • Switch off the engine and wait for it to cool down.
  • No smoking.
  • No naked flames.
  • Make sure that there is a fire extinguisher nearby.

WARNINGS

During each refitting, replace the gaskets, oil seals, spring washers, safety plates, self-locking nuts and all components which have deteriorated.
